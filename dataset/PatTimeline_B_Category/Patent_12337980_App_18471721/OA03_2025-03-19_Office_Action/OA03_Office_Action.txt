The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The prior art of record fails to disclose or make obvious the combined limitations of applicants claimed invention. Specifically, Combes (FR 2 917 713 - A1) teaches a propulsion assembly for an aircraft, said propulsion assembly having a median vertical plane (Examiner notes propulsion assembly in Fig. 1 having median vertical plane Pm detailed in Fig. 2) and comprising:
a turbomachine (motor 6, e.g., turbo jet engine; Fig. 1),
a pylon (pylon, Fig. 1; see merged document, page 3, final paragraph: Figure 1 shows a general schematic side view of an engine assembly for an aircraft, comprising an attachment pylon)
at the front, a front engine attachment fastening the turbomachine to the pylon (engine attachment 10, Fig. 1),
at the rear, a rear engine attachment fastening the turbomachine to the pylon (engine attachment 12, Fig. 1), and
a device for reacting thrust forces (device 14, Fig. 1) comprising:
a main fitting fastened beneath the pylon (connecting fitting 24, Fig. 2),
a spreader (connecting fitting 40, Fig. 2) mounted so as to be able to pivot on the main fitting about a main axis (Examiner notes 40 is mounted by means of an axis 48 passing through a bore 49 and is configured to pivot about a main axis, i.e., axis 48) and having an opening (49, best seen in Fig. 4),
two rods for reacting the thrust forces of an engine that are disposed on either side of the median vertical plane (push rods 26, Fig. 2), each rod having a front end articulated to the turbomachine (front end 26a, as depicted in Fig. 1) and a rear end articulated to the spreader (rear end 26b, as depicted in Fig. 1),
wherein each rod has a longitudinal axis, wherein the two longitudinal axes define a mean plane for transmission of the thrust forces (merged document, page 4, 4th line form bottom: FIG. 2 shows the detail of the thrust load recovery device 14 according to the present invention, transmitting the thrust forces of the engine to the casing 8 via push rods 26),
wherein the main axis is orthogonal to the mean plane for transmission of the thrust forces and comprised in the median vertical plane (Fig. 2; Examiner notes the transmission of thrust forces along the rods 26 is orthogonal to the main axis 48 passing through bore 49 and bore 50),
wherein the rear end of each rod is articulated to the spreader about a front connection axis parallel to the main axis and wherein the two front connection axes are symmetrical with respect to the median vertical plane (Examiner notes the rear end of each rod is articulated to the spreader, via 28, about a front connection axis parallel to the main axis and wherein the two front connection axes are symmetrical with respect to the median vertical plane, see two instances of 30 in Figs. 2-3),
a secondary fitting fastened beneath the pylon in front of the main fitting and having a secondary female clevis constituted of two secondary arms (Examiner notes additional fitting 52, Fig. 4, having clevis, i.e., yoke 56 constituted of two secondary arms, i.e., branches 60) each one being passed through by a hole (see holes in piece 52 best depicted in Fig. 4), wherein the two holes are aligned along a secondary axis included in the mean plane for transmission of the thrust forces and perpendicular to the median vertical plane (Figs. 2, 4), and wherein a major axis of each hole is included in the mean plane for transmission of the thrust forces and perpendicular to the secondary axis (Figs. 2, 4), wherein the spreader is housed in the secondary female clevis of the secondary fitting (best depicted in Fig. 2), wherein the opening is coaxial with the secondary axis (Figs. 2, 4), and
a backup connection pin inserted with a tight fit into the opening and with clearance into the holes (connecting pin 39, Fig. 2).
However, the prior art does not teach the combined limitations of the claimed invention, specifically, wherein the secondary fitting fastened beneath the pylon in front of the main fitting and having the secondary female clevis constituted of two secondary arms each one being passed through by a hole specifically of oblong shape; and
wherein the main fitting comprises a central flange passed through by a through-bore coaxial with the main axis,
wherein the spreader has a female rear clevis into which the central flange is introduced,
wherein the female rear clevis comprises two rear arms wherein each one is passed through by a rear bore coaxial with the main axis,
wherein the device for reacting thrust forces has a rear connection pin fitted in the through-bore of the central flange and the rear bores of the female rear clevis,
wherein, for each rod, the spreader has a female front clevis into which the rear end of said rod, which is passed through by a through-bore coaxial with the corresponding front connection axis, is introduced,
wherein each female front clevis is constituted of two front arms and each one is passed through by a front bore coaxial with the corresponding front connection axis, and
wherein for each rod, the device for reacting thrust forces has a front connection pin fitted in the through-bore of said rod and in the front bores of the female front clevis.
