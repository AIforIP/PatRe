Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
6.	Claims 1-9, 12, 14, 16-19 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor (or for applications subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 112, the applicant), regards as the invention.
Claim 1 first recites the limitation “a fixture to a monument provided for installation in a passenger cabin” as part of the intended use for “a fastening system” in the preamble of claim 1. However, claim 1 goes on to recite various details directed to the monument, such as “a wall portion” and “at least two first fastening elements”, and goes on to positively recite the at least two first fastening elements in at least lines 15-16 of claim 1, stating that “the at least two first fastening elements are displaced from one another along the fastening direction”. Examiner further notes limitations directed to the at least two first fastening elements in at least claims 2-5, 12. Further, dependent claims 8-9 and 19 recite details directed to the wall portion, such as the wall portion having a second opening “which aligns with the first opening in the fastening state, and… is misaligned with the first opening in the pre-assembled state” and that the second opening receives the blocking element in claim 9, and wherein the wall portion has a fourth opening “which aligns with the third opening” of the frame and the fourth opening receives a second blocking element in claim 19. It is therefore unclear from the preamble of claim 1 if Applicant is intending to positively claim that the fastening system comprises the fixture and monument as part of the fastening system, or that the fastening system comprises the wall portion, or if they are all intended to be functional limitations of the fastening system. If the fixture, monument, and wall portion are directed as being only functional limitations of the fastening system, it is Examiner’s understanding that the only positively recited structure within the fastening system of claim 1 pertains to the frame having at least two second fastening elements. As such, for the purpose of this action, Examiner will interpret that the fixture and monument are intended to be positively recited within the claim.
Examiner recognizes within applicant’s response filed June 14th, 2024 that the monument and fixture within claim 1 are not to be positively recited, however, if the claimed fastening system only requires the positively recited structure of the frame having at least two second fastening elements, limitations directed to the monument and/or fixture (i.e. the wall portion, the at least two first fastening elements, the openings within the wall portion) cannot be given proper patentable weight. Examiner proposes amending claim 1 to recite that the fastening system positively comprises the wall portion, without reciting that it is a part of the monument and within a passenger cabin. Examiner proposes amending the beginning of claim 1 to recite:
“A fastening system configured to attach a fixture
a wall portion which has at least two first fastening elements;
a frame which has at least two second fastening elements…”

It would be obvious to one of ordinary skill in the art prior to the effective filing date of the claimed invention to modify Seeley with the teachings of Chavarria, to have the fastening system further comprise locking units, such that each locking unit comprises a first opening in the frame, a blocking element, and a second opening within the wall portion, wherein the blocking element extends through the first and second openings to produce a locked state and thereby secure the frame to the wall portion and mechanically prevent movement of the frame relative to the wall portion. In doing so, the locked state would thereby be capable of preventing a release of the frame from the wall portion when in the fastened state. Also, in a released (unlocked) state, the frame would thereby be capable of being released from the wall portion to all for the releasee of the fastened state.
