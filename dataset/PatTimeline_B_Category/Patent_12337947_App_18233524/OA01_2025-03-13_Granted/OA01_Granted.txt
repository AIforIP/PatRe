Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The prior art does not teach or render obvious an aircraft having a crash unit having a crash box disposed at the front end of the body frame, in combination with the other limitations of the claim(s).
Crash units/boxes disposed at the front of automobiles are generally known, as protection from forward collisions is an area of concern. Crash boxes are also known for use in aircraft. However, aircraft crash boxes of the prior art are generally provided on the underside of the aircraft—as the aircraft descends, the underside/belly would be the expected primary point of contact with the ground. There is no clear motivation for providing a crash box at the front of the aircraft when the main concern is generally understood to be the underside.
