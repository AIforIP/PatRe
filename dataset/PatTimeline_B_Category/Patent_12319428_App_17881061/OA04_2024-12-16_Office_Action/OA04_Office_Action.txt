Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s reasons for allowance:
Regarding Claim 1, there is no prior art nor reasons to modify any prior art to have the combustion chamber and the inter-turbine burner fluidly coupled to the fuel source; a generator operably coupled to a drive shaft of the gas turbine engine; a clutch between the generator and the drive shaft; and an electric propulsion unit including a second propulsor, a plurality of blades moveable between an unfolded position and a folded position, and during a second mode of operation, the gas turbine propulsion engine is activated to produce thrust, the generator is disconnected from the drive shaft via the clutch, the electric propulsion unit is deactivated, the plurality of blades are in the folded position, and the combustion chamber receives fuel from the fuel source, a fluid flow path between the inter-turbine burner and the fuel source closed during the second mode of operation. The limitations stated along with the other limitations of the independent claim give reason for allowance. Claims 3, 5, 9-10, 21 and 23-26 are allowable as being dependent on Claim 1.
Regarding Claim 11, there is no prior art nor reasons to modify any prior art to have the gas turbine propulsion engine including a combustion chamber and an inter-turbine burner, the combustion chamber and inter-turbine burner fluidly coupled to the fuel source; a generator to be driven by a drive shaft of the gas turbine propulsion engine; a clutch between the generator and the drive shaft and an electric propulsion unit coupled to the wing outboard of the gas turbine propulsion unit, the electric propulsion unit electrically coupled to the generator via a power line extending along a portion of the wing, and during a second mode of operation, the generator is disconnected from the drive shaft via the clutch, the electric propulsion unit is deactivated, the first fluid flow path is open, and the second fluid flow path is closed. The limitations stated along with the other limitations of the independent claim give reason for allowance. Claims 14, 16-17, 27, and 29-31 are allowable as being dependent on Claim 11.
Regarding Claim 18, there is no prior art nor reasons to modify any prior art to have a gas turbine propulsion engine having a combustion chamber and an inter-turbine burner fluidly coupled to the fuel source, a generator to be driven by the gas turbine propulsion engine, a clutch between the generator and a drive shaft of the gas turbine engine, and an electric propulsion unit, wherein during the second mode of operation, the gas turbine propulsion engine is activated and the electric propulsion unit is deactivated, the generator is disconnected from the drive shaft via the clutch, and a fluid flow path between the inter-turbine burner and the fuel source is closed when the hybrid-electric powertrain is operating in the second mode of operation. The limitations stated along with the other limitations of the independent claim give reason for allowance. Claims 19-20 and 32-33 are allowable as being dependent on Claim 18.
