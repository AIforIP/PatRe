Claim Objections
Claims 3-4, 6, 8 and 21-23 are objected to because of the following informalities:
Claim 3 line 8 reads “be at least”, --by the at least-- is suggested.
Claim 4 line 3 reads “element comprises”, --elements comprise-- is suggested.
Claim 6 line 3 reads “element comprises”, --elements comprise-- is suggested.
Claim 8 line 3 reads “element comprises”, --elements comprise-- is suggested.
Claim 8 line 2 reads “III”, --IV-- is suggested.
Claim 21 line 6 reads “fixing point”, --fixing terminal-- is suggested.
Claim 22 line 4 reads “Please add new claim 23 as follows:”, this line should be deleted.
Claim 23 line 3 reads “of one end”, --of the one end-- is suggested.
Appropriate correction is required.
Claim 6 is objected to under 37 CFR 1.75 as being a substantial duplicate of claim 4. When two claims in an application are duplicates or else are so close in content that they both cover the same thing, despite a slight difference in wording, it is proper after allowing one claim to object to the other as being a substantial duplicate of the allowed claim. See MPEP § 608.01(m). Note that while claim 4 reads “a type II tendon sheath constraint element, which comprises a second structure” and claim 6 reads “a type III tendon sheath constraint element, which comprises a third structure”, there are no limitations in the claims to differentiate a type II or type III structure, or a second and third structure.
Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
Claims 3-9 and 14-22 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor, or for pre-AIA  the applicant regards as the invention.
Claim 3 lines 3-4 recite the limitation “one or more first guide grooves or first guide holes”. It is unclear if this limitation is referring to the guide grooves and guide holes recited in claim 1, or if this limitation requires additional guide grooves and guide holes.
