Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-12, 14 and 16 filed 11/20/2025 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The prior art of record fails to disclose or make obvious the combined limitations of applicants claimed invention. Specifically, Carpenter (US 4,354,647) teaches a kite (Fig. 10) comprising:
a body (body section 24, Fig. 10), wherein the body comprises:
a wing (wing section 23, Fig. 10); and
a supporting member directly formed from the wing (rib 17, Fig. 10;  col. 3, ln. 36: Slit 16 defines a brace or rib 17 folded up as [best] shown in FIG. 8. This reinforcing rib has been found to be extremely helpful in preventing a partial collapse of wing section 23 under certain flying conditions; which happening would further adversely affect the flight of the kite, or the control thereof);
at least one tying place (thru hole 14c, Fig. 10); and
wherein the supporting member maintains a shape of the body (best depicted in Fig. 10).
However, the prior art does not teach the combined limitations of the claimed invention, specifically wherein the supporting member is integrally formed with the wing.
