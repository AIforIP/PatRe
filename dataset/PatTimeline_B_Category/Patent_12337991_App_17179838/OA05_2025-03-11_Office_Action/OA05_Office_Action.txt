REASONS FOR ALLOWANCE
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Regarding Claims 1-10, 14-16 and 18-24, there is no prior art alone or in combination that teaches a cargo handling system that included the combination of recited imitations in Claims 1, 8 and 15. The art alone or in combination did not teach wherein a cargo handling system comprised a cargo compartment, extending from four longitudinal ends; wherein a first tray extends longitudinally from a first to fourth longitudinal end, and a second tray extending longitudinally from a first to fourth longitudinal end, and wherein a top skin is disposed on and mated to the first and second top mating surface of the first and second tray (Claims 1 and 15), and wherein the trays are continuously covered from the first to the fourth longitudinal end, a top skin is disposed on and mated to a first and second top mating surfaces created from a base, sidewalls and flanges of each of the plurality of trays, wherein the flange includes a bottom surface mated to a top surface of the top skin and press fit into the top skin (Claim 8). The closest prior arts of record Huber (United States Patent US 7,556,462 B2) and Huber (United States Patent 6,125,984 A) teach a similar cargo handling system comprising a plurality of trays, a top skin disposed on the trays, and a roller assembly, but fails to teach the relationship between the structure of the cargo compartment and the first and second tray, the mating surfaces and top skin (Claims 1 and 15), and the relationship between the structure of the cargo compartment and the flanges, mating surfaces and top skin (Claim 8). Additionally, no other references, or reasonable combination thereof, could be found which disclose or suggest these features in combination with other limitations in the claims.
