5.	The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
the prior art of record does not teach a system controller configured to perform at least one prerequisite control, where in: (a) the prerequisite control is enabled before starting a vehicle speed control; (b) a storage device to store whether or not switching between enabling and disabling is permitted for at least one prerequisite control; (c) when the driver starts the vehicle speed control, the storage determines whether or not at least one prerequisite control that is currently disabled is permitted to be enabled. If the prerequisite control is permitted to be enabled, then, prerequisite control is enabled before the start of the vehicle control. The prior art of record also does not teach the disabling of the prerequisite control once the vehicle control has ended nor do they teach to keep the enabled prerequisite control when the vehicle speed control is ended and an operation condition is satisfied.
While there are few prior arts exist that mentions the priority of other control over a vehicle speed control and hierarchical system of vehicle controls, they do not mention the determining system of whether or not prerequisite control can be enabled or disabled, they do not mention disablement of prerequisite control with the ending of the vehicle speed control, and they do not mention the enablement of prerequisite control even after the end of the vehicle speed control under certain circumstances.
