Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-10 would be allowable if rewritten or amended to overcome the rejection(s) under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), 2nd paragraph, set forth in this Office action.
The following is a statement of reasons for the indication of allowable subject matter:  the prior art neither anticipates nor renders obvious snake arm robot comprising a series of parallel joints where the individual joints are driven by wires connected to parallel links between a base platform and a movable platform.  Typical parallel robots may have wires connected to and driving the movable platform and not connected to links between the base and movable platform.  Typical snake robots lack parallel robot assemblies that serve as individual links.
The closest, relevant prior art is to Ji et al (CN 111113390) which shows a snake robot having individual joints and wires that drive the individual joints are connected to a proximal end of the joints.  It would not have been obvious to arrange parallel robots as individual links in a snake robot nor drive the movable platform of each parallel link via wires connected to corresponding parallel linkages of each parallel link.
