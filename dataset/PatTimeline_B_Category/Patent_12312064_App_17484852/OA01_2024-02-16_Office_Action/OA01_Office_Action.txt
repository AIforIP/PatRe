DETAILED ACTION
Election/Restriction
Restriction to one of the following inventions is required under 35 U.S.C. 121:
I. Claims 1-7, drawn to a shaped aircraft fuel cell comprising a through-void or an edge cavity, classified in H01M8/002.
II. Claims 8-15, drawn to an aircraft comprising an aircraft structure that translates and a shaped fuel cell comprising a through-void and/or an edge cavity, classified in B64D37/32; 2041/005.
III. Claims 16-20, drawn to a method of preventing rupture of fuel cells in an aircraft during a drop impact of the aircraft, wherein a shaped fuel cell comprising one or more through-voids and/or one or more edge cavities is provided and installed in an aircraft, classified in G06F; B64C; B64D.
The inventions are independent or distinct, each from the other because:
Inventions I and II are related as combination and subcombination.  Inventions in this relationship are distinct if it can be shown that (1) the combination as claimed does not require the particulars of the subcombination as claimed for patentability, and (2) that the subcombination has utility by itself or in other combinations (MPEP § 806.05(c)).  In the instant case, the combination as claimed does not require the particulars of the subcombination as claimed because the shaped fuel cell of invention II can comprise both a through-void and an edge cavity whereas the shaped fuel cell of invention I can only comprise either a through-void or an edge cavity.  The subcombination of Invention I also has separate utility such as being employed on a vehicle or other fuel cell powered device other than an aircraft and/or on an aircraft without an area specifically “designated to house a fuel cell” as required by Invention II (e.g. the fuel cell of invention I could be attached in an exposed manner to the aircraft exterior).
The examiner has required restriction between combination and subcombination inventions. Where applicant elects a subcombination, and claims thereto are subsequently found allowable, any claim(s) depending from or otherwise requiring all the limitations of the allowable subcombination will be examined for patentability in accordance with 37 CFR 1.104.  See MPEP § 821.04(a).  Applicant is advised that if any claim presented in a divisional application is anticipated by, or includes all the limitations of, a claim that is allowable in the present application, such claim may be subject to provisional statutory and/or nonstatutory double patenting rejections over the claims of the instant application.
Inventions [I/II] and III are related as process and apparatus for its practice.  The inventions are distinct if it can be shown that either: (1) the process as claimed can be practiced by another and materially different apparatus or by hand, or (2) the apparatus as claimed can be used to practice another and materially different process.  (MPEP § 806.05(e)).  In this case Invention III can be practiced by another and materially different apparatus in that the shaped fuel cell provided can have more than one through-void and/or more than one edge cavity whereas invention I requires a fuel cell with only a single through-void or single edge cavity and invention II requires a fuel cell with only a single through-void and/or a single edge cavity. Invention III also requires reviewing the layout of an existing aircraft and providing a shaped fuel cell which corresponds to the review step prior to installation. Invention I can be used to practice another and materially different process wherein the fuel cell is employed on a vehicle or other fuel cell powered device other than an aircraft. Invention II can be used to practice another and materially different process wherein a shaped fuel cell having only a single through-void and/or a single edge cavity are provided therein/thereon.

The examiner has required restriction between product or apparatus claims and process claims. Where applicant elects claims directed to the product/apparatus, and all product/apparatus claims are subsequently found allowable, withdrawn process claims that include all the limitations of the allowable product/apparatus claims should be considered for rejoinder. All claims directed to a nonelected process invention must include all the limitations of an allowable product/apparatus claim for that process invention to be rejoined.
In the event of rejoinder, the requirement for restriction between the product/apparatus claims and the rejoined process claims will be withdrawn, and the rejoined process claims will be fully examined for patentability in accordance with 37 CFR 1.104. Thus, to be allowable, the rejoined claims must meet all criteria for patentability including the requirements of 35 U.S.C. 101, 102, 103 and 112. Until all claims to the elected product/apparatus are found allowable, an otherwise proper restriction requirement between product/apparatus claims and process claims may be maintained. Withdrawn process claims that are not commensurate in scope with an allowable product/apparatus claim will not be rejoined. See MPEP § 821.04. Additionally, in order for rejoinder to occur, applicant is advised that the process claims should be amended during prosecution to require the limitations of the product/apparatus claims. Failure to do so may result in no rejoinder. Further, note that the prohibition against double patenting rejections of 35 U.S.C. 121 does not apply where the restriction requirement is withdrawn by the examiner before the patent issues. See MPEP § 804.01.
