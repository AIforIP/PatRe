DETAILED ACTION
This is a non-final rejection in response to application filed 11/29/23. Claims 1-15 are currently pending.
Drawings
Figures 1-2 are objected under 35 CFR 1.83 because the numerals associated with the boxes (or circles) are not indicative as to what said symbol represents. Applicant is required to label in words the function of said rectangles, such that a reader would be appraised of their function without having to read the entire specification in order to figure it out. For example, if according to the specification, element 2 is a normal electric busbar network, it is suggested that applicant labels box 1 – - normal electric busbar network --.
Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph:
The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the applicant regards as his invention.
Claims 1-15 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor (or for applications subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 112, the applicant), regards as the invention.
Claim 1-3 are unclear for the limitation, “data received from the electrical power controller” and “data related to aircraft operational parameters” and “a manually activated signal”. It is unclear what is meant by the “data” and additionally it is unclear what the” manually activated signal” requires. All subsequent dependent claims are further rejected.
Claim 10 is unclear as to how the method is being implemented. It is unclear which components are operating, selecting, and activating as the claim has discussed. All subsequent dependent claims are further rejected.
Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-15 may be allowed upon correction of 112 issues.
Conclusion
The prior art made of record and not relied upon is considered pertinent to applicant's disclosure. Huang et al. (US 9963095) teaches a similar structure but does not teach the multiple controllers and controlling the system in the same manner.
