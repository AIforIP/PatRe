Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: the available prior art fails to teach or suggest using the claimed algorithms in combination to calibrate a 3D imaging system with a robotic manipulator.
Sivanath et al. (US Publication No. 2022/0402131) teaches calibrating a structured-light based 3D machine-vision system for use with a robotic manipulator using Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) at paragraphs [0039-0041], but fails to teach utilizing the Nelder-Mead algorithm to solve for an optimal depth scaling coefficient.
Kubota et al. (US Patent No. 5,280,436) teaches, at column 5, line 26 through column 6, line 46, depth scaling for such structured light systems, but fails to teach the specific algorithms and methods detailed in independent claim 1, such as using the Nelder-Mead algorithm.
