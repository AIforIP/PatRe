The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Regarding claim 1, the prior art of record fails to show or render obvious an automated controller configured to generate the control signal, selectively dependent on feedback from the field sensor, to synchronize a phase of the mechanical response of the motor with a phase of the relative rotation of the exterior surface in combination with the remaining limitations of claim 1.
Regarding claim 11, the prior art of record fails to show or render obvious driving a motor selectively responsive to an automated control signal, selectively dependent on feedback of the determined relative rotation from the field sensor, to synchronize a phase of a response of the motor with a phase of the relative rotation of the surface in combination with the remaining limitations of claim 11.
Regarding claim 18, the prior art of record fails to show or render obvious an automated controller configured to generate the motor control signal, to synchronize a phase of the motor about the axis with a phase of a rotation of the exterior surface about the radial axis of symmetry in combination with the remaining limitations of claim 18.
