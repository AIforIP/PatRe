SEATING APPARATUS COMPRISING A LEG REST TRACK AND FOLLOWER
Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1, 59-65, and 67-77 filed 1/22/2025 are allowed.
The following is an examiner's statement of reasons for allowance:
The prior art of record fails to disclose or make obvious the combined limitations of applicants claimed invention. Specifically, Dowty et al. (US 2010/0253129 A1), hereinafter Dowty.
Regarding claim 1, Dowty discloses an aircraft seat (passenger seat 100, Fig. 3) comprising:
- a seat chassis (rigid frame 110, Fig. 3);
- a seat assembly (Examiner notes the assembly in Fig. 3) comprising a seat pan (seat pan 20, Fig. 3), a seat back (seat back 40, Fig. 3), and a leg rest (leg rest 30, Fig. 3);
- the seat pan being mounted to the seat chassis by a first pivoting connection (seat pan link 26, Fig. 3) defining a seat pan pivot axis about which the seat pan pivots (Examiner notes a seat pan pivot axis defined at the bottom pivot pin point of 26, see Fig. 3; §[0022]), the location of the seat pan pivot axis being fixed relative to the seat chassis (Examiner notes the pivot point is defined by a link fixed with respect to the stationary frame 110, see §[0022]);
-the leg rest being mounted to said seat pan by a pivoting connection defining a leg rest pivot axis about which the leg rest pivots (Examiner notes connection at 38, Fig. 3 and associated pivot axis);
- at least one seat pan link (Examiner notes y-shaped leg rest pivot yoke 32 Fig. 3) for controlling pivoting of the seat pan about said seat pan pivot axis (§[0019]); and
-at least one leg rest link (leg support link 36, Fig. 3) connected to the leg rest and the at least one seat pan link (Fig. 3);
- wherein the at least one seat pan link is coupled to the seat pan in front of the seat pan pivot axis (Examiner notes the at least one seat pan link 32 is coupled to the seat pan 20 via leg support link 36 and leg rest 30, specifically a leg support link 36 is pivotally attached at its opposing ends to the distal end 34 of the yoke and the leg rest 30 respectively… [and] the leg rest 30 is pivotally attached at its upper end 38 to the forward end of the seat pan 20 per §[0019], a connection of which is in front of the seat pan pivot axis defined at 26, Fig. 3).
However, the prior art does not teach the combined limitations of the claimed invention, specifically at least one leg rest track; and at least one leg rest follower adapted to travel in said at least one leg rest track, the at least one leg rest follower being connected to said at least one leg rest link.
