Authorization for this examiner’s amendment was given in an interview with William L. Bartow (Reg. No. 54,981) on 01/07/2025.
The claims have been amended as follows:
Claim 1. (Currently Amended) An unmanned aircraft comprising a [[A]] payload attachment device
a receiver for receiving the payload handle;
a releasable latch in the receiver having an engaged position and disengaged position, the releasable latch biased to the engaged position restraining a payload handle;
a manual release connected to the releasable latch for overcoming the bias and moving the releasable latch to the disengaged position;
an automated release connected to the releasable latch for overcoming the bias and moving the releasable latch to the disengaged position, the automated release connected to a controller of the unmanned aircraft; and
a disconnectable electrical communication connection with a first end connecting at the payload attachment device and a second end configured for connecting to the payload handle when the payload handle is restrained.

Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: none of the prior arts of record individually or in combination discloses or render obvious an unmanned aircraft with the payload attachment device as amended in claim 1.

Stevens individually or in combination discloses or render obvious an unmanned aircraft with the payload attachment device with a disconnectable electrical communication connection with a first end connecting at the payload attachment device and a second end configured for connecting to the payload handle when the payload handle is restrained as currently amended in claim 1.
