REASONS FOR ALLOWANCE
Claims 12 and 25 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Brand et al. (US 2023/0278701) teaches a non-retractable wheel-type landing gear as explained in the non-final rejection mailed 23rd September 2024.
Sikorsky (US 2,843,345) teaches a main landing gear of a helicopter secured to the fuselage by linkages.
Nannoni et al. (US 2011/0147518) teaches landing gear having mounting means fitted to the fuselage.
However, it would not have been obvious to modify these references to arrive at the claimed invention without the use of impermissible hindsight. The attachment of a mounting arm of a landing gear to a first fitting mounted to a cabin floor and a lateral longeron, and arrangement of  link fixation at an angle in a range from 45 degrees to 120 degrees with respect to the mounting arm and its attachment to a second fitting is a novel concept.
Claims 13-24 are allowable due to dependency on claim 12.
Claims 26-31 are allowable due to dependency on claim 25.
