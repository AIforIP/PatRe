Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 102
The following is a quotation of the appropriate paragraphs of 35 U.S.C. 102 that form the basis for the rejections under this section made in this Office action:
A person shall be entitled to a patent unless –
(a)(1) the claimed invention was patented, described in a printed publication, or in public use, on sale, or otherwise available to the public before the effective filing date of the claimed invention.
Claim 16 is rejected under 35 U.S.C. 102(a)(1) as being anticipated by Beblo (US 11933587 B1). Beblo discloses:
16. An articulating motor mount assembly, comprising:
a first frame segment (130) attachable to an airframe (12) of an aircraft;
a second frame segment (240) rotatably coupled to the first frame segment in a plane oblique (at least when angled in Fig. 4) to a longitudinal axis of the first frame segment;
wherein the first and second frame segments define an interior region (volume occupied by 222); and
an actuator (“first and second primary electric motors operable for rotating in the body and head actuation modules”; Column 5 Line 22) positioned in the interior region and operative to rotate the second frame segment relative to the first frame segment.
Claims 16 and 17 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 102(a)(1) as being anticipated by Okamoto (US 6976401 B2). Okamoto discloses:
16. An articulating motor mount assembly, comprising:
a first frame segment (portion to very left in Fig. 1) attachable to an airframe (can be secured to any solid frame) of an aircraft;
a second frame segment (1+2) rotatably coupled to the first frame segment in a plane oblique (“B” to the left in Fig. 1) to a longitudinal axis of the first frame segment;
wherein the first and second frame segments define an interior region (volume occupied motors in Fig. 1); and
an actuator (12, not shown but the motor associated with the gear shown to the left in Fig. 1) positioned in the interior region and operative to rotate the second frame segment relative to the first frame segment.
17. The articulating motor mount assembly of claim 16, further comprising a third frame (3) segment rotatably coupled to the second frame segment in a plane oblique (“B” to the right in Fig. 1) to a longitudinal axis of the second frame segment and a second actuator (12 of segment 1) positioned to rotate (about “P”) the third frame segment relative to the second frame segment.
