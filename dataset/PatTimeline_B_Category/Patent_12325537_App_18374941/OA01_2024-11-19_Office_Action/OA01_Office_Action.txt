Drawings
The drawings are objected to because the drawings fail to meet the standard established in 37 CFR 1.84. Specifically, 37 CFR 1.84(a)(2) states “The color drawings must be of sufficient quality such that all details in the drawings a reproducible in black and white in the printed patent”. In the case of photographs CFR 1.84(b)(1) states “The photographs must be of sufficient quality so that all details in the photographs are reproducible in the printed patent.”.  “Further, 37 CFR 1.84(m) states “The use of shading in views is encouraged if it aids in understanding the invention and if it does not reduce legibility”, “Spaced lines for shading are preferred” and “Solid black shading areas are not permitted, except when used to represent bar graphs or color”. The drawings provided are not sufficient quality for reproduction in black and white and the shading does not follow the guidelines set forth in 37 CFR 1.84(m).

Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
Claims 7, 14, 17 and 20-21 rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor, regards as the invention.
Regarding Claim 7, the limitations “wherein the first end is an outlet having the cathode and the at least one, additional intermediate electrode is at least one ring electrode proximate thereto and the second end is an inlet having the anode proximate thereto” renders the claim indefinite.  It is unclear how the first end has the cathode and is the outlet when Claim 1 from which Claim 7 depends claims “a cathode at the second end of the channel”. Further it is unclear how the anode is at the second end when Claim 1 claims “an anode at the first end of the channel”. Even further it is unclear which end the intermediate electrode is proximate to since the other claimed structure is indefinite. For the purposes of this examination Claim 7 will be interpreted as “the at least one, additional intermediate electrode is at least one ring electrode”.
The terms “a few millimeters” in Line 2, “nearly 1 m” in Line 3, and “several meters” in Line 3 in claim 14 are relative terms which renders the claim indefinite. The terms “a few millimeters”, “nearly 1 m” and “several meters” are not defined by the claim, the specification does not provide a standard for ascertaining the requisite degree, and one of ordinary skill in the art would not be reasonably apprised of the scope of the invention. For the purposes of this examination the claim will be interpreted as “the ionization mean free path has a length”.
Regarding Claim 17, the limitations “wherein the first end is an outlet having the cathode and at least one ring electrode proximate thereto and the second end is an inlet having the anode proximate thereto” renders the claim indefinite.  It is unclear how the first end has the cathode and is the outlet when Claim 15 from which Claim 17 depends claims “a cathode proximate the second end”. Further it is unclear how the anode is at the second end when Claim 1 claims “an anode proximate the first end”. Even further it is unclear which end at least one ring electrode is proximate to since the other claimed structure is indefinite. For the purposes of this examination Claim 17 will be interpreted as being disclosed when the limitations of Claim 15 are disclosed.
The term “about 250 km” in claim 20 is a relative term which renders the claim indefinite. The term “about 250 km” is not defined by the claim, the specification does not provide a standard for ascertaining the requisite degree, and one of ordinary skill in the art would not be reasonably apprised of the scope of the invention. For the purposes of this examination the limitation will be interpreted as “250 km”.
The term “about 70 and 250 km” in claim 21 is a relative term which renders the claim indefinite. The term “about 70 and 250 km” is not defined by the claim, the specification does not provide a standard for ascertaining the requisite degree, and one of ordinary skill in the art would not be reasonably apprised of the scope of the invention. For the purposes of this examination the limitation will be interpreted as “70 and 250 km”.

Allowable Subject Matter
Claim 22 is allowed.
Claims 10-11 and 18 are objected to as being dependent upon a rejected base claim, but would be allowable if rewritten in independent form including all of the limitations of the base claim and any intervening claims.
The following is a statement of reasons for the indication of allowable subject matter:
The limitations “at least one of the plurality of electrodes is an electron-emitting electrode” along with the remaining limitations of Claim 10 are not taught or fairly suggested in the prior art of record.
The plurality of electrodes of Allen are cathodes which as described by Allen in paragraph 0032 that the cathodes are used to collect the electrons and thus would not emit the electrons.  Further it would not be obvious to change the electrodes to be electron emitting since they serve the specific purpose of collecting the electrons.
Perricone is silent regarding the plurality of electrodes including an electron emitting electrode.  Further it is noted that Column 12, Lines 6-42 describe the plurality of electrodes being used to attract electrons and not emit them.
Claim 11 depends from Claim 10 and therefore includes the indicated allowable subject matter indicated above.
The limitations “the at least one exit ring electrode is biased positive” along with the remaining limitations of Claim 18 are not taught or fairly suggested in the prior art of record for substantially the same reasons discussed above.
The limitations “ionizing the volume of air using electron beams generated by cathodes and confined by segmented electrodes and one or more magnetic field cusps, forming ionized air” along with the remaining limitations of Claim 22 are not taught or fairly suggested in the prior art of record.
Allen does not show confining the ionized volume of air with one or more magnetic field cusps and further the cathodes do not generate electron beams since they are used to collect the electron beams.
Hey (U.S. Patent No. 10,184,460) shows a plasma thruster with a thruster wall, 206, an anode, 204, and cathode, 202, with magnetic cusps but does not show a plurality of ring electrodes. Further Hey uses Xenon as the working gas instead of air.
