Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph:
The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the applicant regards as his invention.
Claims 1-10 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor, or for pre-AIA  the applicant regards as the invention.
Claim 1 is rejected because the claim does not have a transitional phrase therefore it is unclear where the preamble ends and the body of the claim begins. See MPEP 2111.03.
It is unclear as to whether the phrase, “having a hollow structure … disposed in the interior of the bracket” is included as a part of the preamble or is to be considered limiting structure. For purposes of applying the prior art elsewhere below, the Examiner will presume the latter.
Claims 1-5 includes a “tool attachment surface”.  It is unclear of the tool attachment surface is that noted as item 2a in the specification or item 4a.  Please clarify.
Claims 6-10 are rejected for their dependency to claim 1.
Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-10 would be allowable if rewritten or amended to overcome the rejection(s) under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), 2nd paragraph, set forth in this Office action.
