Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 102
2.	The following is a quotation of the appropriate paragraphs of 35 U.S.C. 102 that form the basis for the rejections under this section made in this Office action:
A person shall be entitled to a patent unless –
(a)(2) the claimed invention was described in a patent issued under section 151, or in an application for patent published or deemed published under section 122(b), in which the patent or application, as the case may be, names another inventor and was effectively filed before the effective filing date of the claimed invention.
Claims 1-9 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 102(a)(2) as being anticipated by Ganguli (US 11,541,910).
Regarding claim 1, Ganguli discloses a control device that performs travel control of a vehicle (autonomous vehicle travel control and associated device; Ganguli at title, abstract, Fig. 1), the control device comprising:
A processor (processor 106) configured to acquire output information of a sensor configured to detect an object in an around of the vehicle based on a reflected wave from the object (processor configured to interpret radar data from more than one radar sensor to determine adjacent objects in the environment of the ego vehicle; Ganguli at col 1 lines 51-67, col 2 lines 1-15, col 3 lines 18-30), wherein the processor is configured to:
Acquire detection point data of the object in a peripheral region of the vehicle based on the output information of the sensor (range to object acquired relative to ego vehicle; Ganguli at col 3 lines 31-49).
Recognize that a peripheral environment of the vehicle is a travel-restricted section based on a distribution of detection point data a first group including a plurality pieces of the detection point data on one side in a left-right direction of the vehicle in the peripheral region (recognizing from individual sensor return data that the recognized objects’ angle and distance relative to the ego vehicle are on the shoulder of the road and/or occupies an adjacent travel lane; Ganguli at col 3 lines 31-67, col 4 lines 13-34, col 6 lines 7-45).
Regarding claims 2 and 3, Ganguli discloses wherein the processor is configured to: classify the first group (total sensed surrounding data; Ganguli at col lines 31-49) into a plurality of first subgroups; and recognize that the peripheral environment is the travel-restricted section based on a variation amount of positions of the plurality of first subgroups in the left right direction (plurality of sensors obtain first group of data in the surrounding environment of the vehicle, plurality of sensors configured to detect objects longitudinally and laterally with respect to ego vehicle and their mounted positions; Ganguli at col 3 lines 31-49).
Regarding claim 4, Ganguli discloses wherein the processor is further configured to recognize that the peripheral environment is the travel-restricted section based on a distribution of detection point data in a second group including a plurality pieces of the detection point data on another side in the left-right direction  in the peripheral region (plurality of sensors determine surrounding environment of ego vehicle, data from individual sensors are examined and objects lateral to ego vehicle in adjacent lanes are identified; Ganguli at col 1 lines 51-67, col 2 lines 16-62).
Regarding claim 5, Ganguli discloses wherein the processor is configured to:
classify the second group into a plurality of second subgroups; and recognize that the peripheral environment is the travel-restricted section based on a variation amount of positions of the plurality of second subgroups in the left-right direction (objects in adjacent lanes and their relative location and speed to ego vehicle are tracked; Ganguli at col 4 lines 51-59).
Regarding claim 6, Ganguli discloses wherein the plurality of second subgroups are different in position in the front-rear direction of the vehicle (sensors are configured to detect objects in the longitudinal and lateral directions from the ego vehicle, i.e. surrounding; Ganguli at col 3 lines 31-49).
Regarding claim 7, Ganguli discloses wherein the processor is configured to acquire, as the detection point data, detection point data based on the past output information and detection point data based on the latest output information (determining speed of detected object via radar; Ganguli at col 4 lines 51-59).
Regarding claim 8, Ganguli discloses wherein the sensor is a radar device (one or more radars; Ganguli at col 3 lines 31-49).
Regarding claim 9, Ganguli discloses wherein the processor is configured to perform lane change control of the vehicle; and restrict the lane change control when it is determined that the peripheral environment is the travel-restricted section (lane change allowed when adjacent lane is not restricted, prohibited when restricted due to occupancy; Ganguli at col 2 lines 1-62).
