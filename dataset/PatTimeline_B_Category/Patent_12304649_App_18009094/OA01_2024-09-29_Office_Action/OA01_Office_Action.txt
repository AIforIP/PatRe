Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 7-9 is/are objected to as being dependent upon a rejected base claim, but would be allowable if rewritten in independent form including all of the limitations of the base claim and any intervening claims.
The following is a statement of reasons for the indication of allowable subject matter: The reasons for indicating allowable subject matter over the prior art of record is Bosson (US20190196927A1).
Bosson discloses the computer has a control module implementing respective critical functions i.e. avionics functions, and delivering an output data item associated with the critical functions. The control module receives the result of implementing the corresponding critical function from a monitoring module, and inhibits the delivery of the associated output data item in an event of detection of an inconsistency between results of implementation of the respective critical function.
