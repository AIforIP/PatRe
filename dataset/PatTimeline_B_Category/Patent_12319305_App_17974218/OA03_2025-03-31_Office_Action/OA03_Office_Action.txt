Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-9 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
As per claims 1 and 8, US20180345790A1 Mimura teaches the display control device (Mimura abstract), obtaining switching information (Mimura [0078-0079]), and a first display with first information and a second display with second information (Mimura FIG. 31, [0050]).
Mimura does not disclose the third information being on the first display which notifies the user that the second display has the second information. Despite US20070061068A1 Okamoto teaching a warning device to warn the driver when the indication of control by the second indication control device is executed (Okamoto [0076]), the warning is not provided on the first display, as claimed. The prior art is silent on this feature.
For the aforementioned reason(s) claims 1 and 8 are found allowable over the prior art. Claims 2-7 and 9 are also allowable based on their dependency on claims 1 and 8.
