The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the applicant regards as his invention.

It is unclear for instance in claim 8 if applicant intends to introduce a new term which is different than the one already claimed in claim 1 (and/or claim 3) since the phrase “a term” is used again which implies that a new term different from the one in claim 1 (and/or claim 3) is being introduced to the claim, hence this limitation renders the claim to be indefinite.
The claims 5-7 and 9-13 are dependent upon claims 4 and 8 are also rejected under 112 second paragraph by the fact that they are dependent upon the rejected claims 4 and 8.

The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Claim 5 would be allowable for disclosing: the term, added to the expression for the ego vehicle acceleration, includes a product of a gain multiplied by a difference, and
in response to the time, when the preceding vehicle speed will equal the desired speed, being during:
the deceleration phase:
the gain is a first gain, and
the difference is the desired maximum gap subtracted from a current gap between the ego vehicle and the preceding vehicle, or
the acceleration phase:
the gain is a second gain, and
the difference is the desired minimum gap subtracted from the current gap.
The combination of Stern in view of Avedisov discloses adding a term to the expression for the ego vehicle acceleration but fails to teach the term, added to the expression for the ego vehicle acceleration, includes a product of a gain multiplied by a difference, and
in response to the time, when the preceding vehicle speed will equal the desired speed, being during:
the deceleration phase:
the gain is a first gain, and
the difference is the desired maximum gap subtracted from a current gap between the ego vehicle and the preceding vehicle, or
the acceleration phase:
the gain is a second gain, and
the difference is the desired minimum gap subtracted from the current gap.
Based on the above, the combination of feature of claim 5 is considered to be allowable.
