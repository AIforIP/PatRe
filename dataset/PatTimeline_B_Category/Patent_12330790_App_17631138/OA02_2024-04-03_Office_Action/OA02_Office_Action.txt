Response to Arguments
Applicant’s arguments, see pages 7-8 of applicant’s reply, filed 03/20/2024, with respect to the rejections under 35 USC 112(b) to claims 6 (for antecedent basis), 7, 17, and 18 have been fully considered and are persuasive.  These rejections have been withdrawn.
Applicant's arguments filed 03/20/2024 with respect to drawing objections and the rejection of claim 6 under 35 USC 112(b) have been fully considered but they are not persuasive.
Applicant attempts to clarify that the terms first and second position refer to forward and rearward positioning of the seat rather than upright and lie-flat positioning of the seat. While this does clarify the intended operation of the seat, the language of claim 6 rather requires that the seat be effectively inaccessible from the aisle when in the first position (through the limitation “the wall thereby blocking access to the seat from an aisle”), however the seat would still be accessible from the aisle in both positions. Hence it is unclear how the seat can be blocked in one position but not the other. Thus the drawing objection and rejection to claim 6 under 35 USC 112(b) are being maintained.
Applicant's arguments filed 03/20/2024 have been fully considered but they are not persuasive. Applicant argues Nicholas fails to meet new claim limitations requiring that the seat be moveable such that seat pan and foot rest be capable of forming a contiguous surface together.
In response to applicant's argument that the references fail to show certain features of the invention, it is noted that the features upon which applicant relies (i.e., moving seats which move to from a contiguous surface between a seat pan and footrest) are not recited in the rejected claim(s). Although the claims are interpreted in light of the specification, limitations from the specification are not read into the claims. See In re Van Geuns, 988 F.2d 1181, 26 USPQ2d 1057 (Fed. Cir. 1993). The broader language used in the claim still appears to read on Nicholas (see the rejection of claim 1 above. Nevertheless, an alternative rejection has also been put forth in view of Wallace above.
