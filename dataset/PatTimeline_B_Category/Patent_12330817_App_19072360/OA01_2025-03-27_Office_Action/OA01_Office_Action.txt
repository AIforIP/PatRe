This is a decision on the petition under 37 CFR 1.102(c)(1), filed March 6, 2025, to make the above-identified application special based on applicant’s age as set forth in MPEP § 708.02, Section II.
The petition is GRANTED.
A grantable petition to make an application special under 37 CFR 1.102(c)(1) and MPEP
§ 708.02, Section II: Applicant’s Age, must be accompanied by evidence showing that the applicant is 65 years of age, or more, such as applicant’s statement or a statement from a registered practitioner that he or she has evidence that the applicant is 65 years of age or older.  No fee is required.
The instant petition includes a statement from inventor Arthur M. Dula attesting that they are 65 years of age or older. Accordingly, the above-identified application has been accorded “special” status.
