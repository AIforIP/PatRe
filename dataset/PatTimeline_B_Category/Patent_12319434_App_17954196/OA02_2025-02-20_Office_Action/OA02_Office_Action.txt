DETAILED ACTION
Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-2 and 5-16 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Regarding claims 1 and 15-16, the prior art of record does not teach, or fairly suggest, a method of determining the position of the components of a landing gear assembly by scanning the landing gear assembly with a lidar system to generate a set of data points, partitioning the data into clusters based only on the difference in the horizontal coordinates of each data point, and identifying the position of each component based on the position of the data points in the cluster representing that component.
