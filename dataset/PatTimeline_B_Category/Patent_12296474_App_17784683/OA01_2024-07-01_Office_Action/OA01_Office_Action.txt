Claim Objections
Claim 7 objected to because of the following informalities: The claim recites ” causing the arm to perform an operation of indicating a type of control that is currently being performed among multiple types of control”. This claim limitation is being interpreted to include the robot having a user interface through which indications to a user are made in line with the presentation device disclosed in par. 0042 of the specification as filed. However the claim language implies that the robot performs some kind of motion that indicates the type of control. Examiner recommends to amend the claim to clarify that the indications are made through a user interface like the presentation device disclosed. Appropriate correction is required.
