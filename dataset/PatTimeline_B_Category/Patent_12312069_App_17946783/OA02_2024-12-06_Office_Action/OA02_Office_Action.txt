Drawing Objections
The drawings are objected to as they use a scheme where numbers are inserted into the drawings to represent items instead of descriptive labels. This makes the drawings useless without a key, a legend, or a specification. The drawings submitted with a patent application are supposed to help describe and set the metes and bounds of the claimed invention and when the meaning of the drawings cannot be easily or clearly derived without a specification, key, or legend, the value of the drawings and use is diminished. Proper action is requested.

Claims 2-3 and 6-7 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 103 as being unpatentable over Swanson in view of Lauwerys (US 20050240326 A1).
Regarding claim 2, Swanson does not teach the passenger discomfort-aware control unit generates the passenger discomfort-aware actuator control signals based on a linear feedback function that minimizes a transfer from the sensor data to control signals that increase a difference of a passenger discomfort index from a reference value, wherein the reference value is indicative of an absence of passenger discomfort.
Lauwerys teaches the passenger discomfort-aware control unit generates the passenger discomfort-aware actuator control signals based on a linear feedback function that minimizes a transfer from the sensor data to control signals that increase a difference of a passenger discomfort index from a reference value, wherein the reference value is indicative of an absence of passenger discomfort (Paragraph 0051 “The linear controller in one embodiment consists of several feedback and feedforward modules, each tackling a specific comfort or handling issue. The output of all modules are summed to a desired modal virtual damper force. The goal is to suppress the modal motions of the car to increase the passengers' comfort. The feedback linearization controller and modal decoupling transformations allow to directly specify desired modal forces, to be delivered by the shock-absorbers, from measured modal motions. Based on the skyhook principle the diagonal modal controllers consist of three first order low pass filters of which the bandwidth f.sub.b and gain can be tuned online to meet an optimal trade-off between desired comfort specifications and input saturation”).
It would have been obvious to one of ordinary skill in the art before the time of filing to modify the invention of Swanson with a linear feedback function that minimizes passenger discomfort of Lauwerys with a reasonable expectation of success. One of ordinary skill in the art would understand that shock absorbers on a car and vibration cancelling actuators on an aircraft may both perform active vibration cancelling. Active shock absorbers may require more complex control, however they add benefits of increased damping performance. The control may involve feedback linearization to simplify control design and tuning. One would have been motivated to combine Swanson with Lauwerys as this achieves simplifying control of vibration dampeners. As stated in Lauwerys, “One goal of feedback linearization is to transform the original control inputs of the system (the currents to the semi-active shock absorber CVSA valves) into virtual control inputs, in order to linearize the dynamic relation between these new control inputs and the outputs of the system to be controlled (the measured body accelerations). If the relation between the system inputs and outputs is linear (or sufficiently linear), control design and tuning is simplified since well known and Computer Aided Control System Design (CACSD) supported linear control design techniques can be applied successfully” (Paragraph 0028).
