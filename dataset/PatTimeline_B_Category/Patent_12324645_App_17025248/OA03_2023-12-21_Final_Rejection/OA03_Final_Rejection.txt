DETAILED ACTION
This is a non-final rejection in response to amendments filed 10/23/2023. Claims 1, 5-6, 9-19, 21-29, and 31-37 are pending.
Notice of Pre-AIA  or AIA  Status
The present application, filed on or after March 16, 2013, is being examined under the first inventor to file provisions of the AIA .
Response to Arguments
Applicant’s arguments with respect to the prior art of record not teaching the amended features of claims 1, 19, 25, and 29 not teaching have been considered but are not persuasive. As discussed in the prior art rejection below, Johnson teaches detecting that a collision has occurred and providing a view of the collision to the surgeon within the virtual environment. As this view would require an updated view to provide the view of the collision to the surgeon it teaches amended claim limitations of 1, 19, and 29. Roh teaches updating the patient model based on contact with the anatomy of the patient where tissues such as nerves can be marked as areas that the end effector should not be in contact with. Therefore, this teaches the amended claim limitations of claim 25.

A patent for a claimed invention may not be obtained, notwithstanding that the claimed invention is not identically disclosed as set forth in section 102, if the differences between the claimed invention and the prior art are such that the claimed invention as a whole would have been obvious before the effective filing date of the claimed invention to a person having ordinary skill in the art to which the claimed invention pertains.
