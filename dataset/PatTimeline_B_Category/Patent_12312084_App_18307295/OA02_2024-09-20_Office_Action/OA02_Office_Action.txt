Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
Claims 14-15 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
Claim 14  recites the limitation “A trailing edge high lift assembly for the wing according to claim 1”. The claim is indefinite as it is unclear how many trailing edge high lift assemblies are being claimed. For example, claim 1 contains a trailing edge high lift assembly, so is claim 14 claiming an additional assembly or attempting to claim just a the assembly for a wing, that is any wing. Likewise, claim 15 introduces yet another wing. The claim is indefinite as it is unclear what and how many wings/devices are being claimed. Correction/clarification is required.
