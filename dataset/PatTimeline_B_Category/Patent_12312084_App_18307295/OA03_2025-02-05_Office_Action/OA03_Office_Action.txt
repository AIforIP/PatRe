WING FOR AN AIRCRAFT HAVING A FLAP WITH A SPEED BRAKE PANEL MOUNTED IN A ROTATABLE MANNER
Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-3, 5-6, 8, 11-13 and 15 are allowable.
The following is an examiner's statement of reasons for allowance:
The prior art of record fails to disclose or make obvious the combined limitations of applicants claimed invention. Specifically, Johnson (US 3,874,617 A) teaches a wing for an aircraft (Figs. 5, 6 and 7 are sectional views of a wing; col. 2, ln. 56) comprising:
a main wing (10, best depicted in Fig. 8); and
a trailing edge high lift assembly movably arranged at a trailing edge of the main wing, the trailing edge high lift assembly (Examiner notes the assembly movably arranged at the trailing edge, i.e., opposite of leading edge 36, best depicted in Fig. 8) comprising:
a flap (fore flap FF and aft flap AF, Fig. 8); and
a connection assembly movably mounting the flap to the main wing, such that the flap is movable between a retracted position and at least one extended position (fore link BDF and drive crank 24, Fig. 8; Examiner notes take-off position Fig. 6 and landing position Fig. 7),
wherein the connection assembly comprises a first actuator unit for moving the flap between the retracted position and the at least one extended position (hydraulic actuator HA, Fig. 8),
wherein the flap comprises a leading edge part (fore flap FF, Fig. 8) and a trailing edge part (aft flap AF, Fig. 8) mounted to the leading edge part in a manner pivotable about a pivot axis (Examiner notes aft flap is mounted via the swinging link FG which extends from its pivotal connection F at the rear end of fore link BDF to pivot point G on the aft link, col. 3, ln. 33),
wherein the flap comprises a second actuator unit for moving the trailing edge part relative to the leading edge part within an operating range between an upper operating position and a lower operating position (actuator FHA, Fig. 8; upper operating position, i.e., cruise, Fig. 5 and lower operating position, i.e., landing, Fig. 7),
wherein the flap is configured to be brought into a speed brake setting where the trailing edge part is moved relative to the leading edge part beyond the upper operating position or beyond the lower operating position to a speed brake position (see the following excerpts for further detail, noting that extending link CDE would inherently extend the aft flap AF, col. 3, ln. 52; col. 4, ln. 21; Examiner specifically notes the following indicates the trailing edge part is configured to be brought into a speed brake setting where the trailing edge part is moved relative to the leading edge part beyond the upper operating position or beyond the lower operating position, col. 4, ln. 19),
wherein the connection assembly is configured such that the flap is movable relative to the main wing in a rotational manner (Examiner notes the pivot points and rotational links of the connection assembly),
wherein the second actuator unit is formed as a linear actuator that is mounted to the leading edge part and mounted to the trailing edge part in a position spaced apart from the pivot axis (Examiner notes the second actuator unit FHA is depicted as a hydraulic cylinder that extends and retracts producing a linear movement; Additionally, Examiner notes FHA is mounted to FF and AF via links in a position depicted in Fig. 8 that is spaced apart from the pivot axis F, G),
wherein the flap comprises a speed brake panel (spoiler S, best depicted in Fig. 7) mounted to the leading edge part or to the trailing edge part in a manner rotatable about a panel axis between a stowed position and a deployed position (Examiner notes spoiler S is mounted via swinging link CDE to the leading edge part or the trailing edge part, see Fig. 7-8, in a manner rotatable about panel axis Y between a stowed and deployed position, i.e.,  With the spoiler S pivoting downwardly (stowed), instead of upwardly (deployed) to interrupt air flow, it assists the lifting characteristics of the flaps FF, AF, col. 2, ln. 60),
wherein the flap is configured such that the speed brake panel is in the stowed position when the trailing edge part is operated within the operating range (col. 2, ln. 60; Examiner notes the panel is stowed to assist in lifting characteristics), and
such that the speed brake panel is in the deployed position when the trailing edge part is in the speed brake position (col. 2, ln. 60; Examiner notes the panel is deployed to interrupt airflow configured as a speed brake),
wherein the speed brake panel and the trailing edge part extend in opposite directions or in partly opposite directions when the speed brake panel is in the deployed position and the trailing edge part is in the speed brake position (Examiner notes the speed brake panel 30 pivots upwardly when deployed per col. 2, ln. 60; and Examiner notes the aft flap AF never pivots upwardly in any of the positions/configurations, therefore when the speed brake panel is in the deployed position and the trailing edge part is in the speed brake position the speed brake panel and the trailing edge part extend in opposite directions or partly opposite directions),
wherein the flap comprises an actuation mechanism (crank CR, Fig. 8) for moving the speed brake panel between the stowed position and the deployed position by transferring movement of the trailing edge part or of the second actuator unit to the speed brake panel (Examiner notes crank CR moves the speed brake panel 30 by transferring movement of the second actuator unit FHA to the panel 30; see col. 2, ln. 37).
However, the prior art does not teach the combined limitations of the claimed invention, specifically, wherein the panel axis is located at a trailing edge end of the speed brake panel.
