Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 102
The following is a quotation of the appropriate paragraphs of 35 U.S.C. 102 that form the basis for the rejections under this section made in this Office action:
A person shall be entitled to a patent unless –
(a)(1) the claimed invention was patented, described in a printed publication, or in public use, on sale, or otherwise available to the public before the effective filing date of the claimed invention.
Claims 1-4 and 6-12 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 102(a)(1) as being anticipated by Geiger (WO 2020123833).
Geiger discloses and shows a modular robot comprising:
a plurality of modules 30A/30B that are coupled together, wherein each of the plurality of modules includes:
a first link 18;
a second link 19 movably linked to the first link; and
a liquid pressure cylinder 34A/34B configured to move the first link and the second link relative to each other by a working fluid that is supplied to a cylinder chamber from a liquid pressure source 32A/32B located outside the plurality of modules and is discharged from the cylinder chamber to a tank 41 located outside the plurality of modules, wherein
the first link includes a cylinder block in which the cylinder chamber of the liquid pressure cylinder is formed, the cylinder block having a plurality of outer surfaces, and at least two of the plurality of outer surfaces of the cylinder block each have both a pump port connectable to the liquid pressure source and a tank port connectable to the tank (para. 0028).
Cl. 2 – the plurality of modules includes a first module 30A and a second module 30B that are coupled by connecting the first link or the second link of the first module to the first link or the second link of the second module (see Fig. 6, at 26A/B and 28A/B).
Cl. 3 – the cylinder block of the first module is connectable to the first link.
Cl. 4 – the plurality of modules are partially integrated.
Cl. 6 – the cylinder block includes a supply passage connecting the pump port to the cylinder chamber and a discharge passage connecting the tank port to the cylinder chamber (see Figs. 9-11).
Cl. 7 –  each module further includes:
a state-quantity detector 51A/51B configured to detect a state quantity of the module;
a control valve 46A/46B configured to control communication between the cylinder chamber and the pump port and the tank port; and
a controller 50A/50B configured to control motion of the module by controlling operation of the control valve based on a detection result from the state-quantity detector (para. 0034).
Cl. 8 –  at least one of the plurality of outer surfaces of the cylinder block has at least two supply-discharge ports in total, one of which is selectively connectable to one of the liquid pressure source or the tank, and another one of which is selectively connectable to the other one of the liquid pressure source or the tank (see Fig. 11), and
the cylinder block includes at least two passages respectively (not shown) connecting respective ones of the at least two supply-discharge ports to the cylinder chamber.
Cl. 9 – the first and second modules are identical to each other (Figs. 2-8).
Cl. 10 – the cylinder block of the first module has a joint hole (at 26A/B) into which a joint tool (dowel/pivot pins) is inserted, the joint tool being configured to connect the cylinder block with the first link.
Cl. 11 – in each module, the state quantity detected by the state-quantity detector is at least one of a stroke amount of the liquid pressure cylinder, a relative rotational angle between the first link and the second link thereof, a pressure in the liquid pressure cylinder, a load acting on the liquid pressure cylinder, a stroke speed of the liquid pressure cylinder, or a flow amount of the working fluid supplied to the liquid pressure cylinder (para. 0034).
Cl. 12 – the at least two supply-discharge ports are different from the pump and tank ports in each module (see Fig. 11).
