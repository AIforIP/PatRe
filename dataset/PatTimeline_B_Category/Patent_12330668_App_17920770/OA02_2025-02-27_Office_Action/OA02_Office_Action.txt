Allowable Subject Matter
The following is a statement of reasons for the indication of allowable subject matter:
In regard to the amended independent claims 1, 18 & 20; Velazquez et al., US 20210078581 A1, teaches SYSTEM AND METHOD FOR COORDINATING INDEPENDENT AXLES FOR CONTINUOUS WHEEL SLIP CONTROL, taken either individually or in combination with other prior art of record fails to teach or render obvious “a control system and to a method of controlling, receiving, producing and modifying torque requests to enable a sum of the first/second axle wheel torque to approach or satisfy the total torque request, controlled by a torque rate modifier” in regard to the independent claims 1, 18 & 20 limitations.
Further prosecution of the claims revealed that some of the claim elements may not have been disclosed by any prior art as a whole. There are some prior art which provides “torque request modification strategies for vehicles”, with addressing some of the claimed limitations, however, fail to teach or render obvious ALL claim elements, especially “a control system and a method of controlling a total driven wheel torque for a vehicle by controlling torque output , modifying torque requests to enable a sum of the first/second axle wheel torque to approach or satisfy the total torque request, controlled by a torque rate modifier” providing either alone or in combination with other prior art of record; in regard to the independent claims 1, 18 & 20 limitations.
Therefore, Applicant’s arguments, see Applicant Arguments/Remarks pages 1-3, filed on 01/21/2025, with respect to claims 1-20  under 35 U.S.C. § 102/103 have been fully considered and are persuasive; the rejection of claims 1-20  under 35 U.S.C. § 102/103 have been withdrawn.
Some of the closest prior art found on search which all are fail to disclose above limitations;
Velazquez Alcantar; Jose et al., US 20210078581 A1, SYSTEM AND METHOD FOR COORDINATING INDEPENDENT AXLES FOR CONTINUOUS WHEEL SLIP CONTROL;
Remarks: Discloses a control system and a method of controlling a total driven wheel torque for a vehicle by controlling torque output of different torque sources having different torque response capabilities. However, fails to disclose the claim elements at steps 1-2 above.
Aswani; Deepak et al., US 20090234551 A1, INCREASED CAPABILITY MODULAR VEHICLE-DYNAMICS CONTROL ARCHITECTURE;
Remarks: Discloses a system and method for a vehicle-dynamics control architecture in hybrid electric vehicles (HEVs). A coordination layer controls the torque generating device to generate the first/second amount of torque in response to the arbitrated torque request signal and the torque reservation request signal indicating that the vehicle is in the non-anticipated/anticipated vehicle dynamic state. However, fails to disclose the claim elements at steps 1-2 above.
As a result, claims  1-20 are allowed;
Claims 1, 18 & 20 are allowed independent claims.
Claims 2-17 are allowed due to dependencies to the allowed claim 1.
Claim 19 is allowed due to dependencies to the allowed claim 18.
