Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The prior art of record neither anticipates nor renders obvious the claimed subject matter of the instant application as a whole either taken alone or in combination.  In particular, the prior art of record does not teach the gripper mechanism further having a piston rod comprising a driving shaft, a cross slider coupling  and a gripping head which are connected in turn, wherein the gripping head  partly protrudes from a cylinder barrel, and  the gripping head  screwed into or out of a connecting hole in a part to be gripped to grip and release the part to be gripped the cross slider  is provided with a third arc surface  matched with a first arc surface on a side, facing a second end face of the cross slider, so that relative rotation around a first direction and relative sliding in the first direction are able to be realized between the cross slider  and a first sleeve; and wherein the cross slider is provided with a fourth arc surface  matched with a second arc surface on a side, facing a fourth end face.
