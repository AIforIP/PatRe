Allowable Subject Matter

The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: the prior art of record, including that which is listed on the attached PTO-892, too numerous to list here, while teaching a number of lighting pods for attachment to an aircraft including housings, brackets, and electrical interfaces, fails to disclose or render obvious in particular an aircraft lighting pod having a housing of which the front has a nose formed by two diagonal flat sides and a head above the nose, the left has two diagonal sides, and the right has two diagonal sides, different lights are mounted to the diagonal flat sides, the head, and the diagonal sides on the left and right; and a vibration-proof and G-force absorbing coupling bracket mounted to a base plate at the bottom of the housing configured to mount the lighting pod to the aircraft as called for in the claimed combination of independent claim 1.
