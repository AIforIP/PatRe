Allowable Subject Matter
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
In regard to the independent claims 1 & 23-25, ANDERSON et al., US 20200225684 A1, teaches PERSISTENT AERIAL COMMUNICATION AND CONTROL SYSTEM, taken either individually or in combination with other prior art of record fails to teach or render obvious “controlling an aircraft to avoid overheating components such as controlling the electric propulsion unit of the aircraft to avoid exceeding the temperature limit based on the determined proximity.” as inventive differences emphasized in claims 1 & 23-25.
Claim 1 (with common inventive elements of claims 23-25): “A method /A system of controlling an aircraft, comprising: receiving, during flight, first sensor data indicating at least one attribute of high voltage wiring of the aircraft; receiving, during flight, a state of operation of the aircraft; determining, during flight, a proximity of a temperature of the high voltage wiring to a temperature limit based on the first sensor data and the state of operation of the aircraft; and controlling at least one electric propulsion unit of the aircraft to avoid exceeding the temperature limit based on the determined proximity.”
Further prosecution of the amended claims revealed that some of the claim elements may not have been disclosed by any prior art as a whole. There are some prior art which provides “A motor/battery control of an electric aircraft”, with addressing some of the claimed limitations, however, fail to teach or render obvious ALL claim elements, especially “receiving, during flight: “high voltage wiring” data & “a state of operation”  of the aircraft; determining, during flight, a proximity of a temperature of the high voltage wiring” providing either alone or in combination with other prior art of record; in regard to the independent claims 1 & 23-25 limitations.
Therefore, Applicant’s arguments, see Applicant Arguments/Remarks pages 7-12, filed on 02/18/2025, with respect to claims 1-25 have been fully considered and are persuasive;
the objection of claim 19  have been withdrawn.
the rejection of claims 1-25,  under 35 USC § 102 & 103, have been withdrawn.
Some of the closest prior art found on search which all are fail to disclose above limitations;
ANDERSON; Bretton E. et al.	US 20200225684 A1	PERSISTENT AERIAL COMMUNICATION AND CONTROL SYSTEM;
Remarks: Discloses systems and operational methods for powering and controlling flight of an unmanned aerial vehicle. The control station and aerial vehicle include power management systems and control circuits to control takeoff, flight operation and landing of the vehicle. However, fails to disclose the claim elements at steps 1-2 above.
Hirono, Daisuke	US 20040124808 A1	Motor control system;
Remarks: Discloses a motor control system for driving a motor with a PWM control using an electric power converter such as a three-phase inverter, etc. Junction temperature reduction processing is performed to make the junction temperature equal to or less than the temperature limit, whereby the switching elements can effectively be used to their maximum temperature limit, thus expanding the operating range of a motor. However, fails to disclose the claim elements at steps 1-2 above.
Followell; David Allan et al., US 9784638 B1, Method and system for diagnoses and prognoses of low temperature limit control valve;
Remarks: Discloses a method and system for diagnosing and predicting performance below a specified performance level of a low temperature limit valve in an environmental control system (ECS). However, fails to disclose the claim elements at steps 1-2 above.
The Examiner reasoning based on this specific claim limitation clearly present in the allowed claims which is neither taught nor suggested by the prior art as a whole, either alone or in combination.
As a result, claims 1-25 are allowed;
Claims 1 & 23-25 is allowed independent claims.
Claims 2-22 are allowed due to dependencies to the allowed claim 1.
