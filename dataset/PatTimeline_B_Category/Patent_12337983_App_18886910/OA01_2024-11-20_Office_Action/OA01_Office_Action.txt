wherein the proximity to the temperature limit is based on a proximity to a curve proportional to (current of the high voltage wiring)^2 * (a temperature of the high voltage wiring) ([0041] In FIG. 4, Td denotes a temperature detected by the temperature sensor 6; Ts-Tj, a value obtained by subtracting a calculated junction temperature Tj of the switching elements from a predetermined temperature limit Ts; X1, a junction temperature limit line; and OR1, an operating range (dot-meshed portion in FIG. 4) of the switching elements formed below the junction temperature limit line X1.
