Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph:
The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the applicant regards as his invention.
Claims 1 and 16 rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor (or for applications subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 112, the applicant), regards as the invention.
It is not clear to the examiner what “an artificial intelligence (AI) software” encompass  and not clear if the AI software is also capable of performing other functions other than for example the “…obtain at least one of space and earth observation data collected by the observation antenna arrays of the satellites in the multi- satellite formation, obtain radiation sensor data from the sensor unit and pre-known knowledge or artificial intelligence from the artificial intelligence software and dynamically and continuously change the configuration and shape of said multi-satellite formation…” as recited in claim 1. Since the AI software is claimed to be an “intelligent” does it capable of performing limitations that are not explicitly disclosed in the claims? Can the control system be just a “software”? Appropriate correction/clarification required.
In claim 1, lines 18-19, it is also not clear what a “pre-known knowledge” specifically  is and what encompasses.  The applicant have to specify what a parameters are considered” a pre-known knowledge” and where this data is coming from. Appropriate correction/clarification required.
The method claim 16 is rejected under the same rational as the rejection of claim 1 above.
Dependent claims 2, 9-15 are rejected under the same rational as the rejection of claim 1 above solely based on their dependencies from the rejected claim 1 above.
