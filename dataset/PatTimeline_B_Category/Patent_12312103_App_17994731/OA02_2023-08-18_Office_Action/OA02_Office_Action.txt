Response to Arguments
Applicant's arguments filed 1 and 16 have been fully considered but they are not persuasive.
Applicant's arguments fail to comply with 37 CFR 1.111(b) because they amount to a general allegation that the claims define a patentable invention without specifically pointing out how the language of the claims patentably distinguishes them from the references.
The applicant’s argument on page 4 of the remarks that “…In contrast to Song, the present invention is based on the idea that antenna elements on multiple satellites are virtually aggregated together to form a single large antenna array, and hence requiring a formation flying system to satisfy the requirements of missions that use distributed antennas on multiple satellites to form a single large aggregated virtual aperture for certain applications” does not specifically point out what limitation from the claim 1 and 16 is the combination of Song and Stuart does not teach.
In regards to the applicant’s argument on page 5 of the remarks that the space facing array 18 is not a separate antenna array from antenna array 12, the examiner respectfully disagrees. The “DomeSat” configuration of Fig. 1 of Stuart clearly teaches a satellite system with at least two antenna array directed in mutually different directions (18 (space facing array) and 12 (earth facing array), Fig. 1) as claimed in claim 1. (i.e. they are different antenna rays in different directions). The applicant also seem to argue limitations that are not recited in the claims.
The limitation “…for improved tracking capabilities in that the antenna array of at least one of the satellites in the satellite formation thereby can be adapted to the movement patterns of the object whose observation data is currently collected by at least one of the satellites in the formation” of claim 1, similar limitation in the method claim of 16, is a ‘functional limitation’ and the combination of Song and Stuart references are also capable of performing the identified limitation since the antenna arrays of the satellites in formation can be adapt to the movement pattern of the object as desired via the control system.
In order to overcome, the above rejection, the applicant is advised to amend the claims to structurally distinguish the claimed inventions from the applied prior arts.
