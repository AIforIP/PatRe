Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: the best prior art of Lee (US 10640207 B2) discloses a similar tiltable propulsion system and has a coupling mechanism (111+120+61+131). However the Lee coupling mechanism is rocker slider crank, and not a fourbar crank slider as required by the claims. Modifying the Lee reference would require impermissible hindsight since it changes the mechanical advantage/kinematics.
The other cited prior art also provides varying coupling mechanisms which are less similar to the Lee reference and the current claims. Typically, modern coupling is done with electric controllers and actuators.
