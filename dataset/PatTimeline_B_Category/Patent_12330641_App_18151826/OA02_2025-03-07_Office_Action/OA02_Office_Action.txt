Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-10 are allowed.
The following is an Examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
WANG (CN 109624851) is the closest prior art of record.
Regarding claim 1, WANG discloses a method for a motor vehicle performed using images of a roadway captured by at least one camera of the motor vehicle (abstract, line 4), the method comprising the following steps:
projecting a traffic lane of the roadway to be followed onto the images (abstract, lines 8-10);
determining a travel trajectory of the motor vehicle in reference to the images based on a self-motion estimate (implied abstract, lines 4-6); and
projecting the determined travel trajectory onto the images (abstract, lines 4-7).
The prior art fails to teach or render obvious the limitation “deriving a control variable from a comparison between the traffic lane projected onto the images and the determined travel trajectory projected onto the images; and controlling a lateral guidance of the motor vehicle based on the derived control variable” in the manner defined in claim 1.
Regarding claim 9, WANG discloses a non-transitory computer-readable storage medium on which is stored a computer program that is executable by a computer or control device and that, when executed by the computer or control device, causes the computer or control device to perform a method for a motor vehicle (implied, pg. 5 lines 9-11), the method being performed using images of a roadway captured by at least one camera of the motor vehicle (abstract, line 4), the method comprising the following steps:
projecting a traffic lane of the roadway to be followed onto the images (abstract, lines 8-10);
determining a travel trajectory of the motor vehicle in reference to the images based on a self-motion estimate (implied abstract, lines 4-6);
projecting the determined travel trajectory onto the images (abstract, lines 4-7).
The prior art fails to teach or render obvious the limitation “deriving a control variable from a comparison between the traffic lane projected onto the images and the determined travel trajectory projected onto the images; and controlling a lateral guidance of the motor vehicle based on the derived control variable” in the manner defined in claim 9.
Regarding claim 10, WANG discloses a motor vehicle, comprising: at least one camera which, during operation, captures images of a roadway (abstract, line 4); and a control device communicatively connected to the at least one camera (implied, pg. 5 lines 9-11), wherein the control device is configured to:
project a traffic lane of the roadway to be followed onto the images (abstract, lines 8-10);
determine a travel trajectory of the motor vehicle in reference to the images based on a self-motion estimate (implied abstract, lines 4-6);
project the determined travel trajectory onto the images (abstract, lines 4-7).
The prior art fails to teach or render obvious the limitation “derive a control variable from a comparison between the traffic lane projected onto the images and the determined travel trajectory projected onto the images; and control a lateral guidance of the motor vehicle based on the derived control variable” in the manner defined in claim 10.
