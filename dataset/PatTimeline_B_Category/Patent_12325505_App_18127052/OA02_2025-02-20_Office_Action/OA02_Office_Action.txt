Claims 1-16 allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: prior art US 20080230654 A1 Velicki; Alexander et al. teaches 2 parallel hydrogen tanks, US 20050230554 A1 Schoene, Wofram teaches hydrogen tanks in the aft section of the fuselage, but fails to teach two parallel tanks.
