Allowable Subject Matter
Claim(s) 1-8, 10-13 are allowed over the prior art of record.
The closest prior art of record is US 2022/0348227, (hereinafter Foster), US 2021/0107510 (hereinafter Kato), US 2022/0135039 (hereinafter Jardine), and WO 2020/230304 A1, (hereinafter Taniguchi).
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The primary prior art reference Foster discloses an invention that provides an autonomous vehicle that includes features that allows the autonomous vehicle to comply with applicable regulations and statutes for performing safe driving operation. Foster discloses determining when to fulfill a lane change intention related to a planned or unplanned exit, and determining when the autonomous vehicle is within a predetermined distance from its exit and needs to move to the right lane. Foster further discloses lane change deniers in a case that a lane change would move the autonomous away from the lane the autonomous vehicle needs to be in for an upcoming planned exit that is less than a predetermined distance away; a lane change that would move away from the lane needed to be in to avoid an unplanned exit that is less than a predetermined distance away; and a lane change that would move the autonomous vehicle away from a pre-configured trajectory or an upcoming intersection turn.
The next prior art reference Kato discloses an invention that provides a vehicle control device comprising a driving controller. Kato discloses that the driving controller includes a lane change controller that includes changing lanes from a host vehicle traveling lane in which a host vehicle travels to a lane adjacent to the host vehicle traveling lane. Kato discloses that the lane change controller includes a first operation of changing lanes according to a request from an occupant of the host vehicle and a second operation of changing lanes on the basis of a result of recognition. Kato discloses that a lane change request can be received from the occupant when an operation of the turn signal lever or an operation of the lane change start switch is received. Kato discloses that, when it is determined that the execution conditions for a lane change are not satisfied, the lane change controller does not execute control for causing the host vehicle to change lanes. Kato discloses to notify the occupant the that the lane change cannot be executed and the reason why the lane change cannot be executed. Kato discloses, when a lane change to another lane present in a direction opposite to an adjacent lane to which a lane change is stopped is performed on the basis of a position of the host vehicle, the lane change controller makes the first period shorter than when a lane change to the lane to which a lane change is stopped is performed again.
The next prior art reference Jardine discloses an invention that provides a control system and method for a host vehicle operable in an autonomous mode and configured to cause control of a speed and direction of the host vehicle. Jardine discloses inhibiting a change of lanes of the host vehicle if a condition is satisfied, such as a change of separation between at least two objects external to the host vehicle. Jardine discloses inhibiting a change of lanes in the autonomous mode and inhibiting a manual change of lanes in non-autonomous mode.
The next prior art reference Taniguchi discloses an invention that provides a vehicle travel control method and device including autonomous driving control. Taniguchi discloses a lane change information indication whether to permit execution of an automatic lane change function if a start condition for the automatic lane change function is met. Taniguchi discloses that if the lane change direction presented in the lane change information and the lane change direction instructed by the lane change instruction operation area opposite to each other, the consent input is cancelled. Taniguchi discloses, as a result, even if the driver approves the proposal for changing lanes in the system trigger mode, the driver's intention to change lanes can be prioritized and the lane change function in the driver trigger mode can be executed.
Regarding Claim 1, the prior art taken either individually or in combination with other prior art of record fails to disclose, suggest, teach, or render obvious the invention as a whole:
A travel assistance method for a vehicle, the method comprising:
determining whether a route-following lane change along a set travel route can be assisted with autonomous lane change control;
in response to a determination that the route-following lane change can be assisted with the autonomous lane change control, autonomously controlling the vehicle to make the route-following lane change in a first direction;
in response to a determination that the route-following lane change cannot be assisted with the autonomous lane change control, notifying a driver that the route-following lane change cannot be made with the autonomous lane change control, and prohibiting the autonomous lane change control from assisting a lane change in a second direction opposite to the first direction even in a case where an operation by the driver would otherwise cause the autonomous lane change control to assist with the lane change in the second direction.
Claims 1-6, 8, 10-12 depend from Claim 1 are therefore allowable.
