Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph:
The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the applicant regards as his invention.
Claims 1-12 and 22 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor (or for applications subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 112, the applicant), regards as the invention.
Claim 1 recites “…the one or more mounting plates are configured to provide a base on which one or more of the splice straps securely mount to one or both the one or more struts or the wing, and wherein the one or more of the splice straps are secured directly to one or more ribs of the wings…” which renders the claim indefinite as there are two contrasting limitations i.e., splice straps securely mounted to one or more mounting plates and splice straps secured directly to one or more ribs of the wings. For the purpose of the examination the limitation is interpreted as ““…the one or more mounting plates are configured to provide a base on which one or more of the splice straps securely mount to one or both the one or more struts or the wing, and wherein the one or more of the splice straps are secured directly over one or more ribs of the wings…”.
Claims not addressed above are rejected due to their dependency on rejected base claims.
