DETAILED ACTION
The present application, filed on or after March 16, 2013, is being examined under the first inventor to file provisions of the AIA .
Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-7, 9-11, 13-14, 16-20 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Claim 1 recites, inter alia, an aircraft light with a plurality of contact areas for a selectable light source position, an optical element over the light source,  and “the exterior aircraft light is a wing-mounted forward navigation light; wherein the light output of the forward navigation light has a cut-off angle (γ) with respect to the light source support, and wherein a first cut-off angle (γ), present when the light source is arranged at a first end region of the first and second groups of electric contact areas, differs from a second cut-off angle (γ), present when the light source is arranged at a second end region of the first and second groups of electric contact areas°.”
The references of record do not teach or suggest the aforementioned limitations, nor would it be obvious to modify those references to include such limitations.
Prior art reference Heimendahl (US 2017/0073083 A1) discloses “An exterior aircraft light (2, Fig. 4ab), comprising: a light source (4, Fig. 4ab) having a first electrode and a second electrode (since the light sources are LEDs, they have two electrodes); a light source support (12, Fig. 4ab) for supporting the light source; wherein the light source support comprises a first group of electric contact areas, which are spaced from each other and which are electrically connected with each other, and a second group of electric contact areas, which are spaced from each other and which are electrically connected with each other (¶ [0064] since the LED array is two-dimensional, there are a plurality of contact areas that are spaced from each other).”
However, Heimendahl does not explicitly disclose “wherein the light source support comprises conductive traces for supplying electric energy to the light source” and “wherein the first group of electric contact areas and the second group of electric contact areas form an extended electric contact structure that allows for the light source to be positioned on the light source support in a plurality of different positions; and wherein the first electrode of the light source is soldered to a subset of the first group of electric contact areas and wherein the second electrode of the light source is soldered to a subset of the second group of electric contact areas” and “the exterior aircraft light is a wing-mounted forward navigation light; wherein the light output of the forward navigation light has a cut-off angle (γ) with respect to the light source support, and wherein a first cut-off angle (γ), present when the light source is arranged at a first end region of the first and second groups of electric contact areas, differs from a second cut-off angle (γ), present when the light source is arranged at a second end region of the first and second groups of electric contact areas°.”.
Prior art reference Feng (US 2023/0025975 A1 ) discloses an LED arrangement lighting device with a plurality of spaced apart contact areas (4, Fig. 4-6) that allows for the light sources to be positioned on the support in a plurality of different positions (seen in Fig. 4-6) and soldered to them (¶ [0024]).
However, Heimendahl and Feng do not disclose ““the exterior aircraft light is a wing-mounted forward navigation light; wherein the light output of the forward navigation light has a cut-off angle (γ) with respect to the light source support, and wherein a first cut-off angle (γ), present when the light source is arranged at a first end region of the first and second groups of electric contact areas, differs from a second cut-off angle (γ), present when the light source is arranged at a second end region of the first and second groups of electric contact areas°.”
Claims 2-7, 9-10, 13, 16-19 are allowed due to their dependence on Claim 1.
Claim 11 is allowed for similar reasons as claim 1.
Claims 12 and 20 are allowed due to their dependence on claim 11.
Claim 15 recites, inter alia, a method of assembling an exterior aircraft light with a plurality of contact areas for a selectable light source position, an optical element over the light source,  and “the exterior aircraft light is a wing-mounted forward navigation light and wherein the method comprises: determining a wing angle (α1, α2) of an aircraft wing with respect to an aircraft fuselage; selecting the position of the light source on the light source support depending on the determined wing angle (α1, α2).”
The references of record do not teach or suggest the aforementioned limitations, nor would it be obvious to modify those references to include such limitations.
