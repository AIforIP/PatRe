Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 38-40 are allowed.
The following is a statement of reasons for the indication of allowable subject matter:
Regarding claim 38, Dowty discloses an aircraft passenger seat unit comprising:
a seat (Dowty, ¶127, suite includes a seat);
a privacy shell at least partially surrounding the seat (Dowty, items 2510, 2504, 2508, and 2506, and ¶127-128, form a privacy shell surrounding the seat), the privacy shell being made up of a plurality of walls, including two walls extending in the same plane, and having a primary opening between the two walls allow entry to and exit from the seat unit (Dowty, figure 26e, items 2609 and 2602, door at opening for the privacy shell with left and right sides of door defining a first and second edge); and
a door (Dowty, figure 26e, items 2609 and 2502), movable between a first position in which the door at least partially blocks off the primary opening (Dowty, figure 26e, item 2609, solid line shows door in a position where the door partially blocks of the primary opening), and a second position, in which the door provides at least partial access to the primary opening (Dowty, figure 26e, item 2609 and ¶134, door moves between an opened and a closed position wherein it closes to cover the side edge where the storage opening is located), wherein the door is arranged to move between the first and second positions in a direction offset by an angle from the two parallel walls of the privacy shell (Dowty, figure 26e, see callout below, door direction of movement is offset from an angle of a first (left) edge of the opening), and
wherein the door is arranged to slide between the first position and the second position in a direction along a length of the door (Dowty, figure 26e, items 2602 and 2609, door slides between different positions along a length of the door), except:
wherein the two walls are parallel
wherein the door is arranged to move between the first and second positions in a direction offset by an angle from the two parallel walls of the privacy shell;
and wherein the door is angled relative to the two parallel walls of the privacy shell in the secondary position.
Dowty also teaches the two walls being parallel.
The prior art fails to teach the limitations “wherein the door is arranged to move between the first and second positions in a direction offset by an angle from the two parallel walls of the privacy shell; and wherein the door is angled relative to the two parallel walls of the privacy shell in the secondary position.”
Thus, the prior art fails to teach the claimed matter alone and it would not have been obvious to meet the claims either without undue hindsight based on the applicant' s disclosure.
Claims 39-40 are allowed for being dependent on claim 38.
