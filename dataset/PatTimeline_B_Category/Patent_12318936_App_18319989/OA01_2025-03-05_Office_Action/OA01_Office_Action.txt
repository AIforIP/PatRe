Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: The most relevant prior art identified is Readings et al., US 2022/0324116, hereon ‘116 which describes the tuning of a robotic system. However, it lacks several key elements that are explicitly claimed in the independent claims of this application.
The primary distinctions are as follows:
The claimed invention incorporates machine learning as a core component, whereas the prior art primarily relies on empirical tuning methods. ‘116 focuses on tuning through measurement feedback, while the claimed invention employs a rigid body model with distinct partial torque calculations to enhance accuracy.
The closest comparable feature in the prior art is the determination of actuator configuration parameters, which considers state measurements. However, it does not explicitly disclose the use of separate models for torque computation as claimed.
Therefore, the claimed invention presents a novel approach by integrating machine learning, structured torque modeling, and multi-model computation, distinguishing it from the prior art on record.
