Examiner’s Statements of Reasons for Allowance
The closest art is Wu (NPL Title: Closed-Loop Pose Control and Automated Suturing of Continuum Surgical Manipulators With Customized Wrist Markers Under Stereo Vision) and Tanzini (WO Pub. No. 2022175810).
Regarding the teachings of Wu, Wu teaches closed-loop pose control with wrist markers with real-time pose feedback. Wu fails to expressly disclose accumulating pose differences over multiple detection cycles or generating a control signal when those accumulated differences reach an error threshold.
Regarding the teachings of Tanzini, Tanzini discloses anomaly detection for an unconstrained master device by monitoring positions/velocities. Tanzini fails to expressly disclose specific steps of recognizing multiple pose identifications and an angle identification from an image (including the eight-direction searching, thresholds, and re-determination logic) and does not disclose generating distinct alarm signals for a failed arm-control vs. a failed drive device. Therefore, the claims are allowable.
