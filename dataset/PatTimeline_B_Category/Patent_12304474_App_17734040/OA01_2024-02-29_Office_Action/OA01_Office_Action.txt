Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 103
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 103 which forms the basis for all obviousness rejections set forth in this Office action:
A patent for a claimed invention may not be obtained, notwithstanding that the claimed invention is not identically disclosed as set forth in section 102, if the differences between the claimed invention and the prior art are such that the claimed invention as a whole would have been obvious before the effective filing date of the claimed invention to a person having ordinary skill in the art to which the claimed invention pertains. Patentability shall not be negated by the manner in which the invention was made.
Claim 5 is rejected under 35 U.S.C. 103 as being unpatentable over Anderson, as applied to claim 1, in view of Visintainer et al. (US 2021/0118295 A1, “Visintainer”).
Regarding claim 5, Anderson does not explicitly teach wherein one or more surrounding vehicles demonstrating threatening behavior further comprises a second surrounding vehicle in an adjacent lane to the vehicle slowing down in association with the first surrounding vehicle. However, Visintainer discloses automotive driver assistance and teaches:
wherein one or more surrounding vehicles demonstrating threatening behavior further comprises a second surrounding vehicle in an adjacent lane to the vehicle slowing down in association with the first surrounding vehicle. (In the above-considered crowded motorway scenario, a Nearby Motor-Vehicle proceeding the Host Motor-Vehicle of approximately a hundred meters, basically at the same speed and in the same direction, is not added to the list of Relevant Motor-Vehicles because it is not about to collide with the Host Motor-Vehicle and is not in critical driving conditions, whereas a Nearby Motor-Vehicle that precedes the Host Motor-Vehicle and travels in an adjacent lane, hence, has a trajectory that does not intersect the one covered by the Host Motor-Vehicle, but has abruptly started braking, is added to the list of Relevant Motor-Vehicles because it is not about to collide with the Host Motor-Vehicle but it is in critical driving conditions, as well as a Nearby Motor-Vehicle that is travelling in front of the Host Motor-Vehicle and is slowing down is also added to the list of Relevant Motor-Vehicles because it represents a potential collision threat – See at least ¶ [0088])
In summary, Anderson discloses identifying threatening objects within the environment, including a front vehicle slowing, and then assigning a threat metric to each of those objects. Anderson does not explicitly teach does not explicitly teach wherein one or more surrounding vehicles demonstrating threatening behavior further comprises a second surrounding vehicle in an adjacent lane to the vehicle slowing down in association with the first surrounding vehicle. However, Visintainer discloses automotive driver assistance and discloses identifying objects in the area that are acting abnormally and then adding those vehicles to a “Relevant Motor-Vehicle” list, i.e., a list which identifies these vehicles as potentially dangerous. This includes a vehicle in an adjacent lane from the ego vehicle acting in an abnormal way during a same time period as a frontal vehicle is slowing down.
Therefore it would have been obvious to a person having ordinary skill in the art before the effective filing date of the instant application to have modified the integrated framework for vehicle operator assistance based on a trajectory and threat assessment of Anderson to provide for the automatic driver assistance, as taught in Visintainer, to provide an automotive advanced driver assistance system which implements applications based on a V21 communication or on a combined V2V-V21 communication, according to the SAE J2735 standard and, similarly, to the ETSI ITS-G5 standard, which are based on infrastructure and relative messaging services provided for by the state of the art and, in particular, on: warnings of relevant and/or dangerous road events (traffic, weather, still vehicle), dynamic speed limits, information concerning road signs (prohibitory signs, mandatory signs, danger warning signs, suggestion signs), traffic light phases and local maps, in particular at intersections. (At Visintainer ¶ [0015])
