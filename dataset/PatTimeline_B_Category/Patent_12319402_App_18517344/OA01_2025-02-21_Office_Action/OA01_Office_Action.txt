Regarding Claim 1, there is no prior art nor reasons to modify any prior art to have in each link the three coupling points are distributed forming a triangular geometry, wherein a side Ai-Ci is defined by the coupling points Ai and Ci; wherein a side Bi-Ci is defined by the coupling points Bi and Ci; and wherein a side Ai-Bi is defined by the coupling points Ai and Bi; wherein a length of the side B1-C1 of the first link is equal to a length of the side B4-C4 of the fourth link; and wherein a length of the side A2-B2 of the second link is equal to a length of the side A3-B3 of the third link The limitations stated along with the other limitations of the independent claim give reason for allowance.
