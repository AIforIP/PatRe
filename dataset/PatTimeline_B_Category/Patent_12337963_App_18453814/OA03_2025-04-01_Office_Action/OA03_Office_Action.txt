Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-16 and 21-24 are allowed.
The following is a statement of reasons for the indication of allowable subject matter:
Regarding claim 1, Santini (US 9296469 B2) discloses a wing for use with an aircraft (Santini, figure 3, item 302), the wing comprising:
a wing root comprising a leading edge, a trailing edge, and a distal end (Santini, figure 2, item 126, wing with root, leading edge, trailing edge, and distal end);
a wing tip comprising a leading edge, positioned proximate to the distal end of the wing root (Santini, figure 2, item 122, wing tip at end of wing), and configured to be foldable, relative to the wing root, along a folding plane that is co- planar with or parallel to a wing root plane of the wing root (Santini, figure 2, item 122, wing tip folds aftwards parallel to the wing root); and
a wing-tip hinge pivotally coupling the wing tip to the wing root and defining a wing-tip axis that is perpendicular to the folding plane (Santini, figure 4, item 422), wherein:
the wing tip is pivotable about the wing-tip axis, within the folding plane, between, and inclusive of, an extended position and a retracted position, relative to the wing root (Santini, figure 6, item 610, rotation axis of joint);
when in the extended position, the leading edge of the wing tip extends from the leading edge of the wing root in a spanwise direction of the wing root (Santini, figure 6, item 608, wing tip in extended position);
and when in the retracted position, the leading edge of the wing tip extends from the leading edge of the wing root in a chordwise direction of the wing root (Santini, figure 6, item 608, wing tip in retracted position);
an upper wing-tip panel and a lower wing-tip panel (Santini, figure 4, items 430 and 432, first and second surface pivoted about axis of a second hinge with respect to wing tip);
the wing-tip panels are configured to be pivotable about a first panel axis (Santini, figure 4, items 430, col 7, lines 45-49, pivoted about an axis by a hinge);
except:
the lower wing-tip panel is pivotable, relative to the wing tip, and extends vertically downward from the wing tip when the wing tip is in the retracted position.
Alternatively, Alvarez-Calderon (US 3333792 A) teaches a wing-tip hinge pivotally coupling the wing tip to the wing root and defining a wing-tip axis that is perpendicular to the folding plane (Alvarez-Calderon, figure 1, item 3).
Santini and Alvarez-Calderon are both considered analogous art as they are both in the same field of retractable aircraft wing tips. It would have been obvious before the effective filing date of the application for one of ordinary skill in the art to modify the wing tip of Santini with the hinge between the wing tip and the end of the main wing of Alvarez-Calderon with a reasonable expectation of success in order to provide a redundant mechanism for preventing the wing tip from becoming un-attached.
The prior art fails to teach separate wing tip panel where one panel hinges upwards and the other wing tip panel hinges downwards.
Thus, the prior art fails to teach the claimed matter alone and it would not have been obvious to meet the claims either without undue hindsight based on the applicant' s disclosure.
Regarding claim 3, Santini discloses the wing of claim 2, except:
further comprising:
a bell crank comprising a first bell-crank end, a second bell-crank end, and a fixed pivot point between the first bell-crank end and the second bell-crank end; and
a bell-crank hinge defining a bell-crank axis that is parallel to the wing-tip axis, the bell- crank hinge being mounted to the wing root aft of the wing-tip hinge;
wherein:
the first bell-crank end is mounted to the second actuator end;
the second bell-crank end is mechanically coupled to the wing tip; and the fixed pivot point is pivotally coupled to the bell-crank hinge so that the bell crank is pivotable about the bell-crank axis.
Alvarez-Calderon (US 3333792 A) teaches a bell crank comprising a first bell-crank end (Alvarez-Calderon, figure 1, item 19), a second bell-crank end (Alvarez-Calderon, figure 1, item 17), and a fixed pivot point between the first bell-crank end and the second bell-crank end (Alvarez-Calderon, figure 1, item 11); and
a bell-crank hinge defining a bell-crank axis that is parallel to the wing-tip axis, the bell- crank hinge being mounted to the wing root (Alvarez-Calderon, figure 1, items 3 and 11);
wherein:
the first bell-crank end is mounted to the second actuator end (Alvarez-Calderon, figure 1, item 23);
the second bell-crank end is mechanically coupled to the wing tip (Alvarez-Calderon, figure 1, item 13 and 17, bell crank end coupled with wing tip via coupler); and
the fixed pivot point is pivotally coupled to the bell-crank hinge so that the bell crank is pivotable about the bell-crank axis (Alvarez-Calderon, figure 1, item 11, bell crank hinged to a fixed pivot point of the wing).
Schweighart (US 20100019080 A1) also teaches a bell crank comprising a first bell-crank end (Schweighart, figure 1, item 32), a second bell-crank end (Schweighart, figure 1, item 13), and a fixed pivot point between the first bell-crank end and the second bell-crank end (Schweighart, figure 1, item 12); and
a bell-crank hinge defining a bell-crank axis that is parallel to the wing-tip axis, the bell- crank hinge being mounted to the wing root (Schweighart, figure 1, items 5 and 12);
wherein:
the first bell-crank end is mounted to the second actuator end (Schweighart, figure 1, item 33);
the second bell-crank end is mechanically coupled to the wing tip (Schweighart, figure 1, items 7 and 50, bell crank end coupled with wing tip via coupler); and
the fixed pivot point is pivotally coupled to the bell-crank hinge so that the bell crank is pivotable about the bell-crank axis (Alvarez-Calderon, figure 1, item 11, bell crank hinged to a fixed pivot point of the wing).
The prior art fails to teach the bell crank hinge aft of the wing tip hinge.
Thus, the prior art fails to teach the claimed matter alone and it would not have been obvious to meet the claims either without undue hindsight based on the applicant' s disclosure.
Regarding claim 11, Santini discloses a wing-tip assembly comprising:
a wing tip (Santini, figures 6-8, item 608, 708, and 808) comprising a panel cutout defined by a panel-attachment edge and a panel- abutment edge (Santini, figures 6-8, item 612 and 712, panel cutout with a panel installed), wherein the wing tip is pivotable about a wing-tip axis (Santini, figures 6-8, item 610, 710, and 810, wing tip pivots about wing tip axis);
an upper wing-tip panel comprising a tip-attachment edge and a tip-abutment edge, the upper wing-tip panel being pivotable about a first panel axis that is angled relative to the wing- tip axis, wherein the upper wing-tip panel is pivotally attached to the panel-attachment edge of the wing tip by the tip-attachment edge of the upper wing-tip panel (Santini, figure 4, item 430, first surface attached to the hinge at an angle with respect to the wing pivot axis); and
a lower wing-tip panel comprising a tip-attachment edge and a tip-abutment edge, the lower wing-tip panel being pivotable about a second panel axis that is parallel to the first panel axis, wherein the lower wing-tip panel is pivotally attached to the panel-attachment edge of the wing tip by the tip-attachment edge of the lower wing-tip panel (Santini, figure 4, item 432, second surface attached to the hinge at an angle with respect to the wing pivot axis and parallel to the first panel axis);
wherein:
the wing tip is pivotable about the wing-tip axis, between, and inclusive of, an extended position and a retracted position (Santini, figures 6-8, wing tips travel between extended and retracted positions);
when in the extended position, the tip-abutment edge of the upper wing-tip panel and the tip-abutment edge of the lower wing-tip panel are laterally aligned with the panel-abutment edge of the wing tip (Santini, figure 6, item 624, wingtip and panel abutment edges are laterally aligned); and
when in the retracted position, the tip-abutment edge of the upper wing-tip panel and the tip-abutment edge of the lower wing-tip panel are unaligned with the panel-abutment edge, such that the tip-abutment edge of the upper wing-tip panel and lower wing-tip panel extends vertically downward from the panel-abutment edge of the wing tip (Santini, figure 7, item 712)
except:
wherein the upper panel extends vertically upward from the panel-abutment edge of the wing tip and the tip-abutment edge of the lower wing-tip panel extends vertically downward from the panel-abutment edge of the wing tip
Thus, the prior art fails to teach the claimed matter alone and it would not have been obvious to meet the claims either without undue hindsight based on the applicant' s disclosure.
