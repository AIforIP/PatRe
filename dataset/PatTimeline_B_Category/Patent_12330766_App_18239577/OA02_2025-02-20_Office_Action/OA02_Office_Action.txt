Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-20 allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: Prior art US 7578477 B2 French; Clive et al. teaches an internal and external panel with resilient connection, but fails to teach where panels are pressurized and “wherein the resilient connection is configured to isolate the external panel from a movement of the internal panel relative to the external panel”. French also fails to teaches where the resilient member is a leaf spring.
It would have not been obvious to one of ordinary skill in the art to add the resilient connection or the leaf spring into the pressurized internal and external aircraft door panels without the benefit of the present disclosure.
