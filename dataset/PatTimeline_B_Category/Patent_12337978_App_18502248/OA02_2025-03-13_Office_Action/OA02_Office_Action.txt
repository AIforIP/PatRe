Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The primary reason for allowance of the claims are the limitations which detail the connection means to a wing spar using a primary structure of a blade extending rearward from the structure, having a number of bores on the top and sides. The bores used to couple double shackles from the blade to the receiving suction side panel. Further comprising two fittings with clevis engagement portions on the lower portion of the mounting. Criticality for this arrangement is given as providing for fastening to the wing that ensure an improved transfer of forces towards the structure of the wing while at the same time allowing the engine to be installed in close vertical proximity to the wing
