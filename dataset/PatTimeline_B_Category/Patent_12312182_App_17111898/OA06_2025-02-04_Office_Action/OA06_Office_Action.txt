Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-6, 8-16, and 19-30 are allowed.
The following is a statement of reasons for the indication of allowable subject matter: The prior art does not teach a detachable self-contained robotic system having the claimed combination features as defined by the claims.  For example, Brazeau et al. (USP 9,656,806) teaches (Fig. 5) a detachable, self-contained robotic arm system comprising: a mounting subsystem (505) configured to releasably engage an operating platform (top surface of 120), wherein the operating platform is an autonomous mobile base (120).  However, Brazeau et al. does not teach a slew ring, or a first machine vision subsystem mounted on a mast assembly.  The prior art to Seavey et al. (USP 8,322,249) teaches a slew ring (204) supporting a robotic arm system (100) for pivoting the robotic arm, a vision subsystem (camera 118) mounted on a mast assembly, the mast assembly being directly mounted to a gear box (120).  The claims distinguish over the prior art because Seavey et al. does not teach the mast being coupled directly to the slew ring, but teaches the mast assembly being indirectly coupled to the slew ring via the gearbox.  In view of the foregoing, the claims are allowable over the prior art
