ALLOWABILITY NOTICE
Response to Amendment
Applicant's Amendments filed 1/2/25 have been entered.  Claims 1-3 and 5-15 are pending in the application.  Claims 5-6 and 10-15 are withdrawn.  Claims 1 and 7 are amended.
Response to Arguments
Applicant’s arguments, see pages 7-8, filed 1/2/25, with respect to the 112 and 102 rejections have been fully considered and are persuasive.  Applicant argues Whitman fails to teach “the third connection is positioned between the leading edge and the first connection”.  Examiner generally agrees.  Regarding the combination of Whitman with other prior arts of record, it would not be obvious to one having ordinary skill in the art to reposition the fuse element 90 to be downstream of 66 since 90 is located, as taught by Whitman at Col 4, ln. 56 to Col 5 ln. 19, to provide a weak point at a specific location to better accommodate bird/foreign object strikes.  In the Examiner’s opinion, relocation of 90 would render the invention of Whitman inoperable for its intended use.  As such, the rejections have been withdrawn, and the claims are in condition for allowance.
Regarding Claims 5-6, they depend (see the Examiner’s Amendment below) from an allowable claim thereby making claims 5-6 eligible for rejoinder and in condition for allowance.
Furthermore, Claims 10-15 do not require all the limitations of an allowable claim, therefore, claim 10 and its dependents will not be rejoined.

Election/Restrictions
Claims 1-3 and 7-9 are allowable. The restriction requirement between Species 1-6 and Inventions I and II, as set forth in the Office action mailed on 8/14/24, has been reconsidered in view of the allowability of claims to the elected invention pursuant to MPEP § 821.04(a). The restriction requirement is hereby withdrawn as to any claim that requires all the limitations of an allowable claim. Specifically, the restriction requirement of 8/14/24 is partially withdrawn.  Claims 5-6, directed to a nonelected species are no longer withdrawn from consideration because the claim(s) requires all the limitations of an allowable claim. However, claims 10-15, directed to a method of assembling an air inlet of a nacelle of an aircraft are withdrawn from consideration because they do not require all the limitations of an allowable claim.
Accordingly, Claims 10-15 are cancelled and Claims 5-6 are hereby rejoined and fully examined for patentability under 37 CFR 1.104.
In view of the withdrawal of the restriction requirement, applicant(s) are advised that if any claim presented in a continuation or divisional application is anticipated by, or includes all the limitations of, a claim that is allowable in the present application, such claim may be subject to provisional statutory and/or nonstatutory double patenting rejections over the claims of the instant application.
Once the restriction requirement is withdrawn, the provisions of 35 U.S.C. 121 are no longer applicable. See In re Ziegler, 443 F.2d 1211, 1215, 170 USPQ 129, 131-32 (CCPA 1971). See also MPEP § 804.01.
