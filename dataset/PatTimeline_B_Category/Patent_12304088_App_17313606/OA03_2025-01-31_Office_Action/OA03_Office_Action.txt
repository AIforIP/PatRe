Examiner’s interpretation
The deformable membrane is of more specific type/ particular machine and not a generic membrane which is used for defining of an enclosure that is configured to be filled with a medium, and comprises a filter layer on a surface of the deformable membrane configured to assist in detection of a deformation of the deformable membrane (see fig. 1 reproduced below). The adjusting of the pressure based on the similarity value presents the eligibility under 35 USC 101 as a practical application since it overrides any errors which may cause undesirable results, such as incorrectly detecting a type of object, inability to detect a type of object, incorrectly detecting a pose of an object, inability to detect a pose of an object, incorrectly detecting a geometry of an object, inability to detect a geometry of an object, and the like according to paragraph [0049] of the specification.
