Response to Arguments
Applicant’s arguments filed 03/19/2025 have been fully considered. Applicant’s recent amendments to independent claims 1 & 11 have required further searching and consideration by the Examiner. Upon further searching and consideration, the Examiner submits that claims 1-20 are in condition for allowance and the Examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance is provided below.
Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-20 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: prior arts of record do not teach or fairly suggest each element and limitation of independent claims 1 and 11.
A list of the closest prior arts of record and a brief description of what they disclose is provided below.
Brahma et al. (US 2021/0383209 A1) discloses a method for collecting and processing data from vehicles. The method of Brahma describes generating a reference path via a neural network model and determining whether the path along which an autonomous vehicle is driving deviates from the reference path by a threshold. If the path deviates from the reference path by a threshold, a data collection module may deem it necessary to store the sensor data collected by the vehicle.
Yamada (JP2005332041A) discloses a road shape collection device that is mounted on a vehicle and collects travel path data of the vehicle in order to create a road map.
Shapira et al. (US 2022/0082403 A1) discloses systems and methods for mapping lanes for use in vehicle navigation. Shapira describes storing one or more target trajectories representing intended paths of travel for vehicles in one or more lanes associated with the multi-lane road.
Claim 11 recites substantially similar technical features as claim 1 and is therefore considered to be in condition for allowance for the same reasons provided above. Claims 2-10 and 12-20 depend from claims 1 & 11 and, therefore, are also considered to be in condition for allowance at least based on their dependency from an allowable independent claim.
