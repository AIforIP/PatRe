Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
Claims 1-10 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
The limitation “a second stage reduction assembly comprising: an input coupled to an output of the first stage reduction assembly and adapted” (claims 1 and 10) is unclear as to which of the “second stage reduction assembly”, “input”, “output” or “first stage reduction assembly” is to be “adapted”.
The limitation “a second axis offset from the first axis” (claims 1 and 10) is disclosed as being in combination with “bevel gears” (written description and claim 7) such that the meaning of the term “offset” is unclear.  It would appear that bevel gears would necessarily have axis that are intersecting with one another.  For purposes of application of the prior art elsewhere below the term “offset” is taken to broadly include not only offset spacing without intersection (i.e., parallel and spaced by a constant offset) but also offset angling with intersection (intersecting at an offset angle therebetween).
The limitation “an output” (claims 1 and 10) are recited twice such that it is unclear if the second recitation is to refer to the same element as the first or to an additional element.
The limitation “output coaxially arranged on a periphery of the hollow shaft and adapted to engage with the input” is contrary to the embodiment shown in the figures which show the input (1041) and output (1042) engaged with a belt (1043) therebetween rather than with each other.  For purposes of applying the prior art elsewhere below the term “engage” is taken not to require direct contact.
The limitation “the output component” (claim 9) lacks proper antecedent basis.  For purposes of applying the prior art elsewhere below the examiner takes the limitation to mean   –the output--.
Claim 10 is worded similarly to claim 1 and claims 2-9 depend from claim 1 such that they are similarly rejected.
