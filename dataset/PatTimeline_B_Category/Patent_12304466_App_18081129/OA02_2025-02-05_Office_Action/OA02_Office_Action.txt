Reasons for Allowance
1.	The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Claim 1 is distinguishable over the prior art insofar as the prior art of record does not disclose or reasonably suggest, in combination with the other claimed limitations, generating a plurality of virtual lines in a moving direction of the vehicle within an entire width of the vehicle, obtaining a plurality of contour points of the object by processing the lidar data, and determining a collision risk for the avoidance control or the warning control based on a number of crossing points between the plurality of virtual lines and the plurality of contour points.  Claim 11 recites similar subject matter.  Dependent claims 2-10 and 12-20 are distinguishable for at least the same reasons.
