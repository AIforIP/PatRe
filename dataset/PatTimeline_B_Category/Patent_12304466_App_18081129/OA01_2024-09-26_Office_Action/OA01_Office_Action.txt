The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph:

Claims 11-20 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor (or for applications subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 112, the applicant), regards as the invention.
A.	As per claim 11, the claim preamble sets forth a method of controlling a vehicle; however, the body of the claim fails to positively recite a controlling step.  The examiner suggests amending the claim to include the step of “performing avoidance control or warning control based on the determined collision risk” (see for example Fig. 3:309).
B.	All claims dependent from a rejected claim are also rejected for the same reasons.
6.	Claim 1 is distinguishable over the prior art insofar as the prior art of record does not disclose or reasonably suggest, in combination with the other claimed limitations, generating a plurality of virtual lines in a moving direction of the vehicle within an entire width of the vehicle, obtaining a plurality of contour points of the object by processing the lidar data, and determining a collision risk for the avoidance control or the warning control based on a number of crossing points between the plurality of virtual lines and the plurality of contour points.  Claim 11 recites similar subject matter.  Dependent claims 2-10 and 12-20 are distinguishable for at least the same reasons.
7.	The prior art made of record and not relied upon is considered pertinent to applicant's disclosure.  In particular,
• Chen et al. (US 2022/0204029)- collision avoidance system which uses contour of a detection object to determine distance to object; lidar sensor obtains information regarding external environment of the vehicle; determines likelihood of a collision; may provide collision avoidance and/or warning.
• DE102018105665- collision avoidance system which calculates TTC with an object; determines obstacle point with minimum collision time period.
None of the cited references, either alone or in combination with the other prior art of record, disclose or reasonably suggest the distinguishing features noted above.
8.	Claims 1-10 are allowed.  Claims 11-20 would be allowable if rewritten or amended to overcome the rejection(s) under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), 2nd paragraph, set forth in this Office action.
