Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Applicant’s arguments and amendments dated 01/29/2025 provide the basis for reasons for allowability (see remarks, pages 10-13).
It is hereby asserted by the Examiner that, in light of the amendments and remarks and in further deliberation over all of the evidence at hand, that the claims are allowable as the evidence at hand does not anticipate the claims and does not render obvious any further modification of the references to a person of ordinary skill in the art. Thus, the prior art references do not disclose the recited claim limitations when considered as a whole, and the rejections in previous office action has been withdrawn.
As such, the combination of the claimed limitations is novel and found to be allowable over the prior art. The cited references taken singly or in combination do not anticipate or make obvious the Applicant's claimed invention. A hypothetical prior art rejection would require impermissible hindsight reasoning.
