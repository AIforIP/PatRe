Specification
The disclosure is objected to because of the following informalities:
[0041]: as mentioned above, 202 as a label is missing from Fig.2, where the object node is labeled 114.
[0049]:  “…consuming one or more distance and/or velocity bounds.”  Is this really the word wanted here?
[0061]: The situation shown in Fig. 7a does NOT show what is described here.  Note that the dotted line supposedly indicating the second step (determining the target velocity corresponding to the total distance) is not at a distance of 200 meters but something obviously short of it. Nor is “the target velocity is 15 m/s” what is pictured here.
[0066]: “position bound(trajectory end time) can be the position or distance of the maximum distance bound at the end of the candidate trajectory…” should probably be restated as “position bound(trajectory end time) can be the position or distance of the maximum distance bound at the end time of the candidate trajectory;…”
[0066]: “trajectory end position(trajectory end time) can be the position or distance of the vehicle relative to the initial position of the vehicle at the end of the candidate trajectory;…” is confusing because “initial position of the vehicle at the end of the candidate trajectory” makes no sense.  If this phrase has been interpreted correctly, it would be better stated as: “trajectory end position(trajectory end time) can be the position or distance of the vehicle at the end of the candidate trajectory, relative to the initial position of the vehicle;…”
[0066]: “the comfort deceleration parameter can be a or a defined value”; it looks like there is a word missing.
[0068]: “Using the slope of the maximum distance bound and transforming the slope into an available distance…” would seem to have a different interpretation of “available distance” than what is found in earlier paragraphs. Isn’t “available distance” supposed to be the distance necessary for a comfortably decelerating vehicle, starting at the final velocity of the trajectory, to reach zero? (as in [0059].)
[0072]: the second instance of “At step 812,…” should be “At step 814,..”
[0073]: The two mentions of “candidate trajectory” in line 2 should probably be “candidate trajectories”.
[0073]: “…at step 814,…” should be “…at step 816,…”
[0073]: “At step 816, the data processing system…”  should be “At step 818, the data processing system…”
Appropriate correction is required.
Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-20 allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: No applicable prior art was found. The closest art is US 2021/0089036 (Banzhaf) and US 2021/0034072 (Max. et al., hence Max). Banzhaf teaches a method and a device for calculating a trajectory of a vehicle through calculating a number of possible trajectories, but links the beginning and end of each of the trajectories to a fixed starting position and a fixed ending position, rather than the multiple ends of possible trajectories as is done by applicant.  Max teaches how to produce swarm trajectories for a specified lane of a roadway section, where each of the trajectories used in producing the swarm trajectory has different boundaries and constraints, which are taken into account when determining the swarm trajectory.
In particular, the following was not found in either of the above:  the consolidation of maximum distance/velocity bounds in one general maximum distance/velocity bound, then used to compare with a set of trajectories such that the final distance and velocity of a picked trajectory remained within bounds, with the further constraint that the vehicle is able to comfortably come to a stop from the final velocity.
