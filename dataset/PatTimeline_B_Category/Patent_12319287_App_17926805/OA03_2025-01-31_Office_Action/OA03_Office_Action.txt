Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1, 3 and 9 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:  prior arts of record do not teach or fairly suggest each element and limitation of independent claims 1 and 9.
Claim 9.	A travel control apparatus to be mounted on a vehicle comprising a map locator, the travel control apparatus comprising
a computer device configured to perform control to cause the vehicle to travel at a set constant vehicle speed or to cause the vehicle to travel following a preceding vehicle, 	wherein, the computer device comprises a program stored in a storage medium, the program being executable to cause the computer device to:
determine, before turn steering is started, whether it is a turn opportunity for the vehicle to make a first direction turn, the first direction turn being a turn made by the vehicle deviating from a current travel course without crossing an oncoming travel course,
assess accuracy of the map locator, and
upon assessing the accuracy to be unfavorable, determine that it is the turn opportunity in a case where a turn signal for the first direction turn is detected to be ON and a vehicle speed of the vehicle is less than a predetermined threshold.
The closest prior arts of record and a brief description of what they disclose is provided below.
a.	Kazuyuki (JP2009126433 (A)) discloses a vehicle control device equipped with an automatic vehicle speed control means for automatically controlling a vehicle speed. The system of Kazuyuki comprises detecting an intersection ahead of the vehicle (based on information from the navigation system), determining, based on information from the navigation system and the state of the turn indicators, whether the vehicle is located within a predetermined intersection control implementation range (which corresponds to the range in which speed control is required to pass through the intersection at an appropriate speed), and calculates a target acceleration for turning at the intersection approaching ahead of the vehicle at an appropriate speed.
b.	Yoshitomi (US 2017/0154527 A1) discloses an apparatus and method for driving assistance. The driving assistance system comprises a variety of sensors, a control device, and an assistance execution unit (including a central processing unit (CPU)) mounted on a vehicle. The CPU may determine that the driver has activated a turn signal of the vehicle (indicating that the driver intends to make a right or left turn) and the CPU then proceeds to determine if the vehicle speed is lower than a pre-set threshold velocity. When the vehicle velocity is lower than the threshold velocity, it is judged that the driver intends to make a left or right turn.
c.	Badshah et al. (“Vehicle navigation in GPS denied environment for smart cities using vision sensors”) discloses a system for vehicle navigation based on vision sensors using modified normalized phase correlation. The disclosure of Badshah is concerned with using vision based navigation in order to determine an estimated position of the vehicle while driving in urban environments (such as in crowded and congested areas) and estimating the position accuracy in comparison with GPS calculated data.
Claim 1 has substantially similar technical features as claim 9 and is therefore considered to be in condition for allowance under the same rationale used for claim 9. Claim 3 depends from claim 1 and, therefore, is also considered to be in condition for allowance under the same rationale used for claim 9.
