Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1, 3, and 9 allowed.
The following is a statement of reasons for the indication of allowable subject matter:   prior arts of record do not teach or fairly suggest each element and limitation of independent claims 1 and 9.
Claim 9.	A travel control apparatus to be mounted on a vehicle comprising a map locator, the travel control apparatus comprising
a computer device configured to perform control to cause the vehicle to travel at a set constant vehicle speed or to cause the vehicle to travel following a preceding vehicle, 	wherein, the computer device comprises a program stored in a storage medium, the program being executable to cause the computer device to:
determine, before turn steering is started, whether it is a turn opportunity for the vehicle to make a first direction turn, the first direction turn being a turn made by the vehicle deviating from a current travel course without crossing an oncoming travel course,
assess accuracy of the map locator, and
upon assessing the accuracy to be unfavorable, determine that it is the turn opportunity in a case where a turn signal for the first direction turn is detected to be ON and a vehicle speed of the vehicle is less than a predetermined threshold.
Rather, the closest prior arts of record and a brief description of what they disclose is provided below.
Kazuyuki (JP2009126433 (A)) discloses a vehicle control device equipped with an automatic vehicle speed control means for automatically controlling a vehicle speed. The system of Kazuyuki comprises detecting an intersection ahead of the vehicle (based on information from the navigation system), determining, based on information from the navigation system and the state of the turn indicators, whether the vehicle is located within a predetermined intersection control implementation range (which corresponds to the range in which speed control is required to pass through the intersection at an appropriate speed), and calculates a target acceleration for turning at the intersection approaching ahead of the vehicle at an appropriate speed.
Yoshitomi (US 2017/0154527 A1) discloses an apparatus and method for driving assistance. The driving assistance system comprises a variety of sensors, a control device, and an assistance execution unit (including a central processing unit (CPU)) mounted on a vehicle. The CPU may determine that the driver has activated a turn signal of the vehicle (indicating that the driver intends to make a right or left turn) and the CPU then proceeds to determine if the vehicle speed is lower than a pre-set threshold velocity. When the vehicle velocity is lower than the threshold velocity, it is judged that the driver intends to make a left or right turn.
Badshah et al. (“Vehicle navigation in GPS denied environment for smart cities using vision sensors”) discloses a system for vehicle navigation based on vision sensors using modified normalized phase correlation. The disclosure of Badshah is concerned with using vision based navigation in order to determine an estimated position of the vehicle while driving in urban environments (such as in crowded and congested areas) and estimating the position accuracy in comparison with GPS calculated data.
Claim 1 has substantially similar technical features as claim 9 and is therefore considered to be in condition for allowance under the same rationale used for claim 9. Claim 3 depends from claim 1 and, therefore, is also considered to be in condition for allowance under the same rationale used for claim 9.
Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 103
In the event the determination of the status of the application as subject to AIA  35 U.S.C. 102 and 103 (or as subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 102 and 103) is incorrect, any correction of the statutory basis (i.e., changing from AIA  to pre-AIA ) for the rejection will not be considered a new ground of rejection if the prior art relied upon, and the rationale supporting the rejection, would be the same under either status.
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 103 which forms the basis for all obviousness rejections set forth in this Office action:
A patent for a claimed invention may not be obtained, notwithstanding that the claimed invention is not identically disclosed as set forth in section 102, if the differences between the claimed invention and the prior art are such that the claimed invention as a whole would have been obvious before the effective filing date of the claimed invention to a person having ordinary skill in the art to which the claimed invention pertains. Patentability shall not be negated by the manner in which the invention was made.
Claim(s) 10 is/are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 103 as being unpatentable over Kazuyuki (JP2009126433 (A)) in view of Kameoka (US 2022/0340123 A1).
Regarding claim 10, Kazuyuki teaches
A travel control apparatus to be mounted on a vehicle comprising a map locator see at least [0001]-[0005] and [0014], the travel control apparatus comprising
a computer device configured to perform control to cause the vehicle to travel at a set constant vehicle speed or to cause the vehicle to travel following a preceding vehicle, see at least [0012]-[0014] where the vehicle control device 1 comprises an electronic control unit (ECU) 10. The automatic vehicle speed control is configured by (normal) following control in which the vehicle follows the preceding vehicle at a specified distance and time but may also be configured by constant speed control in which the vehicle is configured to maintain a specified vehicle speed [0039].	wherein, the computer device comprises a program stored in a storage medium see at least [0014], the program being executable to cause the computer device to:
determine, before turn steering is started, whether it is a turn opportunity for the vehicle to make a first direction turn, the first direction turn being a turn made by the vehicle deviating from a current travel course without crossing an oncoming travel course see at least [0017] where a navigation system 16 is connected to the control ECU 10. The navigation system includes a map having map data comprising, for example, coordinate information of intersections and lane information (including information such as lanes for right and left turns and lanes where right and left turns are possible). Further, see at least [0026]-[0027] where, in step 72, an intersection ahead of the vehicle is detected. When the intersection is detected and the turn signal is turned on, it may be determined that the vehicle has entered a predetermined intersection control implementation range. As shown in FIG. 3, the upcoming turn is a left turn in which the vehicle does not cross an oncoming travel lane,
in a case where it is determined that it is the turn opportunity, perform control to reduce acceleration to a target acceleration rate which is calculated by a first calculation method on a basis of at least a vehicle speed of the vehicle, before the turn steering is started See at least [0008] & [0030] where intersection following control may be executed in a manner as shown in FIG. 5. A target acceleration for turning at an intersection approaching ahead of the vehicle at an appropriate speed is calculated. In step 214, the traveling speed is controlled via the brake device 40, the engine 42, and the transmission 44 so as to realize the set target acceleration. In other words, the automatic vehicle speed control means executes intersection deceleration control which automatically controls the vehicle speed to a low speed suitable for turning right or left at an intersection.
Kazuyuki teaches all of the elements of the current invention as stated above except in a case where it is determined that it is not the turn opportunity, perform control to reduce a target acceleration rate which is calculated by a second calculation method on a basis of a steering angle and the vehicle speed acceleration, after the turn steering is started.
However, Kameoka teaches that it is known to provide the travel control apparatus wherein in a case where it is determined that it is not the turn opportunity, perform control to reduce a target acceleration rate which is calculated by a second calculation method on a basis of a steering angle and the vehicle speed acceleration, after the turn steering is started.
See at least Kameoka FIG. 4; FIG. 6; and [0063]-[0066] and [0108]; [0125].	As shown in FIG. 4, the vehicle is travelling on a curved road (e.g., a situation in which it is not a turn opportunity) in which turn steering has already started. While the vehicle is travelling along a curved road, this is being interpreted by the examiner as a case where it is not determined that it is the turn opportunity since the vehicle is not anticipating a turn, rather, the vehicle is simply following the contours of the road.
When the system determines a deviation between a target trajectory and an actual trajectory of the vehicle 100 (such as the case in FIG. 4 where the vehicle is travelling on a curve and experiences an icy patch of the road surface), the system calculates a control input for controlling the steering and speed of the vehicle to realize a target steering angle and acceleration/deceleration rate.
It would have been obvious before the effective filing date of the claimed invention to a person having ordinary skill in the art to have modified Kazuyuki to incorporate the teachings of Kameoka and provide the travel control apparatus wherein the target acceleration setting unit calculates the target acceleration rate by a second calculation method on a basis of a steering angle (and the vehicle speed) in a case where it is not determined that it is the turn opportunity. In doing so, this provides an improvement in the overall safety of the system. for example, in situations where an icy road surface is present on a curved road along the target path of the vehicle, the vehicle may deviate from the target path due to a deterioration of the vehicle’s control performance or the vehicle may experience destabilization. In order to avoid this, the system is configured to calculate an updated target acceleration rate based on the detected deviation.
