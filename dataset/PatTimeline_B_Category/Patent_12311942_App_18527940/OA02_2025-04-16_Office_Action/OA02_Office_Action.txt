Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-20 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The closest prior art reference from Sim (US 2019/0113926) teaches a system which checks for the level of congestion in the environment around the vehicle and also detects whether an emergency vehicle is behind it.  If so, a rescue lane is created. With regard to independent Claims 1, Sim, taken either independently or in combination with other prior art references of record, fails to teach or render obvious where the system makes a distinction between the case where an emergency lane already exists in the lanes surrounding the vehicle or where one does not exist when determining how to implement a rescue lane.
