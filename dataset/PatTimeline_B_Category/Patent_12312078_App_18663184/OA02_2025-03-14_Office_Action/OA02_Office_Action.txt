Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: The prior art of record fails to show alone or in combination the features of an aircraft as claimed comprising two wing segments as claimed, a fuselage as claimed, three or more spokes coupling the fuselage and the wing segments, three or more motors disposed within or attached to the spokes, a propeller connected to the motors as claimed, wherein the nose section of the fuselage is above the wing segments in vertical takeoff and landing and forward of the wing segments in forward flight, or of an aircraft as claimed comprising two wing segments as claimed, a fuselage as claimed, a plurality of spokes coupling the fuselage and the wing segments, one or more motors disposed within or attached to the spokes, a propeller connected to the motors as claimed and proximate the leading edge of each of the spokes, wherein the nose section of the fuselage is above the wing segments in vertical takeoff and landing and forward of the wing segments in forward flight or of an aircraft as claimed comprising two wing segments as claimed, a fuselage as claimed, six spokes coupling the fuselage and the wing segments, one or more motors disposed within or attached to the spokes, a propeller connected to the motors as claimed and proximate the leading edge of each of the six spokes, wherein the nose section of the fuselage is above the wing segments in vertical takeoff and landing and forward of the wing segments in forward flight.
