Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: The prior art of record fails to show alone or in combination, the following, in summary, as claimed:
An air vehicle having a body, wings, a control surface on the wings, a main actuator moving the control surface, a shaft enabling the control surface, an additional actuator enabling the angle of attack, and a support element located on the wing and enabling the wing to be movably connected onto the body, an opening extending thoroughly within the support element and enabling the shaft to trigger the control surface, and it is this configuration of this support element that is the novelty of this invention, whereby the shaft that extends through the opening that extends thoroughly within the support element creates a coaxial/concentricity of the support element and the shaft, and this feature along with all the other elements and features of all the amendments and other claims overcome the prior art of record.
