REASONS FOR ALLOWANCE
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
None of the prior art of record teaches:
change a mode of the aircraft from a full authority mode to a reduced authority mode in response to determining the detected face within the image is unauthorized; wherein the unauthorized user has full authority to control one or more user interface elements of the aircraft in the full authority mode; wherein the unauthorized user has reduced authority to control the one or more user interface elements of the aircraft in the reduced authority mode.
There are many pieces of prior art that discuss flight deck security generally. Two of these are:
EP2050671 which discusses changing aircraft authorizations based on modes
WO03045782 which discusses preventing the control of an aircraft from the cockpit including disabling all normal cockpit control.
The difference between these types of control override systems and the present claims is that the claims do this in response to facial recognition technology. While it would be possible to find another reference and combine it with the two pieces of art above, this would be improper because there would be no motivation to combine.
To support this, please see the Cockpit Door NPL included on the PTO-892 form which states that in the future the cockpit could be secured by facial recognition technology. This reference is dated 2022, after the filing date of the present invention. There was nothing at the time that disclosed the specific way facial recognition technology could be used in relation to reducing or limiting operation of the airplane. Finding a generic facial recognition algorithm and applying it to the references listed above would be improper hindsight reasoning.
The present claims are completely technical in nature and thus are not subject to a 35 U.S.C. 101 rejection. Changing a mode of the aircraft is not an abstract idea.
