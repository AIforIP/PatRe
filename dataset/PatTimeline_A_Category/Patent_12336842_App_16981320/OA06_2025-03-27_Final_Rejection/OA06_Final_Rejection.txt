Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: The amendments filed on February 27, 2025 do not impact the allowability of the claims and the claims are allowable for the reasons provided in the Non-Final Office Action mailed on February 11, 2025.  Claims 10 and 12 are now independent claims, which include all of the limitations of claim 1 including “the temporal segments each have a periodic duration (TPeriodic) matching the frequency of the electric power grid” in combination with the recitation of “the temporal segments of the noise signal (SNoise) are detected during a time interval (TNoise) not overlapping with a stimuli signal interval (TStimulus) of the single stimuli signal (Sstimuli) and not overlapping with the duration of the response signal (TResponse)”, which were not found to be taught in any other reference found during a further prior art search.
