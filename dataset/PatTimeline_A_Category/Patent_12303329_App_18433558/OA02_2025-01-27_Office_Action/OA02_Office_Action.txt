The previous Double Patenting and 35 U.S.C 103 rejections of claims 1-11 are withdrawn in light of Applicant’s amendments and remarks filed on December 20, 2024. Accordingly claims 1-11 are found allowable as provided by Examiner’s reasoning’s below.
Reasons for Allowance
Claims 1-11 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: The prior art of record fails to teach or render obvious the limitations of independent claim 1, when the claim is taken as a whole.
Claim 1 recites in summary an apparatus. The apparatus consists of: an intravascular catheter or guidewire designed to obtain intravascular data from a blood vessel of a patient while positioned within the blood vessel. The intravascular catheter or guidewire consists of a first segment, second segment and a coating. The first and second segments are physically distinct components and coupled to one another to form a length of the intravascular catheter or guidewire. The coating is separate from and disposed outside of the first segment and the second segment and further extends over a location where the physically distinct components are coupled to one another. The location is where a proximal portion of the second segment is coupled to a distal portion of the first segment. The coating forms a seal preventing fluid ingress and directly contacts an exterior surface of the first segment and an exterior surface of the second segment. The coating consists of a proximal boundary at the first segment, a distal boundary at the second segment and a continuous length between the proximal and distal boundaries. The continuous length overlaps the location where the proximal portion of the second segment is coupled to the distal portion of the first segment.
Applicant’s arguments are persuasive regarding the combination of exemplary prior art of record  Stigall, Dausch and Wada as to not providing teachings/suggestions nor an apparent rational or desirability to modify individually or in combination to render obvious the disclosed amended claims when considered as a whole. Specifically, the amended claim limitations regarding the coating position and connections with respect to the first and second segments of the catheter or guidewire to seal the coating preventing fluid ingress as provided.
Dependent claims 2-11 are allowed at least by virtue of their dependency upon an allowable base claim.
