Allowable Subject Matter
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Regarding the previously applied claim objections, the claim amendments filed 10 February 2025 and the Examiner’s Amendments authorized 3 April 2025 amended subject matter to overcome the previously applied claim objections.
Regarding the previously applied rejections under § 112(b), the claim amendments filed 10 February 2025 and the Examiner’s Amendments authorized 3 April 2025 amended subject matter to overcome the previously applied rejections under § 112(b).
Regarding the previously applied art under § 103, claim 1 was amended in the claims filed 10 February 2025 to incorporate subject matter not considered to be taught by prior art, and claim 10 was amended in the Examiner’s Amendments authorized 3 April 2025 to incorporate similar subject matter, such that the claims are considered to overcome the previously applied rejections under § 103. The Examiner notes that the closest prior art of record with respect to claim 1 is Clarke (US-4447235-A, previously presented) in view of Ellingson (US- 6217556-A, previously presented) and Langevin (US-6156019-A, previously presented), which fails to teach, disclose, or suggest, alone or in combination, the amended limitations of claim 1 of “a cannula insert disposed within the inlet fluid passage comprising a lengthwise inlet fluid pathway”, “a blood sample cone disposed within the blood sample fluid passage comprising a lengthwise blood sample fluid pathway”, and “a waste cone disposed within the waste fluid passage comprising a lengthwise waste fluid pathway”. It would not have been obvious to one of ordinary skill in the art before the effective filing date of the claimed invention to have modified Clarke, Ellingson, or Langevin, alone or in combination, to employ or incorporate the noted limitations of claim 1 without the benefit of hindsight. The Examiner additionally notes that with respect to claim 10, the closest prior art of record is Ellingson in view of Jho (US-20180207398-A1, previously presented) and Altman (US-6416510-B1, previously presented), which fails to teach, disclose, or suggest, alone or in combination, the amended limitations of claim 10 of “a cannula insert disposed within the inlet fluid passage comprising a lengthwise inlet fluid pathway”, “a blood sample cone disposed within the blood sample fluid passage comprising a lengthwise blood sample fluid pathway”, and “a waste cone disposed within the waste fluid passage comprising a lengthwise waste fluid pathway”. It would not have been obvious to one of ordinary skill in the art before the effective filing date of the claimed invention to have modified Ellingson, Jho, or Altman, alone or in combination, to employ or incorporate the noted limitations of claim 10 without the benefit of hindsight.
