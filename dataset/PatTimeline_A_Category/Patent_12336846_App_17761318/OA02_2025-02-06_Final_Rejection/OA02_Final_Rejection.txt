Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Claim 24 is the broadest independent claim. Claim 24 recites an apparatus for extracorporeal blood treatment. The closest prior art of record is Olde. Olde discloses the invention substantially as claimed as outlined in the previous non-final rejection mailed on 9/11/2024. However, Olde fails to discloses a source for directing an optical signal or multiple detectors positioned at different radial directions or angular degrees as claimed. Thus Olde fails to fully disclose the heartbeat sensor comprises: “at least one source for directing an optical signal towards the blood flowing in the extracorporeal segment, the optical signal being directed along an emission axis” or “detectors placed at different radial directions, or arranged at different angular degrees, with respect to the emission axis of the source to collect a reflected signal, a scattered signal and a transmitted signal depending on their respective position, and wherein each detector is configured to receive an optical informative signal comprising the signal emitted by said source after passing at least partially the blood flowing in the extracorporeal segment, wherein each detector is configured to emit a respective output signal related to the received optical informative signal”. Furthermore, nothing in the prior art when viewed with Olde obviates these deficiencies. It is important to note that no limitation by itself is renders the claim allowable by itself. Rather, it is the combination of all the claim limitations of claim 24 together that define the invention over the prior art. Therefore, the combination of claimed limitations recited by claim 24 is neither anticipated, nor obviated in view of the prior art.
