Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-21 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Claims 1, 8 and 15 recite a specific system and method for detecting and preventing fraudulent activities in digital advertising.  Nguyen et al. (US patent 8,360,838); Rogas (US PG pub 2018/0108015); and Wang (US patent 10,319,182) show the closest prior art references.  Nguyen et al. teaches a detecting and preventing fraudulent activities system.  Rogas teaches using many different sensors in a mobile device to detect fraud.  Wang teaches a nexus between detecting fraud in a game to detecting fraud in digital advertising.
The full combination of limitations is not shown or taught, especially, to “analyse at least one of: a number of taps indicated in the user-interaction data, touch pressures during the taps, sizes of touch areas on the touchscreen during the taps, locations of the touch areas on the touchscreen during the taps, time stamps of the taps, to detect whether a user of the user device is a bot”
Claims 2-7, 9-14 and 16-21 are dependent upon claims 1, 8 and 15.
