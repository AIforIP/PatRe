REASONS FOR ALLOWANCE
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The scope of a fetal health monitoring device configured to use the first acoustic sensor while operating in a first mode, and upon detecting reduced fetal movement activity, transitioning to a second mode was not found in the prior art alone or in combination with one another to be obvious over the prior art of record. The closest prior art of record is Queyam (NPL “An IoT Based Multi-Parameter Data Acquisition System for Efficient Bio-Telemonitoring of Pregnant Women at Home” published in 2018); however it fails to recite these features.
