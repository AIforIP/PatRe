Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: with respect to claims 1, 11, and the corresponding dependent claims, the prior art of record (Sipe) does not teach of an apparatus and method for generating cardiac catheterization data with a processor to process the cardiogram training data examples and “sanitize the plurality of cardiogram training data examples, wherein sanitizing the plurality of cardiogram training data examples comprises: identifying one or more cardiogram training data examples of the plurality of cardiogram training data examples which are outliers; and removing the one or more cardiogram training data examples from the plurality of cardiogram training data examples; train a catheter data predictor using the sanitized plurality of cardiogram training data examples; input a plurality of cardiogram data signals; generate a plurality of catheterization parameters from the cardiogram data signals and the catheter data predictor; and display the plurality of catheterization parameters”.
Other related prior art:
Villongco et al. (2019/0328457)
Directed to generating a cardiogram and generating training data for training the classifier [0067, 0068].  Reference is therefore directed to method and system generate patient classifier based on patient training data and generate patient specific model classifier based on model training data [0130] and receiving a cardiogram of a patient and apply the patient classifier to the cardiogram [0132].  But the reference does not teach of processing the cardiogram training data examples and “sanitize the plurality of cardiogram training data examples, wherein sanitizing the plurality of cardiogram training data examples comprises: identifying one or more cardiogram training data examples of the plurality of cardiogram training data examples which are outliers; and removing the one or more cardiogram training data examples from the plurality of cardiogram training data examples; train a catheter data predictor using the sanitized plurality of cardiogram training data example”.
Neumann (2024/0414135)
Directed to method and system of telemedicine including processor configured to sanitize training data by removing duplicative or other redundant data and identifying outliers [0041] with respect to various medical conditions including cardiac conditions [0104]. But the reference does not teach of generating cardiac catheterization data with a processor for processing the cardiogram training data examples and “sanitize the plurality of cardiogram training data examples, wherein sanitizing the plurality of cardiogram training data examples comprises: identifying one or more cardiogram training data examples of the plurality of cardiogram training data examples which are outliers; and removing the one or more cardiogram training data examples from the plurality of cardiogram training data examples; train a catheter data predictor using the sanitized plurality of cardiogram training data example”.
