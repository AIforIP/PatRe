This is a decision on the petition under 37 CFR 1.137(a), filed March 31, 2025, to revive the above-identified application.
The petition is GRANTED.
The application became abandoned for failure s to timely pay the issue fee on or before March 11, 2025, as required by the Notice of Allowance and Fee(s) Due, mailed December 11, 2024, which set a statutory period for reply of three (3) months.  Accordingly, the application became abandoned on March 12, 2025. The Office mailed a Notice of Abandonment on March 28, 2025.
The petition satisfies the requirements of 37 CFR 1.137(a) in that petitioner has supplied; (1) the required reply in of the Part B – Fee(s) Transmittal form for the issue fee payment of $1200.00, (2) the petition fee in the amount of $2260.00, and (3) a proper statement of unintentional delay.
The file does not indicate a change of correspondence address has been submitted, and the address found on the instant petition differs from the correspondence address of record. If appropriate, a change of correspondence address should be filed in accordance with MPEP § 601.03(a). A courtesy copy of this decision is being mailed to the address found on the petition; however, the Office will mail correspondence not related to this petition solely to the correspondence address of record.
The application is being forwarded to the Office of Data Management for processing into a patent.
