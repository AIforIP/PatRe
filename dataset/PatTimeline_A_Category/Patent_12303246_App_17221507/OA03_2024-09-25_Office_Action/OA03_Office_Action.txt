Claim 19 is rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor (or for applications subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 112, the applicant), regards as the invention.

Response to Arguments
Applicant’s arguments, see pages 5 and 6, filed 7/25/2024, with respect to the rejection(s) of the independent claim have been fully considered and are persuasive.  Therefore, the rejection has been withdrawn.  However, upon further consideration, a new ground(s) of rejection is made in view of the newly cited non-patent literature.
Applicant has amended the independent claim to present a more narrow scope of invention and has also incorporated some of the subject matter of the dependent claims into independent form. As was discussed in the interview that took place on 7/25/2024, these amendments overcame the 102 rejection that had been previously set forth to the applicant. However, a further search and consideration uncovered the Lui reference that is cited here, which is relevant to the subject matter of independent claim 1. As a result, the claim set remains rejected under 35 USC 103.
