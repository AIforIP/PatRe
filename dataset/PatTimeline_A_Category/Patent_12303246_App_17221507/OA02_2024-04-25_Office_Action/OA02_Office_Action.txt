Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph:
The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the applicant regards as his invention.
Claims 1, 8, and 9 rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor (or for applications subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 112, the applicant), regards as the invention.
Regarding claim 1, the limitation ‘flattening the cortical surface of the human brain is unclear. Specifically it is unclear what the nature of said flattening is. For the purposes of this office action the flattening is interpreted as a two-dimensional representation of the anatomy.
Regarding claim 8, in the limitation reading ‘different individuals’, it is unclear what manner of individuals is being referred to. For the purposes of this office action, the individuals are interpreted as a variety of patients.
Regarding claim 9, it is unclear what the limitation reading ‘a common area’ is referring to.
For at least the aforementioned reasons, the depending claims are rejected as well.
