Election/Restrictions
Restriction to one of the following inventions is required under 35 U.S.C. 121:
I. Claim 1-7, drawn to a method and its apparatus for generating a script using one or more parameters of a cognitive appraisal model for participants in character interactions, generating a plurality of scripts by the at least one processor at least in part by modeling the character interactions while varying the parameters between different ones of the scripts, and for each script, estimating a measure of effectiveness compared to one or more targeted story based on a cognitive appraisal model, classified in A61B 5/361.
II. Claims 8-20, drawn to a method for generating communication contents, using sensors to capture neuro-physiological data, selecting segments from the library of alternative segments, calculating a measure of the at least one user’s neuro-physiological response for each segment…producing communication content including selected ones of the segments or selecting participants for a multi-participant communication session, classified in A61B 5/7275.
The inventions are independent or distinct, each from the other because:
Inventions of group I and group II  are related as combination and subcombination.  Inventions in this relationship are distinct if it can be shown that (1) the combination as claimed does not require the particulars of the subcombination as claimed for patentability, and (2) that the subcombination has utility by itself or in other combinations (MPEP § 806.05(c)).  In the instant case, the combination as claimed does not require the particulars of the subcombination as claimed because group II requires one or more sensors coupled to a user, detecting neuro-physiological data, providing segments of communication content, determining neuro-physiological response for each segment and etc..  The subcombination has separate utility such as a device detecting and determining physiological parameters of a user based on data collected from sensors attached to the user.
The examiner has required restriction between combination and subcombination inventions. Where applicant elects a subcombination, and claims thereto are subsequently found allowable, any claim(s) depending from or otherwise requiring all the limitations of the allowable subcombination will be examined for patentability in accordance with 37 CFR 1.104.  See MPEP § 821.04(a).  Applicant is advised that if any claim presented in a continuation or divisional application is anticipated by, or includes all the limitations of, a claim that is allowable in the present application, such claim may be subject to provisional statutory and/or nonstatutory double patenting rejections over the claims of the instant application.
