Allowable Subject Matter
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
As outlined in the Non-Final Rejection filed 12/27/2024, claims 1 and 7 indicated allowable subject matter for reasons reiterated in the paragraph below.
Regarding claims 1 and 7, the closest prior art of Yokoyama (JP 2013034791 - cited by Applicant) discloses a sphygmomanometer cuff comprised of an inner and outer cloth with an expansion bag, a ring-shaped member, a retaining tube and fasteners. The prior art of record does not disclose an end opening nor a retaining member with the specific structure of a flexible projection part with flat, plate-like support parts that is welded to the back surface of the outer fabric in the end opening. Vivenzio (US 8652057) discloses a blood pressure cuff with an opening of the first sheet. However, the socket inserted into the opening is not similar in structure to the claimed retaining member welded to the cuff.
Additionally, Nakanishi (US 20100268100) discloses a blood pressure measurement apparatus with an “anti-drop” member made of resin to prevent the loop from falling out (Fig. 3, element 36). While the prior art such as Nakanishi teaches ways of preventing the two ends of the cuff from detaching, the prior art fails to teach the claimed retaining member and its corresponding structure.
Therefore, the prior art of record does not read on the claims nor does it provide sufficient obviousness rationale to modify the teachings together to read on the claims.
