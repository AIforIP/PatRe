EXAMINER REASONS FOR ALLOWANCE
Claims 1, 5-8, 11, 13-14, 21, 25-28, 31, 33-34, and 81-84 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Independent claim 1 recites, inter alia, “…compute in real time, utilizing the captured brain angle measurements as input in a mathematical mass-spring-damper model, a brain angle metric for the one or more anatomical directions…”. The added limitations overcome the prior art of record and would require an unreasonable combination of references that would not suffice for a realistic case of obviousness.
The closest references found during Examiner’s search of the prior art were Benzel et al (U.S. Publication No. 2012/0220893), Kuo et al (U.S. Publication No. 2017/0156635), Howard et al (U.S. Patent No. 9,129,499), Kirovski et al (U.S. Patent No. 8,761,437), and Gonzales et al (U.S. Publication No. 2017/0238850). None of these references, alone or in combination with one other or any prior art reference, would anticipate or render obvious the invention of claim 1 including “…compute in real time, utilizing the captured brain angle measurements as input in a mathematical mass-spring-damper model, a brain angle metric for the one or more anatomical directions…” in combination with all other claim elements.
Applicant's remarks and amendments with respect to the rejections under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) have been fully considered and are persuasive.
Applicant's remarks and amendments with respect to the rejections under 35 U.S.C. 101 have been fully considered and are persuasive.
