DETAILED ACTION
Claim Objections
Claims 2-5, 7, 9, and 16 are objected to because of the following informalities: the lists in each of the claims should include an “and” before the final listed feature.  For example, in line four or claim 2, “being one of: a linear segment, a curved segment” should be “being one of: a linear segment and a curved segment”.  Please correct all similar issues throughout the claim set. Appropriate correction is required.
Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph:
The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the applicant regards as his invention.
Claims 3 and 7 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor (or for applications subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 112, the applicant), regards as the invention.
The use of the term “freeform” pattern in claim 3 and “freeform” geometry in claim 7 appear to encompass all possible patterns and geometry, which thereby fails to further limit the base claim.  That is, if any pattern or any geometry would qualify under the “freeform” option, then claims 3 and 7 fail to add any addition limitation on the base claim.  Correction is required.
