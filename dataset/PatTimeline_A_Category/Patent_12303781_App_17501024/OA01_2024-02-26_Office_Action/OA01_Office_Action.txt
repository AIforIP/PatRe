Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 103
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 103 which forms the basis for all obviousness rejections set forth in this Office action:
A patent for a claimed invention may not be obtained, notwithstanding that the claimed invention is not identically disclosed as set forth in section 102, if the differences between the claimed invention and the prior art are such that the claimed invention as a whole would have been obvious before the effective filing date of the claimed invention to a person having ordinary skill in the art to which the claimed invention pertains. Patentability shall not be negated by the manner in which the invention was made.
Claim(s) 6 is/are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 103 as being unpatentable over Seok in view if Wang (US 20180121086)
In claim 6, Seok discloses the controlled virtual object to cast the skill at a position indicated by the skill casting operation (paragraph 101-103), Seok fails to disclose controlling, in response to a skill casting operation received on a wheel virtual joystick corresponding to the skill control, however Wang discloses controlling, in response to a skill casting operation received on a wheel virtual joystick corresponding to the skill control (figure 3, paragraphs 32, 49, 73).  It would have been obvious to one of ordinary skill in the art before the effective filing date of the invention to combine Seok with Wang in order to allow for the equipment to be changed more easily thru use of the virtual joystick of Wang
Claim(s) 7 is/are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 103 as being unpatentable over Seok in view if Wang in view of Jiang (US 20190303201)
In claim 7, Wang discloses the first click/tap operation in the screen is detected, obtaining, in response to presence of a UI control at a position indicated by the first click/tap operation, a control type of the UI control, (figure 3, paragraphs 32, 49, 73).  Seok in view of Wang discloses the claimed invention except obtaining triggered states of other skill controls in response to the control type of the UI control being the skill control corresponding to the skill, the other skill controls being skill controls of other skills different form the skill and determining that the first click/tap operation on the skill control is detected in response to the other skill controls not being in triggered states, however Jiang discloses obtaining triggered states of other skill controls in response to the control type of the UI control being the skill control corresponding to the skill, the other skill controls being skill controls of other skills different form the skill and determining that the first click/tap operation on the skill control is detected in response to the other skill controls not being in triggered stat (paragraph 85, a spell may be on a cooldown, which would prohibit use of the spell during that timeframe).  It would have been obvious to one of ordinary skill in the art before the effective filing date of the invention to combine Seok in view of Wang with Jiang in order to incorporate spells on cooldowns into the invention of Seok.
Claim(s) 10 and 14 is/are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 103 as being unpatentable over Seok in view of Miyamae (US 20180304158)
In claims 10 and 14, Seok discloses the skill is for producing a skill effect on a single skill target, and the first skill circle comprises at least two target, the controlling the controlled virtual object to cast the skill in the first skill circle further comprises determining a first target from the at least two targets in the first skill circle according to a target determining principle and controlling the controlled virtual object to cast the skill to produce the skill effect on the first target (paragraph 99) and the target determining principle comprises a principle of nearest distance first (paragraph 99, 102). Seok fails to disclose prioritizing based on a principle of lowest health point first, and a principle of lowest health bar first, however Miyamae discloses automatically selecting based on the lowest hit point value (paragraph 146)  It would have been obvious to one f ordinary skill in the art to combine Seok with Miyamae in order to cause for smarter auto targeting by targeting the enemy closest to being defeated.
