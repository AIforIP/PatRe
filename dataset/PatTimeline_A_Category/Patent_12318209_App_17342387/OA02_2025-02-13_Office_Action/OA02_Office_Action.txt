Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-20 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The subject matter of the independent claims could either not be found or was not suggested in the prior art.  The subject matter not found was a method or an apparatus comprising a processor configured to perform the method, in which identified sub-regions of an ECG are cross-correlated with each other and differentially processing two or more correlated to generate a de-noised ECG signal, in combination with the other remaining steps in the claim.
