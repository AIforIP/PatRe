Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: After further review, the amendments have overcome the prior art rejection. More specifically, Ye does not expressly disclose input the first screen render to at least one machine learning (ML) model; receive from the ML model at least one transform of the first screen render, the transform comprising at least a second screen render from a second viewpoint, the second viewpoint being of a non-player character (NPC); and input the second screen render to a ML-implemented agent controlling the NPC, or present on at least one display the second screen render, or both input the second screen render to a ML-implemented agent controlling the NPC and present on at least one display the second screen render and inputting the NPC video stream to a ML-implemented agent controlling the NPC, wherein the method comprises avoiding rendering multiple viewpoints simultaneously at least in part by: inputting only the PC video stream to the ML model; and using the ML model, transforming the PC video stream to output the NPC video stream. Therefore, claims 1 – 20 are allowed.
