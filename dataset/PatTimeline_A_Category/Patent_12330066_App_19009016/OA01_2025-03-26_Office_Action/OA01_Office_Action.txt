Notice of Pre-AIA  or AIA  Status

(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph:

Claims 4 and 7 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor (or for applications subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 112, the applicant), regards as the invention.

Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 103
Claim(s) 11-13, 21, 22, and 29 is/are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 103 as being unpatentable over Ignatchenko
In claims 11 and 29, Ignatchenko discloses looping through one or more of the data field entries in the encyclopedia that is stored in the data store, populating by the oracle and based on the looping, one or more basic fields in the encyclopedia, upserting, by the oracle, one or more well defined objects in the encyclopedia, (this entire section is simply describing inserting data into the database.  Paragraph 50 which stores a wide variety of data including basic fields and well defined objects)  Ignatchenko fails to explicitly disclose processing, by the oracle and based on the looping, hash-table data structures to update one or more entries in the encyclopedia, and returning information about the one or more data entry fields, however Ignatchenko paragraph 35 discloses JavaScript and python which both have built in hash table data structures, and paragraph 50 discloses use of persistent storage database, which would typically use hash functions as well.  As such, Official notice is taken that it would have been notoriously well known to one of ordinary skill in the art before the effective filing date of the invention to allow for a use of hash functions within the data storage solution, and it would have been obvious to combine Ignatchenko with this well known technique in order to improve synchronization and efficiency.
In claim 12, Official notice is taken that the user of existing and new keys for updates to data entry within a hash function was notoriously well known before the effective filing date of the invention to combine Ignatchenko with this well known technique in order to use a standard hash function in its typical functioning.
In claim 13, Ignatchenko discloses establishing a change plan, looping through the change plan, and processing each of the one or more indexed data entry fields in the list/array based on the change plan (Paragraph 50.  it is noted by examiner that the term “change plan” is extremely broad, and however Ignatchenko changes the data would read upon the “change plan” inherently, as the data is being changed, and a plan would need to be in place, as a computer can only function with programming, which is a “plan”.  Further, as the data is stored in the database, this would mean that the data entry fields needed to be changed would be changed)
In claim 21, Ignatchenko discloses establish data connections between actions in the JIT game world during the JIT gameplay, the entities in the JIT game world, and the user input (paragraph 50).  Ignatchenko discloses the claimed invention except for annotate entries in the JIT game world with UUITs, and link the UUITs do a database however Official notice is taken that UUIDs were notoriously well known before the effective filing date of the invention, and it would have been obvious to one of ordinary skill in the art before the effective filing date of the invention to combine Ignatchenko with this well known technique in order to allow for use of a standardized format for identifying objects in a database.
In claim 22, Ignatchenko discloses the claimed invention except for a game marketplace for publishers and the players to produce encyclopedia game states, wherein the encyclopedia game states are traded, shared, sold, and provided to the players in the marketplace, however Official notice is taken that allowing for anything to be traded, shared sold, or provided to people was notoriously well known before the effective filing date of the invention, and it would have been obvious to one of ordinary skill in the art before the effective filing date of the invention to combine Ignatchenko with this well-known technique in order to allow for players to more freely gain access to data that a user is interested in.

It would have been obvious to one of ordinary skill in the art before the effective filing date of the invention to combine Igantchenko in view of Arnone with this well known technique in order to allow for the NPC to act more autonomously
