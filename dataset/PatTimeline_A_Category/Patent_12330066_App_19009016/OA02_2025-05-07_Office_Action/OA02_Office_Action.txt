Reasons for Allowance
Claims 1-30 are allowed.  Applicant’s amendments and arguments overcome the prior art of record.  Particularly, see applicants arguments pages 13 and 14, and the new claim language of “updating the new JIT game world and the corresponding JIT gameplay based on processing, by the oracle and based on the data field entries in the encyclopedia associated with the new JIT game world for the player that is maintained in the data store, the continuously received user input, and the JIT gameplay” as well as “linking the uuids to one or more of the data field entries in the encyclopedia to establish data connections between the entities in the new JIT game world and the continuously received user input and generating, responsive to the continuously received user input and based on the data connections, actions ion the new JIT game world”.  In combination with the other elements, this would mean that the JIT game world would not simply be updated based on the user input but also based upon the connections identified in the data field and the links between them, so as to create a cohesive game.  As such the claims are allowable over the prior art of record including Ignatchenko and Arnone.
