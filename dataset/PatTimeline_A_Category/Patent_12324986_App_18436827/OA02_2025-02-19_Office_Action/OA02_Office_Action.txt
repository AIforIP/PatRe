DETAILED ACTION
Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 65-85 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: The specific limitations of (with respect to Claims 65, 72, 79) “identifying, by one or more processors, a group of users, based on: a presence, for a period of time, by each user in the group of users, each user representing a respective independently controlled vehicle, an indication of interest by each user in a communication network- mediated interaction with another of the group of users during the period of time, and trip data for each of the vehicles, wherein the trip data comprises one or more locations associated with an interactive media session for execution by interactive media players, each interactive media player associated with a respective user of the group of users; selecting or configuring, by the one or more processors and based on the trip data for each of the vehicles and the locations, an interactive media program for execution by the interactive media players; and providing, by the one or more processors, the interactive media program to the interactive media players during the period of time”, in combination with the remainder of the respective claims are not anticipated or made obvious over the prior art of record in the Examiner’s opinion.
An inter-vehicle game is well known in the art. For instance, Bavitz et al. (2014/0128144) teaches an inter-vehicle game. However, Bavitz is silent on “identifying, by one or more processors, a group of users, based on: a presence, for a period of time, by each user in the group of users, each user representing a respective independently controlled vehicle, an indication of interest by each user in a communication network- mediated interaction with another of the group of users during the period of time, and trip data for each of the vehicles, wherein the trip data comprises one or more locations associated with an interactive media session for execution by interactive media players, each interactive media player associated with a respective user of the group of users; selecting or configuring, by the one or more processors and based on the trip data for each of the vehicles and the locations, an interactive media program for execution by the interactive media players; and providing, by the one or more processors, the interactive media program to the interactive media players during the period of time”,.
As a result, in the Examiner’s opinion, the present invention is not anticipated or made obvious over the prior art.
