Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-2 and 4-10 would be allowable if rewritten or amended to overcome the rejection(s) under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), 2nd paragraph, set forth in this Office action.
The following is a statement of reasons for the indication of allowable subject matter:  the closest prior art of record Noras (US 2015/0305647) teaches an MRI coil configured to be placed around the penis (Figures 1 and 2).  It can be seen that the MRI coil is shaped (Figures 1 and 2) to enclose the scrotum and penis and bears against the groin of the patient when the latter slightly spreads his thighs ([0029]).  Noras fails to show the specific features of the claimed MRI coil, such as: coil units combined as a sleeve structure, which is able to wrap around a penis site, and coil units combined to form a curved triangular structure which is able to be closely attached to a perineum site.  Such an arrangement would improve the image quality associated with the proximity of the coil to the penis, as compared to the arrangement of Noras.  Noras also fails to show an erection control system, including a negative pressure aspirator for controlling pressures to adjust blood flow in the penile vessel and thereby allowing for MRI angiography of the penis site.  Noras also fails to show a contrast injection system thereby allowing for MRI angiography of the penis site.
Furthermore, the patent prosecution highway status is noted, where the international patent was previously allowed.
