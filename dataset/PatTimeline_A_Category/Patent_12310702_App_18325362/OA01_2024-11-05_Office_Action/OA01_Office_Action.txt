Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph:
The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the applicant regards as his invention.
Claim 6 is rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor, or for pre-AIA  the applicant regards as the invention.
Claim 6 recites “wherein each of the light transmission areas is closed in a circumferential direction”. This recitation is unclear because it is unclear what is meant by the light transmission areas being closed and by a circumferential direction. It is unclear whether or not the Applicant is attempting to recite, for example, sealing of the light transmission areas at their edges to make them waterproof or enclosing them with an optically transparent material. For examination purposes, Examiner of record takes this to be --wherein each of the light transmission areas is covered with a light transmissive material--.
