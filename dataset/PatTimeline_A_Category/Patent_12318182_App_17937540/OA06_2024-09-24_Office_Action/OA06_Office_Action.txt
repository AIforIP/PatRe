Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph:
The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the applicant regards as his invention.
Claims 1-29 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor (or for applications subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 112, the applicant), regards as the invention.
Claim 1 recites “an antenna” in line 22 and another “an antenna” in line 23 in page 3. However, it is not clear whether “an antenna” is new and distinct from previously recited “three or four antennas,” “the antennas,” “two antennas,” or “each antenna” in claim 1.
Claim 9 recites “an antenna” in lines 2-3 in page 6. However, it is not definite as “an antenna” is new and distinct from previously recited “a transmit antenna,” “a receive antenna,” “the antennas of the antenna array,” or “two of the antennas.”
Claim 17 recites “a side edge of a first one of the antenna faces a side edge of a second one of the antenna” and is not definite because “first and second side edges” have been already recited previously in the claim and thus, it is not certain whether applicant intends to recite a new an distinct “side edge” that is different from “first and second edges.”
In addition, claim 17 already discloses “a first one of the antennas,” “a second one of the antennas,” and “a third one of the antennas” in lines 12-14 in page 8, and is not certain whether amended limitation of “a side edge of a first one of the antenna faces a side edge of a second one of the antenna, and another side edge of the second one of the antennas faces a side edge of a third one of the antennas” refer to same first, second and third antennas recited previously, or meant to recite a new a distinct first, second, and third antennas.
Claims 2-8, 10-16, and 18-29 are rejected as they inherit rejection of independent claims 1, 9, and 17 due to their dependency.
