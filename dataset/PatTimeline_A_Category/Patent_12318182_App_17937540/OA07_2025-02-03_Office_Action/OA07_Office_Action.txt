Reasons for Allowance
Claims 1-29 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance.
The following prior art previously made of record is considered pertinent to the reasons for indicating allowable subject matter:
Bosua (US2021/0244308) discloses most of the claimed feature of arrangement of analyte sensors with different geometries.
However, the prior art previously and currently made of record fails to disclose or make obvious the structure limitations of figures 7 and 8 in combination with the rest of the limitations of independent claim(s) 1, 9, and 17.  There is no reason absent hindsight to have combined and modified teachings of the cited references before the effective filing date of the claimed invention for a user to modify and combine with other prior arts in order to produce the claimed invention.  Furthermore, such a configuration allows adjustable positions to configure multiple antennas in a desired configuration as in figures 7 and 8, advantages claimed by present invention.
Therefore, claim(s) 1,9, and 17 overcome(s) previously and currently cited prior art and is/are found to be allowable.
