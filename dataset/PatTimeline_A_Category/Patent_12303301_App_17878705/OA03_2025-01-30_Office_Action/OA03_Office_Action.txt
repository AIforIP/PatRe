Allowable Subject Matter
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: US 20090105604 A1 teaches EMG to monitor nerve activity and warn of a spontaneous response (¶[0087]; claim 46), but not with MMG (mechanical sensors).  US 10420480 B1 teaches using EMG to monitor for spontaneous muscle activity that might be indicative of danger (col 19, ln 30-34) but not with MMG (mechanical sensors).  The prior art does not teach or suggest “displaying, on a display having a graphical user interface (GUI): if the response is a result of an electrical stimulus, at least one of: an amplitude of the response; or an identity of a nerve associated with the muscle; or if the response is a spontaneous response: a warning” wherein the response is detected using at least one of the plurality of mechanical sensors.
