The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: as noted in the remarks dated 1/29/2025, p. 9, the prior art of record does not teach or make obvious in combination, a system comprising all of the elements of claims 8, 13, 16, and 17, using a recording device consisting only of a stethoscope or digital audio recording device for obtaining recorded auscultated lung sounds. As noted by Applicant on p. 9-10 of the Remarks dated 1/29/2025, the closest prior art reference of Caro (U.S. Patent Application Publication No. 2006/0243280) does not suggest a recording device consisting only of a stethoscope or digital audio recording device because Caro uses and requires signal injection, therefore Caro does not teach a method or system lacking signal injection.
