Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: The prior art of record fails to disclose, teach, or fairly suggest a sphygmomanometer provided with an air system for blood pressure measurement and capable of performing air leakage testing on the air system as claimed. The prior art that comes closest to teaching the limitations is Mori et al. ‘950, which teaches the claimed structure, the main control unit and the steps performed by the main control unit, and the notification control unit, the steps performed by the notification control unit, and the air leakage testing unit. However, it was found that Mori et al. ‘950 does not teach the air leakage testing unit to perform the claimed steps after the blood pressure measurement and before the second transition. An updated search and consideration was performed. Although the Examiner has found art that teaches performing an air leakage test in sphygmomanometers (such as Sawanoi et al. ‘221 (US Pub No. 2011/0282221), there was no art that was found to teach performing the air leakage test after the blood pressure measurement and before the second transition. It would require an unreasonable combination of references that would not suffice for a realistic case of obviousness.
It is noted that the Examiner and Mr. Hibbeler have discussed potential amendments for claim 1, in particular, lines 32-51. However, upon further consideration, Mr. Hibbeler and the Examiner have agreed that the language lines 32-51 do not need to be amended as lines 32-51 state what the processor acting as the notification control unit displays.
