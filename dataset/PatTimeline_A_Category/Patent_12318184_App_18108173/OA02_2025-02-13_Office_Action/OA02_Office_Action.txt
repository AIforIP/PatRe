Allowable Subject Matter
Claim 1-9 & 12-15 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The cited prior art of Mazurkewitz and Leng fail to teach of repeating a first scanning process based on a consistency metric of a localizer scan and a first scanning process {e.g. baseline scan}. Chang et al. (US PGPUB 20190369181; hereinafter Chang) teaches of repeating a localizer and calibration scan when the ROI does not coincide with the coil sensitivity map (see Chang FIG. 3), but does not teach of repeating a high-quality baseline or first scanning process. Chang only distinguishes between the localizer scan and calibration scan in the calibration scan is a full mode scan {i.e. all of the coils}.
