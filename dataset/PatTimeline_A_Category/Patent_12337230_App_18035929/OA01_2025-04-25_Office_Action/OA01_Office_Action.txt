2.	Claims 1-20 are allowed.
3.	The following is a statement of reasons for the indication of allowable subject matter:  the closest prior art is CN 105355496A (‘496).  According to ‘496, the key provides in [0024-0034] disclosure of a base 3, an upper cover {casing} 7, a mechanical shaft 1 {key switch}, a cavity, a top of the key switch exposed from a surface of the casing, a through hole provided at a position on the base, a spring 8, side walls and first and second bumps 61, a plate body 9.  However, ‘496 fails to disclose a gap is present between each of two sides of the plate body and the base.  This gap allows plate body 7 to incline outwards and thus the force curve of a traditional conductive adhesive is simulated (Spec. 6).  Additionally, ‘496 lacks a conductive silica gel.  The Incoming Written Opinion relies on CN 205069465U to provide a conductive adhesive, however this reference fails to provide the lack of a gap between each side of the plate body.  Further search teaches that U.S. Pat. Pub. No. 2004/0118669 to Mou and U.S. Pat. No. 8,698,021 to Chu do not teach a gap on each side of a plate body.
