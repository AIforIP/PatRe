Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The closest prior art of record does not disclose nor render obvious the above claimed invention. The closest art, Baker, does not teach that a battery is within the non-conductive receptacle that then goes on to receive the housing which ultimately contains the other electronic components, where the battery is located between the bottom surface of the non-conductive receptacle and the housing. The claim is being read in that the battery must take up a physically different space than the claimed housing given the above detailed positional relationship.
Bay, US Publication 2015/0250422, teaches a compartment/cavity in the nonconductive receptacle that then is configured to receive a housing with the same claimed electronic components, however the skilled artisan would not have been inclined to place the battery in the compartment as per Figures 2-4 as there are already flexible circuits located there. Further including a battery within the non-conductive receptacle would not have been obvious with the housing (of the monitor) claimed as well without the use of the Applicant’s original disclosure as a blueprint for doing so.
