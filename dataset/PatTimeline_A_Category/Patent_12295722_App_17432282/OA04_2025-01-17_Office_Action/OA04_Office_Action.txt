Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: Weiss 2013/031 7393 is representative of the closest prior art. Weiss discloses a pressure sensing mat comprising a first portion including a first conductive layer 220a contacting a first non-conductive layer 240a, wherein the first conductive layer is continuous except for a first plurality of discontinuities defining a first plurality of conductive strips oriented in a first direction, and wherein the first portion includes first and second sides, with each conductive strip of the first plurality of conductive strips formed as columns including first and second columns extending to the first and second sides (Figs. 3A-3B). Weiss further discloses a second portion including a second conductive layer 220b layered to a second non-conductive layer 240b, wherein the second conductive layer is continuous except for a second plurality of discontinuities defining a second plurality of conductive strips oriented in a second direction and an insulative layer 230 disposed between the first portion and the second portion. The first plurality of conductive strips and the second plurality of conductive strips form a conductive strip matrix having a plurality of capacitors 210 wherein each capacitor of the plurality of capacitors are configured to provide a capacitance indicative of a pressure applied at each capacitor of the plurality of capacitors. However, Weiss does not teach or suggest channels defining the plurality of conductive strips.
