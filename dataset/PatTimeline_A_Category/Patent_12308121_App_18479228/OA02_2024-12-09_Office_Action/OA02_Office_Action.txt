The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The art of record, whether taken alone or in combination, does not teach or make obvious the combinations of limitations recited, as exemplified in claim 1:
based on the application of the one or more machine learning models to the    episode data, derive, for each of the arrhythmia type classifications,  respective class   activation data  comprising a respective time series of likelihood values indicating varying    likelihoods of the classification over the period of time;   generate data that indicates that at least one of the arrhythmia type classifications   occurred at any point during the period of time based on the application of the one or   more machine learning models to the episode data; and   generate data that indicates at least one point during the period of time of higher   likelihood for the at least one of the arrhythmia type classifications relative to other points during  over the period of time  based on the class activation data.
The claim limitations are quite unique in the sense that the system store episode data of cardiac electrogram over a period of time. Then uses learning model to categorize the data into different arrhythmia classification during the period of time of collection. Then generate data that indicate the at least one of the arrhythmia type classifications. Then generate data that indicates at least one point during the period time of higher likelihood for at least one of the arrhythmia type classifications. These limitations are not specifically taught by the prior arts.
