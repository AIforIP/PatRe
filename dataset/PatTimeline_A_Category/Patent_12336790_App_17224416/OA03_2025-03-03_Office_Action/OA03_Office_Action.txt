Claims 2-24 are allowed.

Searching by the Examiner yielded prior art as cited in the previous Office Actions.  Based on Applicant’s arguments and the Examiner’s review of the Office Action mailed on September 16, 2024, the subject matter is deemed to be allowable. Further explanation can be reviewed in the Applicant’s arguments filed on January 16, 2025. Therefore, it is concluded by the examiner that claims 2-24 of the present invention are allowable.
