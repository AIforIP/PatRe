Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 102
The following is a quotation of the appropriate paragraphs of 35 U.S.C. 102 that form the basis for the rejections under this section made in this Office action:
A person shall be entitled to a patent unless –
(a)(1) the claimed invention was patented, described in a printed publication, or in public use, on sale, or otherwise available to the public before the effective filing date of the claimed invention.
Claim(s) 1-3 and 5 is/are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 102(a)(1) as being anticipated by Nakagami et al. (Nakagami US 2017/0209230).
1: Nakagami teaches a needle disposal container (generally indicated as 2) capable of disposing of an unclaimed first needle unit having an unclaimed first need hub and an unclaimed second needle unit having an unclaimed second needle hub with a smaller outer diameter than the first unclaimed needle hub, comprising:
a container body (container body 4) having an open top (open top at 6); and
a covering attached to the container body (lid 8), wherein: the covering includes a lid body (body of 8) that covers the open top of the container body and a movable member (member generally indicated as 48) attached to the lid body so as to be movable relative to the lid body (48 moves relative to the body of 8) between a hold position (hold position Figure 3) and a retract position (retract position Figure 4) in a first direction (direction shown in Figure 4, left to right);
the lid body is formed with a first wall (wall, the thickness of 8) extending in a second direction (direction from top to bottom of Figure 2) perpendicular to the first direction, the first wall having a first surface with a concave-arc cross-section (surface along 46, concave arc cross section at 54, 56, 58);
the movable member is formed with a second wall (member 48 is formed with a second wall, formed by the thickness of 48) extending in the second direction (direction from top to bottom of Figure 2), the second wall having a second surface (surface along 66,64) with a concave-arc cross-section, the second surface confronting the first surface of the first wall in the first direction (the second and first surface are opposite one another in the direction shown in Figure 4);
the first surface of the first wall and the second surface of the second wall together form a cylindrical surface (surfaces along 54 and 64) that defines an insertion opening (opening formed by 54 and 64) extending in the second direction, the insertion opening having a top side (top side along 56 and 66) and a bottom side (side bottom of 54 and 64)opposite to the top side in the second direction (in the direction from top to bottom of Figure 2);
when the movable member is moved from the hold position to the retract position, then the second wall of the movable member moves away from the first wall of the lid body to widen the insertion opening, bringing the insertion opening into a widened state (see Figure 4, where the walls separate and the opening is widened);
when the movable member is moved from the retract position to the hold position, then the second wall of the movable member moves toward the first wall of the lid body to narrow the insertion opening, bringing the insertion opening into a narrow state (see Figure 3, where the second wall is moved towards the first wall to  a narrow state);
the cylindrical surface is formed with a first rotation-preventer (first rotation preventer 68) and a second rotation-preventer (second preventer 70) at a position closer to the bottom side of the insertion opening than the first rotation-preventer (preventer 70 is closer to the bottom of 64 and 54); the first rotation-preventer prevents rotation of the first needle unit inserted into the insertion opening (capable of preventing rotation by engaging area 68, 58); and the second rotation-preventer prevents rotation of the second needle unit inserted into the insertion opening (capable of preventing rotation by engaging area 70, 60).
2: Nakagami teaches the claimed invention as discussed above for Claim 1 and Nakagami further teaches that the first rotation-preventer includes at least one first protrusion (first 68 protrudes from 66, Figure 10) that protrudes radially inward of the insertion opening from the cylindrical surface by a first amount, and the second rotation-preventer includes at least one second protrusion that protrudes radially inward of the insertion opening from the cylindrical surface by a second amount greater than the first amount (60/70 protrudes a greater amount than the first amount).
