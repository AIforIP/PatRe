Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-27 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The objections to the drawings and the claims are withdrawn in light of the amendments to the drawings and the claims, filed 01/07/25.
As noted in the Ex Parte Quayle Action, filed 11/02/24, pp. 3-4, the combination of all the elements of the independent claims do not appear in a single reference of prior art. Additionally, based on the art of record, it does not appear that it would have been obvious to a person of ordinary skill in the art at the time the application was filed to combine various pieces of the cited prior art to obtain each and every limitation as currently required by the independent claims. Accordingly, claims 1-27 are allowed.
