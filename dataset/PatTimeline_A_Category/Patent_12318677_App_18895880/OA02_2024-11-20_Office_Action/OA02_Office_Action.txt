Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-6, 8-14, and 16-27 are allowed. Claims 7 and 15 are currently objected to, as presented in detail above, but would be allowable if corrected as previously discussed above.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
As presented, the combination of all the elements of the independent claims do not appear in a single reference of prior art. Additionally, based on the art of record, it does not appear that it would have been obvious to a person of ordinary skill in the art at the time the application was filed to combine various pieces of the cited prior art to obtain each and every limitation as currently required by the independent claims.
The closest relevant prior art, U.S. Pub. 2023/0381629 A1, directed to a device, comprising in part a vibration device, that allows an umpire to control the operations of a baseball game (i.e., a pitch clock) has a public availability date of November 30, 2023, which is after the effective filing date of April 12, 2023 of the present application. Thereby, U.S. Pub. 2023/0381629 A1 is not applicable prior art. The same may be said regarding U.S. Pub. 2023/0158391 A1, which teaches a signaling system for use on a baseball field. The additional relevant prior art, U.S. Pub. 2005/0064961 A1, which discusses a pitch clock, a time limit for a batter to return to the box, and accompanying penalties, as well as the below-recited non-patent literature, fail to disclose, teach, and/or reasonably suggest all claim limitations of the independent claims, such as, for example, the recited hardware working in combination to perform the claimed functions. Accordingly, claims 1-6, 8-14, and 16-27 are allowed.
