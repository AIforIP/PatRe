The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The best prior art of record Abreu (US 20090105605) and of Roth (US 20140003462), fail to specifically teach the invention as claimed. The limitations of a method for measuring a temperature by way of an ABBT (see Examiner’s Note of record in action of 9/18/24 regarding this area) comprising the limitations regarding positioning a sensor with a handle attached to a housing; displaying an advertisement on a display; measuring a temperature; producing a signal and reporting a temperature in independent claim 1 when combined with the specific limitations of the display being attached to and protruding from the housing and configured as claimed as well as all additional limitations also in independent claim 1 distinguish the present invention from the combined prior art.
Hence the prior art of record fails to teach the invention as set forth in claims 1-20. The examiner cannot find specific teaching of the invention, nor reasons within the cited art to combine the elements of these references other than applicant’s own reasoning to fully encompass the current pending claims.
