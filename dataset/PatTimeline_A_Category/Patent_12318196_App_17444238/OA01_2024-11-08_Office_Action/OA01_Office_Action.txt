Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 102
The following is a quotation of the appropriate paragraphs of 35 U.S.C. 102 that form the basis for the rejections under this section made in this Office action:
A person shall be entitled to a patent unless –
(a)(2) the claimed invention was described in a patent issued under section 151, or in an application for patent published or deemed published under section 122(b), in which the patent or application, as the case may be, names another inventor and was effectively filed before the effective filing date of the claimed invention.
Claim(s) 21 and 28 - 32 is/are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 102(a)(2) as being anticipated by Lin et al. (USPN 9,192,330 - cited by Applicant). Lin et al. teach an arrangement (Figures 1 and 2) for measurement of regional oximetry information from a subject (column 2, line 63 – column 3, line 23), including a processor for receiving the optical measurement data from the sensor and a display for providing information on the subject’s regional oxygen saturation measurements. Lin et al. indicate that when the sensor is disconnected and reconnected, the monitor may provide the caregiver with a prompt (column 8, line 44 – column 9, line 41, with particular attention to column 9, lines 26 – 31), which necessarily serves to give the caregiver information on the connectivity status. Additionally in regard to claims 28 – 32, as disclosed by Lin et al. (column 7, lines 18 – 49) the output may provide different traces each with both numerical and graphical outputs and further, particularly as shown in Figure 3 (column 7, line 50 – column 8, line 13) the output includes baseline or baseline trend information related to information about the subject that was obtained prior to the start of a procedure. By showing both the measurement data and the baseline level, the display provides a graphical representation of a differential analysis of those parameters. The description of Figure 4 (column 8, line 14 – 43) provides information on the manner to create, store, and provide the baseline data.
Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 103
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 103 which forms the basis for all obviousness rejections set forth in this Office action:
A patent for a claimed invention may not be obtained, notwithstanding that the claimed invention is not identically disclosed as set forth in section 102, if the differences between the claimed invention and the prior art are such that the claimed invention as a whole would have been obvious before the effective filing date of the claimed invention to a person having ordinary skill in the art to which the claimed invention pertains. Patentability shall not be negated by the manner in which the invention was made.
Claim(s) 25 - 27 is/are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 103 as being unpatentable over Lin et al. as applied to claim 21 above, further in view of Su et al. (USPGPub 2014/0275875). Lin et al. teach a regional oximetry measurement arrangement as discussed above. Further, Lin et al. recognize that measurements may be performed at a variety of locations (column 3, lines 51 – 63). As such, Lin et al. teach all of the features of the claimed invention, except for providing a visual guide on the display.  Su et al. teach a system and method for positioning a sensor and recognize that the display may provide instructions for placement of the various sensors (paragraph [0023]). As such, it would have been within the skill level of the art before the effective filing date of the claimed invention to have implemented Lin et al. to have the monitor provide information for placement of the sensor on a subject, as taught by Su et al.
Allowable Subject Matter
The following is a statement of reasons for the indication of allowable subject matter:  Applicant cites numerous references regarding optical physiological measurement systems. While adapter elements for use between sensors and monitors are known (Kiani et al. - USPN 5,995,855; Mills et al. – USPN 6,165,005; Barrett et al. – USPN 6,615,065; Lisogurski – USPGPub 2011/0077473; Telfort et al. – USPGPub 2011/0209915) these references do not teach or suggest a pod array//connectivity status output configuration, in combination with the other claimed elements.
Claims 22 – 24 are objected to as being dependent upon a rejected base claim, but would be allowable if rewritten in independent form including all of the limitations of the base claim and any intervening claims.
