Allowable Claims

As to claims 31, 35, 39 and 41, in combination with other limitations of the claims, when considered as a whole, the prior art of record fails to disclose or specifically suggested wherein the detection circuit is configured to detect a common element in at least two channels of the multichannel input audio signal; and an adjustment circuit configured to suppress the component of the audio signal to generate an output audio signal according to a characteristic of the component.
