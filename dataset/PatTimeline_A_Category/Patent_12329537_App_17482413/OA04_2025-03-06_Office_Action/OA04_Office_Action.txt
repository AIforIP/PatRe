Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph:
The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the applicant regards as his invention.
Claims 1-4, 6, 7, and 9-15 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor (or for applications subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 112, the applicant), regards as the invention. Regarding claim 1, the added limitations of “and a derivative of each of the epochs of the received epoch signal” and “and a variance of each of the epochs of the received epoch signal” render the claim indefinite. In combination with the other elements of their respective limitations, it is unclear what the addition of each of the newly added phrases means. For example, what is meant by “extract a derivative feature of the received epoch signal by computing a mean of accumulated squared-differences among the epochs in the received epoch signal and a derivate of each of the epochs of the received epoch signal”? Are two different features extracted – (1) a derivative feature, and (2) a derivative of each of the epochs? If so, how is the “derivative of each epoch” different than the “derivative feature”? Or, is the derivative feature extracted by the entirety of “computing a mean of accumulated squared-differences among the epochs in the received epoch signal and a derivative of each of the epochs of the received epoch signal”? If the “derivative of each of the epochs” is used to extract the “derivative feature”, it is still unclear how the “derivative of each of the epochs” is different than the “derivative feature”. The same indefiniteness issues apply to the “extract a variance feature” limitation of the claim. Claim 1 further recites generating a classifier output for “each of the epochs of the received epoch signal”. As a single derivative feature and a single variance feature are extracted for each epoch signal (not for each epoch of the epoch signal), it is unclear how a classifier output would be generated for each of the epochs of the epoch signal. It is further unclear how a plurality of the classifier outputs corresponding to the epochs of the epoch signal would be accumulated. With a single extracted derivative feature and a single extracted variance feature, each mapped to their original value and their square value, a classifier would output a single classifier output for the epoch signal as a whole. The same indefiniteness issues are also present in claim 10. Further regarding claim 10, the claim recites classifying each of the epochs using “the mapped derivate feature and the mapped variance feature” (emphasis added). If each epoch is classified using the same mapped derivate feature and the same mapped variance feature, each epoch would be classified exactly the same. Claim 10 also recites the phrase “the classifier output value”, which lacks proper antecedent basis. Regarding claim 3, the phrase “the frequency band is within 250 Hz” renders the claim indefinite. It is unclear what is meant by a frequency band being “within 250 Hz”. The same indefiniteness issue applies to claim 13. Regarding claim 9, the phrases “the plurality of the determined levels of anesthesia” and “[an] a value of each of the determined levels of anesthesia” lack proper antecedent basis. Regarding claim 11, it is unclear what further limitation the claim provides as the claim fails to recite a further step; the claim recites a result of performing the method. Regarding claim 12, it is unclear what the claimed “confident prediction” is. How is the “confident prediction” different than the predicted likelihood of claim 10?
Claims not explicitly rejected above are rejected due to their dependence on a rejected base claim.
