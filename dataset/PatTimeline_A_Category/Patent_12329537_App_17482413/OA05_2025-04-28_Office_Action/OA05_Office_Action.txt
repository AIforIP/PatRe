The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: None of the prior art teaches or suggests, either alone or in combination a device comprising an epoch generator, wherein the epoch generator comprises a two-input multiplexer, an address generator, and a memory, wherein the two-input multiplexer is configured to receive a down-sampled filtered incoming measured EEG signal, a feedback of an epoch signal, and a selector input from the address generator, and further wherein the address generator is configured to generate the selector input to the multiplexer to generate a multiplexer output from the down-sampler output signal during each of a plurality of slow count cycles and from the feedback of the epoch signal during each of a plurality of fast count cycles, in combination with the other claimed elements..
