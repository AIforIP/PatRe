Summary
This Office Correspondence is based on the Amendment filed with the Office on 18 December 2024, regarding the DexCom, Inc. application.
Claims 1-24 are currently pending and have been fully considered.
Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-24 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: The closest prior art to the instant claims is considered to be the previously applied combination of the Boock reference (US 2009/0247856 A1) in view of the Jiang reference (US 2008/0181861 A1).  However, each instant independent claims 1 and 14 recite that zwitterionic compounds (or such elements) are covalently bounded to the base polymer.  This differs from the teachings of Jiang, which teaches an interpenetrated polymer network that is not anticipatory or obvious of covalently binding to a base polymer.  Thus, the independent claims 1 and 14 are allowable, as are all their dependent claims.
