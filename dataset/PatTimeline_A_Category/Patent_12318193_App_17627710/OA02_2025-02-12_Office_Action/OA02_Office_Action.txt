The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Regarding claims 1 and 18, the closest prior art reference, Lee et al. (US 2008/0105065 A1) (hereinafter Lee), teaches various limitations found in the claims, as described in the Office Action mailed on 27 September 2024. Regarding claims 2-4, 8-17, and 19-21, they are dependent on or include all of claims 1 and 18.
