Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-13 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: the combination of the most pertinent prior art Tulkki et al (US 2005/0268725) and Tenerz et al (US 6,112,598) teaches an apparatus, comprising: a guide wire configured to be positioned within a blood vessel of a patient, wherein the guide wire comprises a proximal portion and a distal portion; a mounting structure positioned at the distal portion of the guide wire, and a sensor configured to obtain data related to the blood vessel, wherein the body portion is mounted in the mounting structure.
The prior art does not teach, disclose and/or fairly suggest the mounting structure comprises a plurality of material layers defining a first section of the mounting structure and a second section of the mounting structure, wherein the plurality of material layers is formed on top of one another such that the second section is lower than the first section; the sensor comprising a body portion and an active portion, wherein the body portion is mounted in the first section of the mounting structure on a first surface of the plurality of material layers, wherein the active portion is cantilevered over the second section of the mounting structure.
