This is a decision on the petition under the unintentional provisions of 37 CFR 1.137(a), filed March 31, 2025, to revive the above-identified application.
The petition is GRANTED.
This application became abandoned for failure to timely pay the issue fee as required by the Notice of Allowance and Fee(s) Due mailed December 4, 2024, which set a statutory period for reply of three months.  Accordingly, the application became abandoned on March 5, 2025.  A Notice of Abandonment was mailed on March 21, 2025.
The petition satisfies the requirements of 37 CFR 1.137(a) in that petitioner has supplied (1) the reply in the form of an issue fee transmittal and issue fee payment of $1200.00, (2) the petition fee of $2260.00, and (3) a proper statement of unintentional delay.
The file does not indicate a change of correspondence address has been submitted, and the address found on the instant petition differs from the correspondence address of record. If appropriate, a change of correspondence address should be filed in accordance with MPEP § 601.03(a). A courtesy copy of this decision is being mailed to the address found on the petition; however, the Office will mail correspondence not related to this petition solely to the correspondence address of record. See 37 CFR 1.33(a) and form AIA /122.
This application is being referred to the Office of Data Management for further processing into a patent.
