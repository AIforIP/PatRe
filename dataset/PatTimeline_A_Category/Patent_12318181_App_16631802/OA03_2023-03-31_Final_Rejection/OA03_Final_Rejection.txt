Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph:
The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the applicant regards as his invention.
Claim 34 is rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor (or for applications subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 112, the applicant), regards as the invention.
Regarding claim 34, it is unclear regarding the interpretation of the claim. The claim may be interpreted as for the pseudo sensor data does not have to have a response to an indication that the user equipment or sensor is providing the native sensor data to the user in order to be derived. The other interpretation may be that the pseudo sensor data is entirely not being derived, because a response was received that there was an indication that the user equipment or sensor is providing native sensor data for the user. For examination purposes, the first interpretation shall be used.

Response to Arguments
Applicant's arguments filed 12/12/2022 have been fully considered but they are not persuasive.
Applicant’s arguments with respect to claims 16, 27, and 35 have been considered but are moot because the new ground of rejection does not rely on any reference applied in the prior rejection of record for any teaching or matter specifically challenged in the argument. Gillian in view of Stivoric has been used to teach the new limitations.
Applicant’s arguments with respect to claim 24 have been considered but are moot because the new ground of rejection does not rely on any reference applied in the prior rejection of record for any teaching or matter specifically challenged in the argument. Gillian in view of Alsheikh has been used to teach the amended limitations.
Regarding claim 34, Applicant has argued that Gillian does not teach the limitations of claim 34. Examiner disagrees, as Examiner disagrees with Applicant’s interpretation of the claim as seen in the 112(b) rejection. The claim may be interpreted so that the pseudo sensor data does not have to have a response to an indication that the user equipment or sensor is providing the native sensor data to the user in order to be derived, and may be derived for any other reason. Therefore, Gillian does teach that the pseudo sensor data is no longer derived in response to an indication that the sensor is providing native sensor data for the user, as the features are being extracted ([0073]) without there being a response that the sensor is providing native sensor data for the user.
