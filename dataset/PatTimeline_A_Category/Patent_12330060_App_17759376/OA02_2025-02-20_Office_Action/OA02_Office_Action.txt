Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The claimed invention of claims 1, 3-13, 15, and 18-21 meets the subject matter eligibility requirements of 35 USC 101 for at least the reason, that the claimed invention incorporates the practical application of an improvement in technology, as defined by MPEP 2106.05(a).  Specifically, the claimed features including those describing an enhanced user interface, mirror similar consideration relied upon the court in determining the patent eligibility of analogous user interface improvements in Data Engine Tech. LLC v. Google LLC 906 F.3d 999; 128 USPQ2d 1381 (Fed Cir. 2018) and are further noted as reflecting similar improvements recognized by the Applicant’s specification in at least paragraphs [0042]-[0046], [0082], [0085], [0092].
Additionally, the Applicant filed amendments, and arguments accompanying the same, are reasonably persuasive in overcoming the most relevant and previously applied prior art of record, Huang et al (CN 109771941) as evidenced by the Dialog® English translation dated September 4th, 2024.  In particular while Huang teaches an improved interface method for selecting co-presented objects on a display it does not teach, or reasonably suggest, the claimed invention as amended and including a displayed detection region with a present length and width presented in a preset direction of the first operation by serving the touch point as a starting point as particularly presented and when considered in context of the remaining respective claim limitations of each presented claim.
