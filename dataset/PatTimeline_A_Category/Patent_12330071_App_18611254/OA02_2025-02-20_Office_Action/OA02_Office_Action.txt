Regarding the claimed terms, the Examiner notes that a “general term must be understood in the context in which the inventor presents it.” In re Glaug 283 F.3d 1335, 1340, 62 USPQ2d 1151, 1154 (Fed. Using these definitions for the claims, the claimed invention was not reasonably found in the prior art.
