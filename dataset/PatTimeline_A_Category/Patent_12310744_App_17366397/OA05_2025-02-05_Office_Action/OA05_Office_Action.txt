The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Regarding claim 1, none of the prior art teaches or suggests, either alone or in combination, a method comprising determining that a bioelectrical brain signal includes a first biomarker, wherein determining that the bioelectrical brain signal includes the first biomarker comprises determining a first peak width less than or equal to a first threshold value or greater than or equal to a second threshold value of a distribution of a first signal strength within two or more frequency sub-bands of a first set of two or more frequency sub-bands of a frequency band of interest, in combination with the other claimed steps.
Regarding claims 11 and 21, none of the prior art teaches or suggests, either alone or in combination, a system comprising one or more processors configured to determine that a bioelectrical brain signal includes a first biomarker, wherein determining that the bioelectrical brain signal includes the first biomarker comprises determining a first peak width less than or equal to a first threshold value or greater than or equal to a second threshold value of a distribution of a first signal strength within two or more frequency sub-bands of a first set of two or more frequency sub-bands of a frequency band of interest, in combination with the other claimed elements.
Regarding claim 22, none of the prior art teaches or suggests, either alone or in combination, a non-transitory computer readable storage medium comprising instructions that, when executed by one or more processors, cause the one or more processors to determine that a bioelectrical brain signal includes a first biomarker, wherein determining that the bioelectrical brain signal includes the first biomarker comprises determining a first peak width less than or equal to a first threshold value or greater than or equal to a second threshold value of a distribution of a first signal strength within two or more frequency sub-bands of a first set of two or more frequency sub-bands of a frequency band of interest, in combination with the other claimed steps.
