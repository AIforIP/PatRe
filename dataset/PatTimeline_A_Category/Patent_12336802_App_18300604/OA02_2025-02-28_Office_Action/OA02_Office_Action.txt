Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-13 allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Regarding claim 1, the closest prior at is considered Nezafat (US 2023/0194641). Nezafat teaches quantitative parameter mapping using a T2 prep sequence [See Fig. 4]. However, Nezafat is at least silent in teaching “performing, by the MRI system, N scans C_1, . . . ,C_N, wherein each scan C_j, with j=1, . . . ,N, includes: performing at least one magnetization preparation radio frequency (RF) pulse series, each followed by M_j readout blocks, wherein two successive said magnetization preparation RF pulse series are separated by a repetition time interval TR_j, wherein each said magnetization preparation RF pulse series is a T2-prepared inversion pulse series containing multiple pulses and characterized by an echo time, being an inter-pulse duration, TEp_j, wherein the inter-pulse duration is a time between first and last pulses, and is varied by the MRI system in order to obtain different T2 weightings, the at least one magnetization preparation RF pulse series being configured for inverting a longitudinal magnetization while encoding T2 contrast in a subsequent T1 recover; acquiring, by the MRI system and during each readout block, an MRI recovery signal generated by at least a part of the biological object, wherein for each said readout block R_i,j, an MRI signal S_i,j is acquired by the MRI system at a different inversion time TI_i,j, with i=1, . . . ,M_j, and M_j ≥ 2; reconstructing, for and from each said MRI signal S_i,j, an image l_i,j of the part; creating a voxel-wise signal by concatenating intensity values obtained for a same voxel for reconstructed images; and fitting a physical model to concatenated intensity values to obtain at least one qMRI map of the at least one qMRI parameter.”
See Fig. 6 of Nezafat which shows the input to the neural network, which is four T1 weighted images with corresponding inversion times. However, the pixel values of the four T1 weighted images do not seem to be concatenated. Further, the T2 weighted sequence in Fig. 4 does not teach different inversion times and multiple readouts after each T2 preparation sequence. Therefore, the claims overcome the most relevant prior art.
Claims 2-13 are considered allowable for depending on claim 1.
