Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The prior art discloses beamforming data transmissions to user terminals within a coverage area (U.S. 2018/0041270 para. 92-97). The prior art discloses predicted user locations and transmission conditions (U.S. 2019/0364492 para. 1008-1010). The prior art provides for moving antennas to improve transmission (U.S. 2022/0264425 para. 58-63). However, no prior art anticipates or in combination renders obvious a hub using “location information and network performance telemetry information from one or more controllers connected to the hub”, “first mapping correlating usage of users of the one or more controllers with an environment around the hub based on the location information” with “a second mapping correlating the network performance telemetry information with the environment around the hub” to determine “first unstable zones based on the first mapping, the second mapping, and a first criteria” and “an action based on the first unstable zones”. Accordingly claims 1-20 are allowed.
