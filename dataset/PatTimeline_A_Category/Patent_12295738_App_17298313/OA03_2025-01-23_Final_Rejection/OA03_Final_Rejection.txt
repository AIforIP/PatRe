Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The prior art of record does not teach or suggest the claimed invention as a whole, especially the following limitations of the independent claim 3: wherein the one or more working electrodes is a complex working electrode consisting essentially of a mixture of 50% gold, 27% platinum, and 33% tungsten or of 60% gold, 10% platinum, and 30% tungsten.
The closest prior art to the present application is Kumar et al. (US20170241945A1) in view of Arbault et al. (US20080294026A1).
As outlined in the rejection of claim 3 in the non-final office action mailed on September 12, 2024, Kumar teaches all limitations of the independent claim 3 except:
wherein the one or more working electrodes is a complex working electrode consisting essentially of a mixture of 50% gold, 27% platinum, and 33% tungsten or of 60% gold, 10% platinum, and 30% tungsten.
Arbault teaches electrochemical device for measuring the redox state of the skin comprising at least one working electrode, a counter electrode and a reference electrode (abstract). The active surface of the working electrode is made of carbon or of metal, the constituent metal being selected from the group consisting of gold, platinum, iridium, tungsten, and palladium, preferably platinum, or an alloy of these metals [para. 0043].
Thus, Kumar as modified by Arbault teaches wherein the one or more working electrodes is a complex working electrode consisting essentially of a mixture of  gold, platinum, and tungsten.
This application is allowable because Kumar as modified by Arbault  does not teach and suggest the specific concentrations of gold, platinum, and tungsten in the complex working electrode: 50% gold, 27% platinum, and 33% tungsten or of 60% gold, 10% platinum, and 30% tungsten, recited in independent claim 3.
The claims are therefore considered to be patentably distinguished from the prior art of record. The prior art of record, whether taken alone or in combination, does not disclose nor render obvious the cumulative limitations of independent claim 3. Claims 1-2, 5-11 and 13 are dependent from or otherwise include the limitations of claim 3 and are allowable for the same reasons above.
