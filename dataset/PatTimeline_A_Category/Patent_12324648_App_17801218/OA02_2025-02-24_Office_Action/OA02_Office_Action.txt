Reasons for Allowance
Claims 1-7, 9-20, and 22-26 are allowed over the prior art.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
With regard to independent claims 1, 22-23, and 25, the prior art fails to anticipate and/or render obvious, either solely or in combination, a method and corresponding non-transitory computer-readable storage medium, computing device, and medical procedure for the acquiring of a luminescence image field of view that includes a region of interest with a target body that has a luminescence substance that defines luminescence values into the luminescence image, acquiring an auxiliary image that is operably different from the luminescence image and is segmented to classes for the segmentation of region of interest and foreign objects, identifying the luminescence values in the luminescence image to correspond to auxiliary values in the auxiliary image, generating a processed luminescence that has the processed luminescence for the representation of the target body for further identification, and the display of the output image substantially in real time and based on the processed luminescence image.
Paragraphs 0026-29 of the specification specifically describe the system’s consideration of luminescence to be with respect to fluorescence that is generated based on fluorophores and acquired by a fluorescence camera. Paragraph 0026 teaches that “these foreign objects may comprise one or more surgical instruments 115 (such as a scalpel), one or more hands 118 (such as of the surgeon), one or more surgical tools 121 (such as a gauze), one or more surrounding body-parts 124 (such as skin around the surgical cavity 109) and/or one or more background materials 125 (such as an operating table).”
Etsuro et al. (JP 2020156860) teaches the superimposition of fluorescent and visible image data for the highlighting of a region of interest. However, the reference is silent regarding segmenting the auxiliary image according to classifying of the region of interest and foreign objects.
Claims 2-7, 9-20, 24, and 26 are allowed at least by virtue of their dependency upon an allowable base claim.
