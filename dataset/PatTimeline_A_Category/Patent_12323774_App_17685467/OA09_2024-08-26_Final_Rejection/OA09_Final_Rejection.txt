Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 112
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112(b):
(b)  CONCLUSION.—The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor regards as the invention.
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph:
The specification shall conclude with one or more claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the subject matter which the applicant regards as his invention.
Claims 8-15, 17-30, and 32-37 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph, as being indefinite for failing to particularly point out and distinctly claim the subject matter which the inventor or a joint inventor (or for applications subject to pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 112, the applicant), regards as the invention.
System type claim 8 recites a headset comprising the element of “communicate directly with the game controller”, on line 9 thereof, that appears to be an unintended artifact of a similar amendment presented on line 8 of the same claim, however this reference renders the claim unclear as it is unclear which element(s) of the claimed system is associated with the recited action as presented.
Claim 11 recites the limitation the result in line 3.
Claim 23 recites the limitation the communication in line 3.
There is insufficient antecedent basis for these limitations in claims 11, and 23.
Claims 13, 18, 19, 28, 33, and 34 contain the trademark/trade name Microsoft XboxTM.  Where a trademark or trade name is used in a claim as a limitation to identify or describe a particular material or product, the claim does not comply with the requirements of 35 U.S.C. 112(b) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA ), second paragraph.  See Ex parte Simpson, 218 USPQ 1020 (Bd. App. 1982).  The claim scope is uncertain since the trademark or trade name cannot be used properly to identify any particular material or product.  A trademark or trade name is used to identify a source of goods, and not the goods themselves.  Thus, a trademark or trade name does not identify or describe the goods associated with the trademark or trade name.  In the present case, the trademark/trade name is used to identify/describe a particular game console and, accordingly, the identification/description is indefinite.
Remaining claims 9, 10, 12, 14, 15, 17, 20-22, 24-27, 29-30, 32, and 35-37 depend from claims 8, 11, 13, 18, 19, 23, 28, 33, and 34 rejected herein above and are additionally rejected based on the incorporation of issues noted herein above with respect to 8, 11, 13, 18, 19, 23, 28, 33, and 34.
