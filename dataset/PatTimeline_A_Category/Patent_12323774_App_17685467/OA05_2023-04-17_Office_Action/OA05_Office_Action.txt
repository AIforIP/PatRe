Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 103
The following is a quotation of pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 103(a) which forms the basis for all obviousness rejections set forth in this Office action:
(a) A patent may not be obtained though the invention is not identically disclosed or described as set forth in section 102, if the differences between the subject matter sought to be patented and the prior art are such that the subject matter as a whole would have been obvious at the time the invention was made to a person having ordinary skill in the art to which said subject matter pertains. Patentability shall not be negatived by the manner in which the invention was made.
Claims 8-15, 17-30, and 32-37 are rejected under pre-AIA  35 U.S.C. 103(a) as being unpatentable over Alden (US 2004/0198436) in view of McLoone et al (US  8,064,972) in further view of Applicant Admitted Prior Art.
Claim 8: The combination of Alden, McLoone, & Applicant Admitted Prior Art teaches a headset, the headset comprising:
a pair of speakers configured to output sound according to one or more of a game signal, a chat signal, a microphone signal and an external audio signal (Alden Abstract; Figure 8; Elements 30, 32);
a microphone configured to generate the microphone signal according to a voice of a user (Alden Figure 8: Elements 17, 29; Paragraph [0035] );
an RF circuit (Alden Figure 8: Elements 76, 78;); and
a signal processor configured to selectively manage the communication of the game signal, the chat signal, the microphone signal and the external audio signal (Applicant admitted prior art as presented in the Applicant’s specification Pages 1-2; Alden Abstract; Figure 7: Element 66; Paragraphs [0011], [0014], [0036] & McLoone Col 3:14-27; 3:64-4:14).
Alden teaches the invention including the ability to integrate multiple sound sources of various types with one another as cited above.  While Alden does not explicitly teach the use of gaming environments and voice chat as additional audio sources these features are taught in  the analogous invention of McLoone. It would have been obvious to one of ordinary skill in the art at the time of invention to have incorporated chat features as taught by McLoone in the invention of Alden in order to provide the predictable and expected result of allowing the use of the invention of Alden with online gaming environments and communications related thereto as taught by McLoone (McLoone Col 1:31-51).
While the combination of Alden & McLoone teaches the inclusion of Xbox game console and management of audio associated therewith(McLoone Col 2:52-3:27), the combination of Alden & McLoone arguably does not explicitly teach the inclusion of a game signal (game sound), with the Xbox game console is applicant admitted prior art that game sound (game signal) and chat sound are separately adjustable so as to allow them to be balanced against each other on the Xbox game console (Applicant’s specification Pages 1-2).  Accordingly, the inclusion of the ability to manage the game signal(game sound) in combination with the additional audio signals is understood as being implicit to the combination of Alden & McLoone and alternatively represent the obvious modification of Alden & McLoone that would have provided the expected and predictable result of allowing the selective modification of known audio signals including the game signal in order to allow the balancing of multiple audio sources with one another in the combination of Alden & McLoone.

Response to Arguments
Applicant's arguments filed March 10th, 2023have been fully considered but they are not persuasive.
In the above dated response, the applicant presents that the prior presented rejection under the combination of Alden & McLoone does not identify the selective management of a game signal as claimed.
It is respectfully noted that the selective management of a game signal in combination with other audio signals is a matter of applicant admitted prior art as reflected in the Background of the Invention section of the applicant’s specification on at least pages 1 and 2 of the same.  Additionally, the applicant’s discussion of this feature is noted as describing the same as a feature of Microsoft Xbox™ gaming console wherein the previously applied combination of Alden & McLoone incorporated the use of audio management features on platforms including same Microsoft Xbox™ gaming console (McLoone Col 2:52-3:27).
In view of the preceding the rejection of claims is respectfully maintained as presented and clarified herein above.
