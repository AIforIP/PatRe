REASONS FOR ALLOWANCE
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The prior art fails to disclose or suggest methods of predicting endometrial receptivity status and classifying the status into four endometrial statuses for implantation of embryos in a human subject.  The method comprises providing a biological sample from a human subject at a first time point within a menstrual cycle and determining expression profile of a panel of genes in the sample, where the genes include Arginase 2 (ARG2), Glutathione peroxidase 3 (GPX3), Mucin 1 (MUC1), Apolipoprotein D (APOD), and Secretoglobin family 2A member 2 (SCGB2A2).  The expression of the genes is compared to the same panel of genes of a receptive endometrial receptivity reference group, a non-receptive endometrial receptivity reference group, a pre-receptive endometrial receptivity reference group, and a post-receptive endometrial receptivity reference group; and identifying the group to which the subject belongs.  When the subject is identified as having a receptive endometrial status, a pre-implantation embryo is transferred into the subject.  If necessary, additional samples are obtained, expression of the same panel of genes is determined, and additional information regarding the endometrial receptivity is determined.
In addition, because the method, taken as a whole, includes significantly more than an abstract idea and the judicial exception is integrated into a practical application, the claimed subject matter is deemed to be patent eligible.
