REASONS FOR ALLOWANCE
The following is a statement of reasons for the indication of allowable subject matter:  no art on record, alone or in combination could be found to teach:
Regarding claim 2, Barnes (US 2016/0081710 A1) discloses all of the elements of claim 2 except:
Activating a locking mechanism on the balloon activation lever to lock the two balloons in an inflated state.
The device of Barnes could not be modified to include the above limitation since the art is silent as to any teaching, suggestion, or motivation to include the step of depressing a balloon activation lever which in turn causes a locking mechanism to lock the balloon activation lever to the handle in a depressed position in a method of cutting a transverse carpal ligament. As such, Barnes could not be modified to include the above limitations without significantly altering the function of the device or completely re-designing the device.
This is the same reasons for allowance as set forth in the parent application 18/226,138.
