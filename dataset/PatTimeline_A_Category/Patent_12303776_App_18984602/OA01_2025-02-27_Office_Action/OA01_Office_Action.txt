Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 102
The following is a quotation of the appropriate paragraphs of 35 U.S.C. 102 that form the basis for the rejections under this section made in this Office action:
A person shall be entitled to a patent unless –
(a)(2) the claimed invention was described in a patent issued under section 151, or in an application for patent published or deemed published under section 122(b), in which the patent or application, as the case may be, names another inventor and was effectively filed before the effective filing date of the claimed invention.
Claim(s) 1-20 is/are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 102(a)(2) as being anticipated by U.S. Pub. 2023/0112293 by Blacoe.
Regarding claim 1, Blacoe discloses a controller (abstract) defined by: a base comprising (fig. 9A-D – see the central block and wedge forming the base): a top surface comprising an upper contact that provides for releasable attachment of an associated first accessory (fig. 9A-D – see the top surface of the central block that provides for releasable attachment of the top block); an opposed bottom surface comprising a lower contact that provides for releasable attachment of an associated second accessory (fig. 9A-D – see the bottom surface of the central block at the wedge extension that connects to the lower block); an elongated handle positioned adjacent to the top surface, the handle comprising a plurality of rotatable handle buttons (fig. 9A-D – see the central block and wedge and the rotatable nature of the central block with respect to the wedge); a foundation positioned between the handle and the bottom surface (fig. 9A-D – see the bottom of the central block, in the alternative see the bottom on the wedge or an additional block that could be inserted as seen in figures 11A-D); one more accessories comprising a plurality of accessory buttons, wherein each accessory is configured to releasably attach to the upper contact, lower contact, or both (fig. 9A-D – see the buttons at 95); wherein the handle buttons can be rotated from a first orientation to a second orientation to accommodate left and right handed use (para. 34; fig. 9A-D – see the rotatable connection of the blocks to orient left and right handed users).
