The present application, filed on or after March 16, 2013, is being examined under the first inventor to file provisions of the AIA .
Response to Amendment
The Amendment filed March 19, 2024 has been entered. Claims 20-25 and 79-92 remain pending in the application.
Claim Objections
Claim 20 is objected to because of the following informalities: “detecting, by the sensor electronic hardware, that the implantable analyte sensor has been inserted into the host by at least receiving a signal indicating a change in an electrical property of the implantable analyte sensor; and” should read “detecting, by the sensor electronic hardware, that the implantable analyte sensor has been inserted into the host based at least on receiving a signal indicating a change in an electrical property of the implantable analyte sensor; and”. The second instance of “by” should read “based at least on”. Normally, performing an action “by xxx” means that xxx performs the action, or one uses xxx to perform the action. In this case, it makes sense that the sensor electronic hardware detects the insertion. The signal should be what the detection is based on (like if there is a signal, it indicates that the sensor is inserted). Appropriate correction is required.
