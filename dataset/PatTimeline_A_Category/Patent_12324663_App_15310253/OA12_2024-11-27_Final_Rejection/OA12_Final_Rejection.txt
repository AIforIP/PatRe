Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-3, 5, 30, 37, 42, 47, 135-145, and 147-152 are allowed.
In addition to the remarks of record, the instant claims define over the prior art because the cited prior art does not teach or suggest an article for inserting a particle into a cell comprising a member configured to guide and/or direct light to analyze the cell, wherein the member comprises a starting portion, an end portion, and a tapered portion.  The starting portion having a substantially constant cross-sectional diameter of at least about 100 micrometers, the end portion having a substantially constant cross-sectional diameter of less than 100 nm and a length of between 750 micrometers and about 3 mm, and the tapered potion connecting the starting portion to the end portion having a length of no more than about 500 micrometers.  The member further comprising at least one particle attached to the end portion by at least one of a polymer or an epoxy, and the member being formed by etching.
