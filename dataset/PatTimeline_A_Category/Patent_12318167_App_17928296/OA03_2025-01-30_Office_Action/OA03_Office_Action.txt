Claims 1, 3-5 and 11 are allowed.

Regarding Claims 3-5 and 11 are directly/indirectly dependent on claim 1 and are allowable based on their dependencies.
Dave et al. (US 2004/0126048 A1), discloses an assembly (Fig. 2, [0038]) comprising:
an interferometer (as shown in Fig. 2 elements 110 +130 + 140 + 150 the combinations of these optical paths comprises the interferometer, [0038]) for carrying out an optical coherence tomography, (Fig. 2 is a PM fiber polarization-sensitive OLCR system, also referred to as (PS-OCT), [0024, 0038])
