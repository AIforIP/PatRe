Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 21-23 and 25-40 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: regarding claim 21 and its dependents, as detailed in the previous Office action, none of the prior art of record teaches or reasonably suggests such an assembly including a cuff with a proximal end configured to slidably attach over the distal end of the shaft to couple the assembly to the shaft, in combination with the other recited components.  Regarding claim 36 and its dependents, similarly to as detailed in the previous Office action, none of the prior art of record teaches or reasonably suggests that such arms move from the undeployed configuration in the first position to the deployed configuration in the second position in response to the shaft moving distally relative to the bodily lumen, and in the reverse manner in response to the shaft moving distally relative to the bodily lumen, in combination with the other recited components.  Regarding claim 40, similarly to as detailed in the previous Office action, none of the prior art of record teaches or reasonably suggests such a controller configured to calculate a separate perfusion index corresponding to a light intensity measured by a detector of each sensor in response to light emitted by an emitter of the same sensor when the assembly is in the second position, in combination with the other recited components.
