Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: based on Applicant’s remarks and upon reconsideration, the Examiner finds Applicant’s arguments persuasive in view of the amendments to the independent claim. The prior art of record fails to teach or reasonably suggest, within the context of the other claim elements, the concept of a monitoring pad having a center, a short axis and a long axis where two microphones are positioned in the padding of the monitor pad symmetrically with respect to the center of the monitor pad and forming an angle of 45 degrees with the short axis or the long axis of the monitor pad, respectively.
