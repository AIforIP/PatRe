REASONS FOR ALLOWANCE
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: The claims contain subject matter that is not found in the art. Such subject matter includes a carrier comprising a belt, wherein the belt has a length within a range of 100 to 600 millimeters to fit around an abdominal part of a body of the baby; wherein the belt comprises an electrode arrangement consisting of electrocardiographic-electromyographic electrodes to simultaneously measure the membrane potential of the thoracic diaphragm of the body on both the side torso and mid-front of a user.  While relevant art teaches belts with electrode arrangements, they do not disclose wherein the belt is configured with both EMG and ECG electrodes that measure different portions of the thoracic diaphragm simultaneously. Other relevant art also teaches belt/strap/carrier arrangements; however, they do not teach this specific sizing designed to fit around the abdomen of a baby. The combination of these claimed elements would place the application in condition for allowance, as there is not art that teaches this.
