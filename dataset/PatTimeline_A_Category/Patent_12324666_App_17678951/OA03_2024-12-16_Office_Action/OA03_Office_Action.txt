DETAILED ACTION
Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 103
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 103 which forms the basis for all obviousness rejections set forth in this Office action:
A patent for a claimed invention may not be obtained, notwithstanding that the claimed invention is not identically disclosed as set forth in section 102, if the differences between the claimed invention and the prior art are such that the claimed invention as a whole would have been obvious before the effective filing date of the claimed invention to a person having ordinary skill in the art to which the claimed invention pertains. Patentability shall not be negated by the manner in which the invention was made.
Claim(s) 1, 8, and 14 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 103 as being unpatentable over HONG KWANG SEOK (KR 20200061016 A), in view of IMOSE KAZUO (WO 2004089212 A1) in view of UMEMATSU, Terumi (WO 2018179150 A1), and further in view of D'MELLO YANNICK (CA 3181091).
Considering claim 1, Hong teaches a heart measurement method, comprising:
obtaining a face video of a target object (camera 51, Fig.1, pg.6, lines 36-46: camera 51 may capture a user and acquire a face image using the captured image);
processing the facial video to obtain a plurality of heart rates and a plurality of frequencies of eyes or mouth of the target object (Fig.1, pg.6, lines 36-46: control device 53 receives the face image from the camera 51 and measures the pulse rate from the pulse wave signal calculated in the skin region of interest of the face image, and detects the peak point from the calculated pulse wave signal to measure the heart rate interval… control device 53 derives a tachogram (Tachogram) through the measured heart rate interval and analyzes the frequency of the tachogram parameter values for measuring the depression index (pulse, low frequency activity (LF'A), HF'A (High Frequency Activity), LF'A / HF'A raio, etc.) to measure and diagnose depression index), wherein the heart is obtained through one or more of a region of interest (ROI) between eyebrows and an ROI of chin (pg.7, lines 7-17: control device 53 is configured to detect positive/negative and neutral emotion recognition results detected through feature values (distance and tremor between elements) calculated from facial elements (head, eyebrows, eyes, nose, mouth, etc.) detected in the face region), and the frequency is obtained through one or more of an ROI of the eyes and an ROI of the mouth (pg.7, lines 7-17: user can take a picture with a camera 81 connected to the computer 85. The computer 85 can receive the face image from the camera 81 and measure the pulse rate from the pulse wave signal calculated in the skin region of interest of the face image, and detect the peak point in the calculated pulse wave signal to measure the heart rate interval. have. Then, the computer 85 derives a tachogram through the measured heartbeat interval and analyzes the frequency of the tachogram to measure parameter values (pulse, low frequency activity (LFA), high frequency HF'A (high frequency) Activity), LF'A / HF'A raio, etc.) to measure and diagnose depression index…);
selecting a target heart rate from the plurality of heart rates and a target vibration frequency from the plurality of frequencies (pg.7, lines 7-17: computer 85 derives a tachogram through the measured heartbeat interval and analyzes the frequency of the tachogram to measure parameter values (pulse, low frequency activity (LFA), high frequency HF'A (high frequency) Activity), LF'A / HF'A raio, etc.) to measure and diagnose depression index…); and
detecting the target heart rate and the target frequency (Fig.1, pg.6, lines 36-46: control device 53 receives the face image from the camera 51 and measures the pulse rate from the pulse wave signal calculated in the skin region of interest of the face image, and detects the peak point from the calculated pulse wave signal to measure the heart rate interval…), and determining, according to the detection result, that the target object is in a heart state (Fig.1, pg.6, lines 36-46, pg.7, lines 7-17).
Hong do not teach vibration frequencies.
IMOSE teaches vibration frequencies (Description: heart rate variability at different vibration frequencies).
Therefore, it would have been obvious before the effective filling date of the claimed
invention to one of ordinary skill in the art to provide above teaching of Imose to Hong, in order to improve subjective symptoms, exercise tolerance, Q0L and life prognosis.
Hong and Imose do not teach fatigue state.
Umematsu teaches fatigue state (pg.18, state detection unit 401; pg.19, lines 1-2).
Therefore, it would have been obvious before the effective filling date of the claimed
invention to one of ordinary skill in the art to provide above teaching of Umematsu to Hong and Imose, in order to detect a suspicious person with an abnormal psychological state, leading to efficient monitoring and maintenance of safety.
Hong, Imose, and Umematsu do not clearly teach quasi-heart rate.
D’Mello teaches quasi-heart rate ([0633] accurate heart rate).
Therefore, it would have been obvious before the effective filling date of the claimed
invention to one of ordinary skill in the art to provide above teaching of D’Mello to Hong, Imose,  and Umematsu, in order to detect irregular and anomalous activity at an early stage, which can then inform subsequent prevention and treatment strategies.
