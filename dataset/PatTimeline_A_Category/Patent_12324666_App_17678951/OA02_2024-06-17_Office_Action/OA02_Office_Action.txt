Claim Rejections - 35 USC § 103
The following is a quotation of 35 U.S.C. 103 which forms the basis for all obviousness rejections set forth in this Office action:
A patent for a claimed invention may not be obtained, notwithstanding that the claimed invention is not identically disclosed as set forth in section 102, if the differences between the claimed invention and the prior art are such that the claimed invention as a whole would have been obvious before the effective filing date of the claimed invention to a person having ordinary skill in the art to which the claimed invention pertains. Patentability shall not be negated by the manner in which the invention was made.
Claim(s) 1-2, 8-9, and 14-15 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. 103 as being unpatentable over LI, Xiao-fei (CN 112819790 B), in view of UMEMATSU, Terumi (WO 2018179150 A1), and further in view of D'MELLO YANNICK (CA 3181091).
Considering claim 1, Li teaches a heart measurement method, comprising:
obtaining a face video of a target object (abstract, obtaining the face region image of the target tracking object);
processing the facial video to obtain a plurality of heart rates (heart rate value) and a plurality of quasi-vibration frequencies (color difference signal) (abstract, performing spectrum analysis obtain the sampling signal of the face positioning ROI region to estimate the heart rate value of the target tracking object), wherein the heart is obtained through one or more of a region of interest (ROI) between eyebrows and an ROI of chin (pg.2, lines 16-17, chin, nose tip, left eye corner, right eye corner, left mouth corner and right mouth corner), and the quasi-vibration frequency obtained through one or more of an ROI of eyes and an ROI of mouth (abstract, selecting the face positioning ROI region of the face region image according to the face key point; performing spectrum analysis obtain the sampling signal of the face positioning ROI region to estimate the heart rate value of the target tracking object, pg.2, lines 16-17, pg.5, lines 20-22 and 31-32);
selecting a target heart rate from the plurality of heart rates and a target vibration frequency from the plurality of quasi-vibration frequencies (abstract, selecting the face positioning ROI region of the face region image according to the face key point; performing spectrum analysis obtain the sampling signal of the face positioning ROI region to estimate the heart rate value of the target tracking object); and
detecting the target heart rate and the target vibration frequency (Fig.3, pg.8, lines 3-14, a heart rate detection), and determining, according to the detection result, that the target object is in a heart state (Fig.3, pg.8, lines 3-14, a heart rate detection method based on computer target tracking technology, obtaining the face area image of the target tracking object, calibrating the face key point, obtaining the face locating ROI area, performing frequency spectrum analysis to the collected signal so as to obtain the estimated heart rate value of the target tracking object).
Li do not teach fatigue state.
Umematsu teaches fatigue state (pg.18, state detection unit 401; pg.19, lines 1-2).
Therefore, it would have been obvious before the effective filling date of the claimed
invention to one of ordinary skill in the art to provide above teaching of Umematsu to Li, in order to detect a suspicious person with an abnormal psychological state, leading to efficient monitoring and maintenance of safety.
Li and Umematsu do not clearly teach quasi-heart rate.
D’Mello teaches quasi-heart rate ([0633] accurate heart rate).
Therefore, it would have been obvious before the effective filling date of the claimed
invention to one of ordinary skill in the art to provide above teaching of D’Mello to Li and Umematsu, in order to detect irregular and anomalous activity at an early stage, which can then inform subsequent prevention and treatment strategies.
