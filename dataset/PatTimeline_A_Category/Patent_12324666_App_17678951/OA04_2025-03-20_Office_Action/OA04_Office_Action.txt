Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1, 3-8, 10-14, and 16-20 are allowed.
HONG KWANG SEOK (KR 20200061016 A) teaches a depression index measuring and diagnosing method using a facial skin image according to the present invention comprises the steps of: photographing a facial skin image and detecting a skin region of interest from the photographed facial skin image; calculating an average value of color data in the detected skin region of interest; calculating a pulse wave signal by applying filtering to the average value of color data; detecting a peak point from the calculated pulse wave signal and calculating a pulse; measuring a heartbeat interval by using the detected peak point, and deriving a tachogram through the measured heartbeat interval; analyzing a frequency of the tachogram to calculate a parameter value for the measurement of a depression index; measuring the depression index by using the calculated parameter value; detecting face elements in a face area and recognizing positive/negative and neutral emotions by using a distance between the face elements or the shaking of the face elements among the face elements; and diagnosing a user′s depression state through the measured depression index and recognized positive/negative, and neutral emotions. According to the present invention, the depression index measuring and diagnosing method using a facial skin image can conveniently and accurately measure the depression index.
IMOSE KAZUO (WO 2004089212 A1) teaches examination device for selecting patients for whom oxygen therapy is effective without depending on hospital sleep examination (PSG) in a medical institute having a large-scale facility such as a sleep laboratory and confirming the treatment effect of the oxygen therapy by means of a reliable and simple structure and for enabling medical staff to find manifestation of sleep respiratory disorder of a patient and the sympathetic hypertonia caused by the sleep respiratory disorder.
UMEMATSU, Terumi (WO 2018179150 A1) teaches heart rate estimation apparatus includes: a region of interest detection unit that detects a region of interest which includes a face region of a subject from images in which the subject has been imaged sequentially by an imaging device; a luminance calculation unit that calculates a luminance for each light wavelength component in the region of interest; a heart rate component enhancing unit that performs an independent component analysis on the luminance using, in an unmixing matrix of the independent component analysis, heart rate component enhancing coefficients in which the weighting of the luminance coefficients for a green light wavelength component is increased and extracts a heart rate component; and a heart rate calculation unit that calculates the heart rate from the heart rate component.
D’MELLO YANNICK (CA 3181091) teaches a system, method, and device for non-invasive hemodynamic measurement of a subject. The method includes identifying vibrational pulses V1 and V2 and vibrations corresponding to cardiac mechanical motion from vibrational cardiography (VCG) data, the VCG data derived from a vibration signal acquired at the surface of the chest of the subject corresponding to cardiac-induced vibrations; determining a vibration feature from the vibration signal; and determining a hemodynamic measurement from the vibration feature.
The prior art of record fails to anticipate or render obvious the limitations of the claims.
The cited art on record, individually or combination, fail to explicitly disclose the combination of the following limitations: “processing the facial video to obtain a plurality of quasi-heart rates and a plurality of quasi-vibration frequencies wherein the quasi-heart is obtained through one or more of a region of interest (ROI) between eyebrows and an ROI of chin, and the quasi-vibration frequency is obtained through one or more of an ROI of eyes and an ROI of mouth; selecting a target heart rate from the plurality of quasi-heart rates and a target vibration frequency from the plurality of quasi-vibration frequencies; and detecting the target heart rate and the target vibration frequency, and determining, according to the detection result, that the target object is in a fatigue state, wherein processing the face video to obtain the plurality of quasi-heart rates and the plurality of quasi-vibration frequencies, comprising: dividing the face video into a plurality of window videos according to a time window of a specific step size; and performing, for each window video of the plurality of window videos, extraction processing on the each window video to obtain an eye feature signal, a mouth feature signal, an eyebrow feature signal, and a chin feature signal, fusing the eye feature signal, the mouth feature signal, the eyebrow feature signal, and the chin feature signal to obtain a fused feature signal, and converting the fused feature signal to obtain a quasi-heart rate and a quasi-vibration frequency.”. Furthermore, it would not have been obvious to one of ordinary skill in the art to modify the prior in order to arrive at claimed invention.
