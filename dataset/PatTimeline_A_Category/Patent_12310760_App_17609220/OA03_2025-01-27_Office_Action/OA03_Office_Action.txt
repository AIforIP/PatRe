In view of the withdrawal of the restriction requirement, applicant(s) are advised that if any claim presented in a divisional application is anticipated by, or includes all the limitations of, a claim that is allowable in the present application, such claim may be subject to provisional statutory and/or nonstatutory double patenting rejections over the claims of the instant application. Once the restriction requirement is withdrawn, the provisions of 35 U.S.C. 121 are no longer applicable. See In re Ziegler, 443 F.2d 1211, 1215, 170 USPQ 129, 131-32 (CCPA 1971). See also MPEP § 804.01.

Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: based on Applicant’s amendments and upon reconsideration, the Examiner was not able to identify any reference that can be used singularly or in combination with another reference including the prior art of record to render the present invention anticipated or obvious to one of ordinary skill in the art. While the updated search yielded US 2017/0143268 to Kovacs, which teaches that physiological signals obtained by one device and physiological signals obtained by another device are time-synchronized to improve the accuracy of the detected signals (e.g., paragraph [0128] of Kovacs), the Examiner was not able to identify a reference that teaches grouping segments of the primary waveforms into self-similar groups of segments based on the time-synchronized primary and secondary waveforms; and then, analyzing extracted features associated with at least one of the self-similar groups of segments to predict a risk of the individual developing a post-procedural dysfunction of the anatomical feature as a result of the medical procedure on the anatomical feature.
