Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 29-46 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: As in parent application 17/112,177, Notice of Allowance dated 2/15/2024, the prior art made of record in the parent application fails to teach or make obvious a SDB analysis in response to not detecting a verbal command in detected speech. The instant claims are a system/device and therefore, both the steps of detecting a verbal command, and the function of performing a SDB analysis when a verbal command is not detected, are both required under the doctrine of BRI and fail to be taught in combination with all of the references cited and considered in the art.
