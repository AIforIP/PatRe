Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1, 3-5, 7-8, 10-11. 13-18, 20-21. 23-24, 26-32 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance: Claims 1, 3-5, 7-8, 10-11. 13-18, 20-21. 23-24, 26-32 are allowable over the cited prior art because the Examiner found that none of the cited prior art discloses processing circuitry of at least one of the insertable cardiac monitor or the remote computing device, the processing circuitry configured to: periodically determine a respective value for each of a plurality of physiological parameters based on one or more of heart sounds or ECG, wherein at least a first physiological parameter of the plurality of physiological parameters is determined based on the heart sounds and at least a second physiological parameter of the plurality of physiological parameters is determined based on the ECG, wherein the first physiological parameter comprises a frequency of occurrence of an S3 heart sound; … and determine whether to present an indication of a heart failure event based on an analysis of the trends of the periodically determined values of the physiological parameters, wherein to perform the analysis of the trends the processing circuitry is configured to: determine a first variation value of at least one physiological parameter of the plurality of physiological parameters for a first period and a second variation value of the at least one physiological parameter for a second period preceding the first period; and determine whether a comparison of the first variation value and the second variation value satisfies a criterion.
Examiner found closest prior art was the most recent combination as shown in the previous office action ( Stahmann, McCabe, Siejko, Zhang, and Gheeraert) which discloses using a vector of dipole differences, but is not a variation for a period.
