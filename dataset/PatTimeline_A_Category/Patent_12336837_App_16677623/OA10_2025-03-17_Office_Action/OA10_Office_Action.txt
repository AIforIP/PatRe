The application has been amended as follows:
21. (Currently Amended) A method of providing a continuity of care for a patient at risk of developing pressure ulcers (PU) during transfer from a first care setting to a second care setting, the method comprising the steps of:
identifying a current PU intervention of level-K received by the patient at the first care setting, wherein the current PU intervention of level-K is provided in a transfer record, wherein the transfer record further comprises results obtained from performing a first SEM scan of at least one body location of the patient while in the first care setting, wherein the first SEM scan comprises a plurality of measurements of an SEM value at the body location and calculation of a first delta value from the plurality of SEM values, wherein if the current PU intervention of level-K is a level-0 PU intervention, the level-0 PU intervention comprises performing a daily SEM scan of at least one body location of the patient;
making a plurality of Sub-Epidermal Moisture (SEM) measurements at a body location on the patient at the second care setting, each SEM measurement comprising a measurement of biocapacitance,
calculating a delta value by a difference between a maximum SEM value and a minimum SEM value of the plurality of SEM measurements,
determining whether the delta value exceeds a threshold,
making a further plurality of SEM measurements at a pre-determined frequency corresponding to level-K if the delta value does not exceed the threshold, wherein the pre-determined frequency corresponding to level-K is longer than every 15 minutes and if the delta value does not exceed the threshold, ing to administer the current PU intervention of level-K based on the delta value, wherein level-K corresponds to a risk level for pressure ulcer, or
making the further plurality of SEM measurements at a pre-determined frequency corresponding to level-N if the delta value exceeds the threshold, where N has a value greater than K, wherein the pre-determined frequency corresponding to level-N is shorter than the pre-determined frequency corresponding to level-K and longer than or equal to every 15 minutes, and ing a new PU intervention of level-N based on the delta value, wherein level-N corresponds to a higher risk level for pressure ulcer than level-K.
Reasons for Allowance
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
The prior art of record fails to disclose the specific method features that are comprised of the combination of components as presented in the claimed invention. More specifically, the closest two pieces of art, Lachenbruch et al. (U.S. 2016/0338591) and Geleijnse et al. (U.S. 2015/0186607), fail to teach all of the limitations of independent claim 1 in combination.
Specifically, Lachenbruch et al. is directed to systems and methods for mitigating tissue breakdown. In one embodiment, an alert indicative of a change in tissue status is provided in response to a tissue status crossing a threshold.
As such, Lachenbruch et al. teaches some of the claimed limitations, but does not teach anything related to identifying a current intervention level, making a plurality of measurements at different frequencies depending on whether a threshold is exceeded or not, continuing administration of a current intervention, and calculating a delta value between a minimum and maximum SEM value. There is nothing in Lachenbruch et al. that would suggest this.
The second reference, Geleijnse et al., deals with a clinical support system and method. As such, while Geleijnse et al. does teach identifying a current intervention level and continuing administration of a current intervention, it too does not teach anything related to making a plurality of measurements at different frequencies depending on whether a threshold is exceeded or not and calculating a delta value between a minimum and maximum SEM value. There is nothing in Geleijnse et al. that would suggest this.
The prior art alone, or in combination, does not disclose or make obvious the claim set of the present invention.
With respect to eligibility under 35 U.S.C. 101, Examiner asserts that the present claims recite additional elements which, when integrated into the abstract idea, become a practical application. The claims adjust a treatment for a particular affliction.
