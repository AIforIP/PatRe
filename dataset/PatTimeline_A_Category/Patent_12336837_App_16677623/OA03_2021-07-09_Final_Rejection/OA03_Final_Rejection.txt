As per claim 21, Lachenbruch et al. teaches a method of providing a continuity of care for a patient at risk of developing pressure ulcers (PU) during transfer from a first care setting to a second care setting, the method comprising the steps of:
--making a plurality of Sub-Epidermal Moisture (SEM) measurements at a body location on the patient at the second care setting, (see: paragraph [0037] where one or more sensors 102 are positioned to collect information regarding the tissue status of the individual.
