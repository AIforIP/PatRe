Allowable Subject Matter
Claims 1-20 are allowed.
The following is an examiner’s statement of reasons for allowance:
Creasey et al. (US 2016/0129248 A1, previously cited by Applicant) discloses a wearable device (par. [0095], claim 8) comprising a body comprising a plurality of energy generators (electrodes, 408, par. [0052]), wherein a processing unit is configured to communicate with nerves associated with the skin (“electronic circuits or chips that perform the functions…”, par. [0052]). Creasey further discloses the use of an adhesive for attaching the device to a biological subject (par. [0057]; adhesive for adherence to skin).
With respect to claim 1 and 17, Creasey does not appear to disclose or reasonably suggest the claimed combination of a pressure generator element and a heat generator element disposed within the body and being independently operable to output a pressure force and a heat flux configured to communicate with nerves associated with skin and an insulating material for limiting flows of the pressure force and/or the heat flux outside the housing.
With respect to claim 10, Creasey does not appear to disclose or reasonably suggest the claimed combination of: a plurality of generator elements disposed within the body and being independently operable to output different energy types configured to communicate with nerves associated with skin and a housing having a proximal end, a distal end, and a wall defining an interior surface, the distal end of the housing being positionable on the skin, wherein at least one of the plurality of generator elements is spaced from the interior surface of the housing.
With respect to claim 17, Creasey does not appear to disclose or reasonably suggest the claimed combination of a plurality of generator elements disposed within the body and being independently operable convert electricity from the power source to a plurality of different energy types configured to communicate with nerves associated with skin and an insulating material for limiting flows of the pressure force and/or the heat flux outside the housing.
Claims 2-9, 11-16, and 18-20 are allowable for reasons of dependency.
